# Zero Touch Provisioning: Instant Collaboration
On Aug 11, 2026Aug 11, 2026By [Rajesh Patwardhan](https://blog.webex.com/contributors/rpat/)2 Min Read
[ ](https://twitter.com/intent/tweet?url=https://blog.webex.com/workspaces/zero-touch-provisioning-instant-collaboration) [ ](https://www.linkedin.com/shareArticle?mini=true&url=https://blog.webex.com/workspaces/zero-touch-provisioning-instant-collaboration&title=Zero%20Touch%20Provisioning:%20Instant%20Collaboration) [ ](https://www.facebook.com/sharer/sharer.php?u=https://blog.webex.com/workspaces/zero-touch-provisioning-instant-collaboration)

Imagine a workspace that self-configures, authenticates to the network automatically, and becomes instantly functional the moment it is plugged in. No activation codes, no manual scripts, and no back-and-forth verification. With Zero Touch Provisioning (ZTP) becoming generally available for all RoomOS 26 devices, this is now the reality.
By planning the onboarding process in Collaboration Control Hub, service owners can ensure that when an installer plugs in a device, the room comes to life immediately. It is the seamless transition from “ordered” to “operational” that modern IT teams require. 
## From Ordering to Operational: Eliminating Friction
Traditional deployments are often labor-intensive, requiring in-person setup, manual wizard navigation, and the entry of individual activation codes. For large-scale rollouts, this creates several bottlenecks:
  * Operational Complexity: Manually creating hundreds of activation codes and tracking device models is prone to error.
  * Communication Silos: Without a centralized plan, IT, partners, and on-site installers often struggle to stay aligned.
  * High Costs: The need for on-site technicians to visit every room to manually onboard devices are both time-consuming and expensive.


ZTP addresses these challenges by automating the entire lifecycle. In the Control Hub, you can now bulk-create workspaces, assign devices, and apply configuration templates before the hardware even arrives on-site. Once the plan is set, the installer simply connects the device, and the room becomes fully functional.
![](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns=%22http://www.w3.org/2000/svg%22%20viewBox=%220%200%202100%201443%22%3E%3C/svg%3E)
## Secure Network Onboarding: Zero Touch, Zero Trust
A modern deployment must be secure by design. Today, provisioning unique certificates and managing six-month renewal cycles creates significant overhead. 
RoomOS ZTP simplifies this by supporting SCEP-based certificate enrollment with automated DHCP support. By integrating with [Cisco Identity Services Engine (ISE](https://www.cisco.com/c/en/us/products/security/identity-services-engine/ise-ds.html)), we enable not just “zero touch” but “zero trust” provisioning—ensuring every device is authenticated to the network before it ever receives its service configuration.
## The Power of the Cisco Ecosystem
When your collaboration hardware speaks the same language as your network, the experience is unparalleled. Using Cisco wired or wireless infrastructure, the network can automatically profile and authenticate devices via [ISE and Catalyst switches](https://www.cisco.com/site/us/en/products/networking/index.html). Furthermore, Cisco Wireless solutions can leverage location intelligence to verify that the correct device is being provisioned in the designated room, providing an extra layer of validation for the installer.
## Provisioning as the First Impression
Great user experiences begin long before the first meeting—they start the moment a device is ordered. With this first phase of ZTP, we are delivering the first of many productivity improvements for service owners. By leveraging the full power of the Cisco portfolio, we are redefining how workspaces are deployed.
Stay tuned as we continue to evolve this experience.
