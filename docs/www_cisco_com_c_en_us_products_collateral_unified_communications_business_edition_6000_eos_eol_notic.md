Overview
EOL13463
Cisco announces the end-of-sale and end-of-life dates for the Cisco Unified Workspace Licensing Meetings Edition for Cisco Business Edition 6000. The last day to order the affected product(s) is February 1, 2021. Customers with active service contracts will continue to receive support from the Cisco Technical Assistance Center (TAC) as shown in Table 1 of the EoL bulletin. Table 1 describes the end-of-life milestones, definitions, and dates for the affected product(s). Table 2 lists the product part numbers affected by this announcement. For customers with active and paid service and support contracts, support will be available under the terms and conditions of customers' service contract.
This bulletin is specific to the following licenses:
●Cisco Business Edition 6000 licenses quoted under top-level product ID R-CBE6K-K9 (licenses quoted under different top-level product IDs like CUWL-12X-K9 are covered in other bulletins).
●Feature-level Cisco Unified Workspace Licensing Meetings Edition for Cisco Business Edition 6000 (abbreviated in this bulletin as "BE6000 UWL MTG").
●Versions 11x and 12x (BE6000 UWL MTG version 10x is covered in bulletins EOL12641 and EOL12775, because version 10x end-of-life dates precede this offer's end-of-life).
**Renewing to Collaboration Flex Plan**
Cisco is committed to long-term support for our customers with on-premises calling deployments. We are also committed to supporting our customers as they move to cloud calling. Our Collaboration Flex Plan supports both deployment models. In September 2021, we announced that customers with perpetual on-premises calling deployments should renew to Collaboration Flex Plan. Some of the customer benefits of Flex Plan include:
●Flexible payment methods – monthly, annual, and full 
●Software Support that is included - never find yourself uncovered
●Simplified licensing structure
●Better license entitlements to Cisco Collaboration solutions
●An easy migration to the cloud
The last day to renew Software Support Services (SWSS) for perpetual on-premises calling has been moved up to October 30, 2021. Customers who need additional licensing and / or to renew software support after this date will be able to get full support for their on-premises calling deployments by renewing to Collaboration Flex Plan.
For more information about Collaboration Flex Plan 3.0, reference the ordering guides at this link: <https://www.cisco.com/c/en/us/partners/tools/collaboration-ordering-guides.html>
