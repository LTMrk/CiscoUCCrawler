Overview
EOL13629 - Amended
Cisco announces the end-of-sale and end-of-life dates for the Cisco Business Edition 6000/7000 embedded virtualization commercial offers, all versions. The last day to order the affected product(s) is March 2, 2023. Customers with active SWSS contracts will continue to receive support from the Cisco Technical Assistance Center (TAC) until the Last Day of Support as shown in Table 1 of the EoL bulletin. Table 1 describes the end-of-life milestones, definitions, and dates for the affected product(s). Table 2 lists the product part numbers affected by this announcement. For customers with active and paid service and support contracts, support will be available under the terms and conditions of customers' service contract. 
This EOL bulletin is for entire commercial offers, entire feature-levels and all versions (first shipping 7.0 and subsequent 8.0). These commercial offers were only sold in conjunction with Business Edition 6000 (BE6000) / Business Edition (BE7000) appliances of select older hardware generations. New purchases of BE6000 / BE7000 appliances starting with M6 hardware generation do not ship with or have options for these embedded virtualization commercial offers. 
Cisco commercial offer EOL milestone dates may occur before VMware by Broadcom's release EOL milestone date published on broadcom.com. Bug/security fixes for ESXi releases come from VMware by Broadcom, and are not available beyond VMware by Broadcom's published End of General Support date for the release in question. 
Customers with active SWSS on these commercial offers may call Cisco TAC for support until the Last Day of Support in Table 1, but note VMware by Broadcom support engagement to Cisco ramps down after VMware's published date for End of General Support for the release in question. 
Perpetual license version upgrade from 7.0 to 8.0 is no longer available from Cisco or from VMware by Broadcom for these commercial offers New major versions will not be available for these End-of-Life commercial offers. 
Customers requiring continued technical support or access to new major versions must procure a new VMware vSphere subscription from a Broadcom channel partner or direct from VMware by Broadcom.
