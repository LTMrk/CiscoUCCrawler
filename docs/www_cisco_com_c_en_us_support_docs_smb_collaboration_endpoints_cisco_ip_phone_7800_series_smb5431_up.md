## Introduction
A firmware is the program coded into the device to make it perform according to its capabilities. Just like software updates on a computer, firmware updates are typically improvements that the manufacturer has made to the code to fix product deficiencies, or roll out new features.
Keeping the firmware of your device updated generally keeps it at its best working condition.
There are two methods on how to upgrade the firmware of your Cisco 7800 and 8800 Series Multiplatform IP Phone:
  * Through the web browser command - This method allows you to upgrade the firmware directly by entering the upgrade command in a web browser Address bar.
  * Through the Upgrade Rule - This method allows you to upgrade the firmware using the web-based utility of your IP Phone. To learn how, click [here](https://www.cisco.com/c/en/us/support/docs/smb/collaboration-endpoints/cisco-ip-phone-7800-series/smb5693-upgrade-the-firmware-of-the-7800-and-8800-series-multiplatfo.html "This article aims to show you how to upgrade the firmware of your Cisco 7800 and 8800 Series Multiplatform IP Phone through the Upgrade Rule in the phone web-based utility.") for instructions.


## Objective
This article aims to show you how to perform a firmware update on your Cisco IP Phone 7800 Series and Cisco IP Phone 8800 Series through the web browser command.

### Upgrade the Firmware
Step 1. On your IP Phone, determine its IP address by pressing the **Applications** button.

Step 2. Using the Navigation cluster [![](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/smb/collaboration-endpoints/cisco-ip-phone-7800-series/images/rjs-08112017-upgradestart-step2.png)](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/smb/collaboration-endpoints/cisco-ip-phone-7800-series/images/rjs-08112017-upgradestart-step2.png "Related image, diagram or screenshot.") button, navigate to**Status** and press the **Select** softkey.

Step 3. Navigate to **Network****s****tatus** and press the **Select** softkey.
[![](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/smb/collaboration-endpoints/cisco-ip-phone-7800-series/images/rjs-08112017-upgradestart-step3.png)](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/smb/collaboration-endpoints/cisco-ip-phone-7800-series/images/rjs-08112017-upgradestart-step3.png "Related image, diagram or screenshot.")
Step 4. Look for the IP address and take note of it. In this example, the IP address is 192.168.2.139.
[![](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/smb/collaboration-endpoints/cisco-ip-phone-7800-series/images/rjs-08112017-upgradestart-step4.png)](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/smb/collaboration-endpoints/cisco-ip-phone-7800-series/images/rjs-08112017-upgradestart-step4.png "Related image, diagram or screenshot.")
Step 5. On your computer, launch a browser and enter the following format in the Address bar: http://<phone_ip>/admin/upgrade?<schema>://<serv_ip[:port]>/filename then press the **Enter** key.
**Note:** In this example, the file has been saved in a tftp server with the IP address 192.168.2.147. Thus, http://192.168.2.139/admin/upgrade?tftp://192.168.2.147/sip88xx.11-0-1MPP-477.loads is entered in the address bar.
[![](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/smb/collaboration-endpoints/cisco-ip-phone-7800-series/images/rjs-08112017-upgradestart-step5.png)](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/smb/collaboration-endpoints/cisco-ip-phone-7800-series/images/rjs-08112017-upgradestart-step5.png "Related image, diagram or screenshot.")
**IMPORTANT:** Make sure that your IP Phone and the computer you are using are both connected to the same network.
Step 6. Wait for the phone to finish upgrading.
**Note:** The phone may restart a couple of times while the firmware is upgrading. Do not power down the phone while the firmware upgrade process is in progress.
