Overview
EOL13540
Cisco announces the end-of-sale and end-of life dates for the Perpetual Licensing for Cisco Expressway Series. The last day to order the affected product(s) is January 23, 2021. Customers with active service contracts will continue to receive support from the Cisco Technical Assistance Center (TAC) as shown in Table 1 of the EoL bulletin. Table 1 describes the end-of-life milestones, definitions, and dates for the affected product(s). Table 2 lists the product part numbers affected by this announcement. For customers with active and paid service and support contracts, support will be available under the terms and conditions of customers' service contract.
Customers should migrate from Expressway Series (Perpetual) to Expressway in Cisco Collaboration Flex Plan 3.0. The ordering guide for Flex Plan 3.0 can be found at the link below: <https://www.cisco.com/c/en/us/partners/tools/collaboration-ordering-guides.html>.
HCS Partners may continue to utilise HCS perpetual licensing for their existing HCS end customers however, Cisco recommends moving customers to Cisco Collaboration Flex Plan subscription licensing, that enables the customer to take advantage of the rich Collaboration feature set available in that offer.
This is a commercial model change only to perpetual licenses. Customer premise deployments are available under Flex 3.0
