## Introduction
This article outlines the procedure for installing RHEL on local RAID configuration in a standalone UCSC C240 M5 server. 
## Setup Used
UCSC-C240-M5L  
RAID Controller: Cisco 12G SAS HBA (UCSC-SAS-M5)  
4K sector HDDs  
CIMC: 3.1(3a)  
OS: RHEL 7.3  

## Prerequisites 
1. Download the RHEL ISO and the Controller Driver ISO:
RHEL: rhel-server-7.3-x86_64-boot.iso
Driver ISO: [ucs-cxxx-drivers-linux.3.1.3.iso](https://software.cisco.com/download/home/286318800/type/283853158/release/4.0%25281a%2529)
(In the ISO, navigate to Storage -> LS -> UCSC-SAS-M5 -> RHEL -> RHEL7.3 -> mpt3sas-23.00.00.00_el7.3-1.x86_64.iso.gz )
2. If you extract the mpt3sas-23.00.00.00_el7.3-1.x86_64.iso.gz file, you will get the driver ISO -> mpt3sas-23.00.00.00_el7.3-1.x86_64.iso
3. Similarly, if you are using a UCSC-RAID-M5 controller in your server, you can navigate to its corresponding driver.
## Procedure
Step 1. Since the server has 4k sector drives, we will do a UEFI boot. The Boot Order should look like below:
  
[![](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/servers-unified-computing/ucs-c-series-rack-servers/214407-installing-rhel-on-a-standalone-ucs-c240-00.png)](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/servers-unified-computing/ucs-c-series-rack-servers/214407-installing-rhel-on-a-standalone-ucs-c240-00.png "Related image, diagram or screenshot.")
  
  

Step 2. Open the KVM and map the RHEL ISO:
  
[![](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/servers-unified-computing/ucs-c-series-rack-servers/214407-installing-rhel-on-a-standalone-ucs-c240-01.png)](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/servers-unified-computing/ucs-c-series-rack-servers/214407-installing-rhel-on-a-standalone-ucs-c240-01.png "Related image, diagram or screenshot.")
  
Step 3. Press F6 and boot from the UEFI: Cisco vKVM-Mapped vDVD1.24
  
Step 4.Select the “Install Red Hat Enterprise Linux 7.3” option and press ‘e’
[![](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/servers-unified-computing/ucs-c-series-rack-servers/214407-installing-rhel-on-a-standalone-ucs-c240-02.png)](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/servers-unified-computing/ucs-c-series-rack-servers/214407-installing-rhel-on-a-standalone-ucs-c240-02.png "Related image, diagram or screenshot.")
Step 5. You will see the below screen:
[![](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/servers-unified-computing/ucs-c-series-rack-servers/214407-installing-rhel-on-a-standalone-ucs-c240-03.png)](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/servers-unified-computing/ucs-c-series-rack-servers/214407-installing-rhel-on-a-standalone-ucs-c240-03.png "Related image, diagram or screenshot.")
  
Step 6. Enter the command “linux dd” and do a Ctrl + x
  
[![](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/servers-unified-computing/ucs-c-series-rack-servers/214407-installing-rhel-on-a-standalone-ucs-c240-04.png)](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/servers-unified-computing/ucs-c-series-rack-servers/214407-installing-rhel-on-a-standalone-ucs-c240-04.png "Related image, diagram or screenshot.")
  
Step 7. The above command will start the Interactive mode:
  
[![](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/servers-unified-computing/ucs-c-series-rack-servers/214407-installing-rhel-on-a-standalone-ucs-c240-05.png)](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/servers-unified-computing/ucs-c-series-rack-servers/214407-installing-rhel-on-a-standalone-ucs-c240-05.png "Related image, diagram or screenshot.")
  
Step 8. Unmap the RHEL ISO and map the controller driver ISO:
[![](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/servers-unified-computing/ucs-c-series-rack-servers/214407-installing-rhel-on-a-standalone-ucs-c240-06.png)](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/servers-unified-computing/ucs-c-series-rack-servers/214407-installing-rhel-on-a-standalone-ucs-c240-06.png "Related image, diagram or screenshot.")
  
Step 9. Type ‘r’ to refresh the driver disk list:
  
[![](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/servers-unified-computing/ucs-c-series-rack-servers/214407-installing-rhel-on-a-standalone-ucs-c240-07.png)](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/servers-unified-computing/ucs-c-series-rack-servers/214407-installing-rhel-on-a-standalone-ucs-c240-07.png "Related image, diagram or screenshot.")
  
Step 10. You will now see the controller driver ISO listed. Type ‘7’ and make the selection:
[![](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/servers-unified-computing/ucs-c-series-rack-servers/214407-installing-rhel-on-a-standalone-ucs-c240-08.png)](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/servers-unified-computing/ucs-c-series-rack-servers/214407-installing-rhel-on-a-standalone-ucs-c240-08.png "Related image, diagram or screenshot.")
  
Step 11. Enter ‘1’ and then ‘c’ to continue:
  
[![](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/servers-unified-computing/ucs-c-series-rack-servers/214407-installing-rhel-on-a-standalone-ucs-c240-09.png)](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/servers-unified-computing/ucs-c-series-rack-servers/214407-installing-rhel-on-a-standalone-ucs-c240-09.png "Related image, diagram or screenshot.")
  
Step 12. The .rpm driver is inside the .iso file. You may get a warning that “DD repo content not a file”, but it is only a warning – the driver would have been installed.
  
Step 13. You should be at the “Driver disk device selection” menu again. Unmap the driver ISO and map your RHEL ISO again. Press ‘r’ to refresh and then – press ‘c’ to continue with the RHEL installation. The installer should be able to recognise the RAID controller and the disks managed by it.
  
[![](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/servers-unified-computing/ucs-c-series-rack-servers/214407-installing-rhel-on-a-standalone-ucs-c240-10.png)](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/servers-unified-computing/ucs-c-series-rack-servers/214407-installing-rhel-on-a-standalone-ucs-c240-10.png "Related image, diagram or screenshot.")
  
  
[![](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/servers-unified-computing/ucs-c-series-rack-servers/214407-installing-rhel-on-a-standalone-ucs-c240-11.png)](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/servers-unified-computing/ucs-c-series-rack-servers/214407-installing-rhel-on-a-standalone-ucs-c240-11.png "Related image, diagram or screenshot.")  
  

[![](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/servers-unified-computing/ucs-c-series-rack-servers/214407-installing-rhel-on-a-standalone-ucs-c240-12.png)](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/servers-unified-computing/ucs-c-series-rack-servers/214407-installing-rhel-on-a-standalone-ucs-c240-12.png "Related image, diagram or screenshot.")
