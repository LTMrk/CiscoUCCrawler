# Authentication using 2FA
Learn procedures to achieve Auth+ (Authentication+) compliant.
[Suggest Edits](https://help.webexconnect.io/edit/authentication-using-2fa)
The Campaign Registry (TCR) has introduced a new brand identity verification process for Public Profit entities. The primary objective of Authentication+ is to help prevent brand impersonation, which can lead to consumer fraud that includes disinformation, smishing, and spoofing. Webex Connect will be aligning to the new Authentication+ process of TCR. 
> ## 🚧
> Note
> All publicly traded companies must begin using the additional 2FA process (Authentication+) starting October 17, 2024 to be Auth+ compliant, and existing brands must achieve Auth+ compliance by October 30, 2025.
> ## 📘
> Note:
> Self-serve campaign creation is currently disabled on the 10DLC module of Webex Connect. To create new campaigns, please reach out to the Support Team after your brand achieves Authentication+ compliant. Follow the procedures below to achieve Auth+ compliant.

What are the possible status types, and what actions should be taken for each?
[](https://help.webexconnect.io/docs/authentication-using-2fa#what-are-the-possible-status-types-and-what-actions-should-be-taken-for-each)  
| Auth+ Status  | Description  | Recommended Action  |  
| --- | --- | --- |  
| Non-compliant  | No verified business email on form.  | Edit the brand, add a valid business email, and submit for verification.  |  
| Pending  | Verification is underway.  | Await completion of the verification process; no action needed unless prompted.  |  
| Active  | Auth+ is current and verified.  | No action needed; your brand is fully compliant.  |

| Renewal Pending  | Auth+ verification about to expire  | Click the 'Renew Auth+' button and complete the re-verification process to restore compliance.  |  
| Failed  | Verification did not succeed.  | A support ticket is automatically generated; await outreach from support or respond if contacted.  |  
### 
What should I do to be Auth+ compliant?
[](https://help.webexconnect.io/docs/authentication-using-2fa#what-should-i-do-to-be-auth-compliant)
For most legacy brands, the initial status is Non-compliant. Brand owners edit the brand, add a valid business email, and submit it for verification to make it Auth+ compliant. This process follows industry standards for business verification, like those on platforms like Google My Business and Facebook Business Manager. For more information, please refer to the [Making the Existing Brands Authentication+ Compliant](https://help.webexconnect.io/docs/authentication-using-2fa#making-the-existing-brands-authentication-compliant) section.
### 
What should I do to renew my Auth+ if the renewal is pending or expired?
[](https://help.webexconnect.io/docs/authentication-using-2fa#what-should-i-do-to-renew-my-auth-if-the-renewal-is-pending-or-expired)
If Auth+ status is nearing expiry or already expired, brand owners will see a ‘Renew Auth+’ option for periodic re-verification to maintain up-to-date records. For more information, please refer to the [Renewing the Authentication+ for your Brands](https://help.webexconnect.io/docs/authentication-using-2fa#renewing-authentication-for-your-brands) section.
### 
Do I get an alert if the Brand Auth+ is about to expire or has expires?
[](https://help.webexconnect.io/docs/authentication-using-2fa#do-i-get-an-alert-if-the-brand-auth-is-about-to-expire-or-has-expires)
If Auth+ status is nearing expiry or already expired, brand owners and tenant owners will receive and an email from the Webex Connect platform with the guidance on the next steps. 
### 
I am not able to see the email, what should I do?
[](https://help.webexconnect.io/docs/authentication-using-2fa#i-am-not-able-to-see-the-email-what-should-i-do)
During the verification process, if the brand owner doesn’t receive the email, there’s an option to resend the two-factor authentication (2FA) email. For more information, please refer to the [Resending the Authentication+ Email](https://help.webexconnect.io/docs/authentication-using-2fa#resending-the-authentication-email) section.
### 
What should I do, when my email verification fails?
[](https://help.webexconnect.io/docs/authentication-using-2fa#what-should-i-do-when-my-email-verification-fails)
If verification fails, a support ticket is automatically generated and sent to our PSG team, who will proactively reach out to resolve the issue.
### 
What are the guidelines for adding the Business email address?
[](https://help.webexconnect.io/docs/authentication-using-2fa#what-are-the-guidelines-for-adding-the-business-email-address)
Please refer to the following guidelines for adding the Business email addresses:
  * Please add a valid email matching the brand or organizations' domain during brand registration.
  * Ensure that the email is not a group or alias email such as sales@brand.com, support@brand.com. The correct input would be a brand representative's email ID representing the brand.


This change is currently applicable to only Public Traded Entity Types and will be rolled out for other Entity Types in the future. 
TCR will refund the cost incurred in reverting existing Public Profit brands with a 'VERIFIED' or 'VETTED_VERIFIED' identity status that choose to go through Authentication+ verification.
### 
What are the different failures associated with 2FA verification?
[](https://help.webexconnect.io/docs/authentication-using-2fa#what-are-the-different-failures-associated-with-2fa-verification)
The following error codes are associated with 2FA verification failures:  
| Error Code  | Message  | Description  | Recommended Action  |  
| --- | --- | --- | --- |  
| 21736  | Domain Ownership Could Not Be Verified  | The domain associated with the brand contact email could not be validated.  | Use an email domain clearly tied to your brand. Contact our support team if you believe your domain should qualify.  |  
| 21737  | 2FA Verification Expired  | The 2FA authentication link or token expired before it was used.  | Re-do the steps mentioned in [Making the Existing Brands Authentication+ Compliant](https://help.webexconnect.io/docs/authentication-using-2fa#making-the-existing-brands-authentication-compliant) section.  |  
| 21738  | 2FA Email Undeliverable  | The verification email failed to reach the brand contact because the email address or domain was invalid.  | Ensure the email address is correct, active, and can receive external messages. Update the contact email if needed.  |  
| 21739  | 2FA Verification Failed or Timed Out  | The verification process failed to complete successfully.  | Re-do the steps mentioned in [Making the Existing Brands Authentication+ Compliant](https://help.webexconnect.io/docs/authentication-using-2fa#making-the-existing-brands-authentication-compliant) section.  |  
| 21740  | Invalid Brand Contact Email Domain  | The domain used in the brand contact email is not supported for Auth+ verification. (e.g., gmail.com or yahoo.com etc).  | Use a business or organizational email domain associated with your brand. Avoid free or generic email providers.  |  
| 21741  | 2FA Code Expired  | The verification code expired before it was entered  | Re-do the steps mentioned in [Making the Existing Brands Authentication+ Compliant](https://help.webexconnect.io/docs/authentication-using-2fa#making-the-existing-brands-authentication-compliant) section.  |
