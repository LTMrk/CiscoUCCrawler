Voice feedback helps people with vision problems to use their phone. When enabled, a voice
        prompt helps you navigate the phone screen and use or configure phone features.

The voice also reads out incoming caller IDs, touchable area information, screen summary,
        virtual keyboard keys, hard keys, and softkey information.

## Configure voice feedback

You can enable voice feedback from phone administration page. You can also set the volume
        and speed of the voice message.

When you enable voice feedback, you hear the following:

- A voice reads out an incoming call and tells the caller ID.

- A voice reads out what buttons you have selected when you click the selectable area.

- A voice reads out the current screen, including the home screen.

- Announcement of important messages when you single-click the menu that contains the
          information.

- A voice reads out the screen name followed by the application that is displayed on the
          phone when you press hard keys once, such as the contacts, applications, and messages
          buttons.

Access the phone administration web page.

Select Voice > User .

Navigate to Voice Feedback section and set the parameters as described below:

Options: Yes|No

Default: No

Options: Lowest|Low|Normal|High|Highest

Default: Normal

Options: Slowest|Slower|Normal|Faster|Fastest

Default: Normal

Options: Yes|No

Default: No
