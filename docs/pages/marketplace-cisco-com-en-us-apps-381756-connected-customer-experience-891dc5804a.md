# Connected Customer eXperience

by Discovery

People BI analytics and reporting as it relates to the correlation of people, activity, associated data, and the physical locations they inhabit

## Overview

### Overview

CCX is our Wi-Fi and CCTV People Business Intelligence, data and motion analytics/management platform, a custom designed service that delivers meaningful insights and easy-to-use reporting - perfectly suited to the complexities and demands of all smart physical spaces enabled by Meraki Cameras, Wireless, and IoT networks.

Discovery Technology, a subsidiary of Data # 3 Limited, developed version 1 of our customer inspired Connected Customer eXperience (CCX) location services platform over 10 years ago and more than a decade later its polished collection of marketing, facilities management, security, and business functions focus’ on the analysis of people and people related activities in physical locations ranging in size from transport, rooms, and concourses to urban spaces.

Ever expanding capabilities include:

- Wireless and AI based people movement and occupancy analytics

- Camera and AI based people counting, path analytics and demographic information

- Privacy focused data collection without facial recognition

- Omni-data analytics input capable (Wi-Fi, Camera, IoT, Weather, PoS, CRM……)

- Guest Wi-Fi with flexible landing page engagement formatting and monetisation support

- Embedded rules-based engine allowing more granular data filtering

- User customisable dynamic reporting and analytics rather than forced templates.

- Zone based movement analytics and reporting

- Machine learning based occupancy forecast verses actual analytics

- Dashboard with Customisable real time widgets

- Data (totals, percentages, path breakdown, change since last period selected etc.) presented for ease of reuse in internal reports and business cases.

- Time aligned analytics and reporting for root cause analysis

- Aggregate analytics for comparison of multiple sites

- AI based service queue management

- Pull API for data transfer

New feature development requests can be undertaken

Deployed by Airports, Local Government, Casinos, Property Management, Retail, Mining, Healthcare and Education to name a few.

The CCX platform can be licensed by its functional components or as-a-whole. The platform is remotely setup and configured to specification. We can provide as much or as little assistance as you require to get started and ongoing. Training via video conference is provided. 24 x 7 support logging is available.

### FAQs

#### Is it possible to do a proof of Concept

- Yes depending on your requirements, please contact us on 1800 116 009 or email sales@discovery.com.au to discuss how we can help.

#### How long does it take to implement CCX

- Install duration is driven by  the number of devices and the type of project for example if all hardware is already installed and operational and there are 5 devices it may take an hour but if it is a large site with 50-100 devices it will take longer.

#### Does CCX support multiple sites

- Yes CCX allows you to setup your sites in a hierarchical structure that can be broken down by Country, State, Region etc.

#### Does CCX have to be installed on Premise

- CCX is a cloud based platform allowing you to utilise it from anywhere in the world.

#### Is any additional Hardware Required

- No if you are using Meraki MR, MV, MX, MS or MT hardware no additional hardware is required to utilise the CCX cloud platform as it integrates directly into the Meraki ecosystem.
