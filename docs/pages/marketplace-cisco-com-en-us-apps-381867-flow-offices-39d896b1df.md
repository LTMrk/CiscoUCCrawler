## Overview

### Overview

Key Features

- Actionable real-time reporting and historical insights on staff presence, office and meeting room usage, as well as space utilization.

- Automatic Staff Alerts can be sent to your team when zones or areas reach preset levels of occupancy so they can adjust operations accordingly. Use for optimizing cleaning schedules, meeting room usage, open work areas, and more.

- Display your real-time occupancy and utilization metrics on on-site Digital Signage

- Seamlessly connect your existing Meraki APs and MV cameras to build a comprehensive view of employee experiences and office operations across thousands of locations.

### FAQs

#### Can Flow be installed at multiple locations?

- Absolutely!  Flow was built to deliver actionable insight into 100s or 1000s of locations.   Flow consolidates your data from multiple locations to a single powerful platform allowing you to enable data-driven decision-making at all organizational levels.

#### What camera hardware integrates with the Flow platform?

- Flow integrates camera hardware from a variety of vendors. Including, Cisco Meraki, Brickstream, Irisys, TDI, Vion, and any other smart camera vendor that supports an API.

#### Does Flow support existing Cisco Meraki hardware?

- Yes! Flow is an end-to-end partner of the Meraki ecosystem, Flow Analytics thrives when combined with the Meraki MR APs, Meraki MV cameras, and MT20 Door Sensors to provide a unified view of your customer behaviour.

#### How does Flow work with MV Smart Cameras?

- Flow leverages the MVSense API and people detection capabilities to allow for persistent identification of customers walking through an office or store. 
The Flow platform ingests this data and unifies it with all other data sources to provide a consolidated view of behaviour in your locations.

#### How do I integrate Cisco Meraki with Flow?

- Getting your Meraki hardware connected to Flow Spaces is easy and can be done within 30 minutes. 
1. Sign up with Flow 
2. Login to the Meraki online dashboard and navigate to the organization where AP's are to be integrated with Flow. 
3. Configure the APs and Cameras and send the validator string to Flow
4. Post the URL and secret in the Meraki dashboard.
