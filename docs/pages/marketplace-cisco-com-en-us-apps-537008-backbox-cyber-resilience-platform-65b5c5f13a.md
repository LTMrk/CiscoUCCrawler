# BackBox Cyber Resilience Platform

by BackBox

BackBox is an enterprise network cyber resilience platform, empowering network and security teams to automate critical processes, maintain business continuity during disruptions, and achieve rapid recovery with a strong ROI.

## Overview

### Address Your Network Cyber Resilience Needs

The BackBox cyber resilience platform automates network infrastructure security, compliance, and lifecycle management, supporting over 180 vendors with thousands of pre-built automations and a no-code way to create new ones. Our API-driven platform empowers teams to automate critical network processes, maintain business continuity, and recover swiftly.

### Key benefits

- Security Proactively identify active exposures to which you are vulnerable and choose to automatically mitigate the vulnerability by making a configuration change or remediate the vulnerability by updating the device software.

- Compliance Quickly and easily run compliance checks on all devices to assess resilience and identify configuration drift. Automatically correct non-compliant configurations to align with industry-standard compliance frameworks and internal golden configurations.

- Automation Back up all your devices, validate them, and restore them with a single click for fast and reliable recovery in case of a service disruption. Over 3,000 pre-built automations provide a single pane of glass for upgrading, patching, and managing all major network vendors with a consistent interface.
