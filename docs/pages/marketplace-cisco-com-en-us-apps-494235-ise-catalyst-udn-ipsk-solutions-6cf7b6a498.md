# ISE + Catalyst UDN / IPSK Solutions

by Splash Access

SplashAccess with ISE User Defined Network provides each end-user with their own network partition. Via our web app, end-users can securely and easily register their devices.

## Overview

### ISE + Catalyst UDN / IPSK Solutions

Cisco ISE + SplashAccess - That Home experience on a single SSID !!

### Key benefits

- Easily deployable, managed, and monitored End users enjoy easy device registration and management of their personal network through a Splash Access portal while IT benefits from monitoring and visibility of all devices on the network.

- Secure and segmented personal networks End users can securely onboard their personal devices and can restrict others from network and device access. Users control their devices and guests on network partitions, reducing IT support because of user connectivity issues.

- Frictionless experiences for users and IT Network admins and end users experience seamless onboarding and use of the network. IT admins get full visibility of all user devices through Cisco Catalyst assurance. No surprises: you know exactly who and what's on the network.

- Easy to Deploy User Defined Network Plus The essential components of User Defined Network Plus include the customer's Catalyst 9800 Series Wireless Controller and Wave 2 and newer access points. Licensing for Cisco Identity Services Engine (ISE) and the Splash Access portal that allows users to register their own devices is required for the solution

- Flexibility and Control Providing complete branding customization for portals and emails, which includes adding your logo and main accent colors  and personalized experience

- User Administration group administrators will have the ability to efficiently manage properties, students , resident services via Azure , SAML or Other IDP providors.

- Simple Intergration Seamless integration into ISE with a simple wizard to automate the process of creating all the tags and requirments .

- Student Portal Full access via IDP into SplashAccess to manager all a users devices . Group administrators will be able to efficiently manage properties, students, and resident services through Azure, SAML, or other identity providers (IdPs)

- IOT Management Allocated to a user SplashAccess now has the ability to bulk upload endpoints into correct Identity groups for ease of management of IOT devices in bulk .
