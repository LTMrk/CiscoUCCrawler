The easiest and first step to clearing the Webex cache is to reset it through the Webex App. This can be done within the application by going to Help > Health Checker and clicking on Reset .

If that does not resolve the issue, or you cannot launch the Webex App, please continue with the steps below.

- Windows

- Mac

Follow the steps below to clear the Webex cache on Microsoft Windows:

- Sign out of and close the Webex client.

- Open the Task Manager by holding Ctrl + Alt + Del on your keyboard.

- Click More details .

- On the Processes tab, right-click Webex and select End Task .

- Right-click on the Windows (Start) button and select Run .

- In the pop-up box, enter %USERPROFILE%\AppData\Local

- Locate the CiscoSpark folder. Delete all contents of the folder.

- Launch Webex

- Sign out of and close the Webex client.

- Open Finder .

- In the Finder menu bar, click Go > Go to Folder...

- Enter ~/Library/Caches

- Locate the Cisco-Systems.Spark folder and/or the Cisco Spark folder. Delete all contents of the folder.

- In the menu bar, click Go > Go to Folder...

- Enter ~/Library/Application Support

- Locate the Cisco-Systems.Spark folder and/or the Cisco Spark folder. Delete all contents of the folder.

- Launch Webex
