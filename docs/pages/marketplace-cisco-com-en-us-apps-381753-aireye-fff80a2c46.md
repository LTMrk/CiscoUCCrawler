# AirEye

by AirEye

AirEye enforces your overall wireless security policy and prevents over-the-air attacks. SaaS, laborless, non-intrusive.

## Overview

### Overview

With AirEye, banking and financial institutions, healthcare delivery organizations, manufacturing plants, retail and hospitality organizations, as well as enterprises build and enforce their wireless security policy.

This policy ensures that the organization’s IT and IoT devices do not connect to available networks such as Guest or a random hotspot, potentially leaking sensitive data and violating security regulations and standards.

Through its protection mechanism, AirEye ensures that any organizational wireless device does not expose the organization’s private wired and wireless networks to unauthorized network access, device hijacking, ransomware and other malware, segmentation hopping as well as data leakage. This also includes protection for the organization’s shadow networks - wireless connections generated by corporate devices that connect to, generate, and/ or open their own Wi-Fi networks or Peer to Peer connections.

Unlike solutions that focus only on the corporate network, such as WIDS/ WIPS, network security or IoT security solutions , AirEye looks at the complete organizational airspace. This includes shadow networks and non-owned wireless devices (such as security cameras, smartphones and IoT devices) and identifies legitimate connections versus those that do not comply with the security policy and protects the organization’s managed and unmanaged devices from unauthorized connections.

### FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

#### What is AirEye Dome?

#### How does AirEye work?

- 1. Monitor all wireless channels
 AirEye constantly monitors and analyzes all wireless communication channels in real time.

2. Identify and Classify
AirEye quickly identifies corporate assets and channels and  classifies the different types of devices and networks around the channel.

3. Control
AirEye detects violations of wireless security policy and automatically terminates any potential connections between devices involved. 

4. Prevent
AirEye detects malicious interactions and terminates the attack

#### What channels does AirEye cover?

- AirEye covers the wireless channels in your network airspace.

#### How easy is it to get started with the AirEye service?

#### Does AirEye impact our network performance?

- No, AirEye analyzes only the metadata of the traffic passing through Meraki’s Access Points. The analysis is performed on AirEye’s cloud service.

#### What is your privacy policy?

- AirEye’s privacy policy ensures that Personal Identifiable Information (PII) is handled properly. You can find it here: https://aireye.tech/aireye-privacy-policy/

#### What type of forensic data does AirEye provide?

- Whenever AirEye issues an alert, it collects all the relevant metadata (description of the related wireless events) and the actual raw data, including all broadcast frames from a few minutes before the alert and a few minutes later. Together, the platform provides all the necessary forensic data required for the investigation.

#### Does AirEye jam networks?

- No, AirEye does not jam networks. The platform terminates the precise violating connection.

#### Does AirEye interfere with my neighbors’ networks?

- No. AirEye only terminates the connection from the company’s equipment to the unauthorized or unmonitored network. All other networks and connections continue as usual.
