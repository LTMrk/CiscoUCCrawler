Follow these steps:

- Sign out of and close the Webex client.

- Open Finder .

- In the Finder menu bar, click Go > Go to Folder...

- Enter ~/Library/Caches

- Locate the Cisco-Systems.Spark folder and/or the Cisco Spark folder. Delete all contents of the folder.

- In the menu bar, click Go > Go to Folder...

- Enter ~/Library/Application Support

- Locate the Cisco-Systems.Spark folder and/or the Cisco Spark folder. Delete all contents of the folder.

- Launch Webex
