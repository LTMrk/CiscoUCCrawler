Webex video devices

Want to know where you can sell Webex video devices? Use the Ciscofax tool to easily determine where you can sell:

Using Mozilla Firefox or Internet Explorer for the best experience, go to the Ciscofax tool.

Select Product Certifications by Country and select Go .

Review the Product Approvals Status page.

Scroll to Option 2 and in the Name dialog box, enter the SKU of the Webex device you want to confirm approval status.

All video device SKUs can be found in the Cisco Video Devices Ordering Guide .

When the search has returned a result for the Cisco video device SKU, select that SKU from the Product Found list and move to Selected Product(s) list.

Select Country and then click Submit .

Ciscofax will return a list of countries where that Cisco video device can be sold.

If your country or region is not on the list and you potentially require it for future opportunity please complete the following Smartsheet and the Business Unit Product Manager and/or World Wide Collaboration Sales team will get back to you.

Important notices about selling Webex Meetings and Webex App

### Cloud services restricted countries

Cisco is unable to sell Webex Meetings and Webex App paid subscriptions in Mainland China, South Sudan and Yemen at this time. If you have any questions regarding the sale of Webex Meetings and Webex App paid subscriptions in these countries, please contact your local Cisco Account Manager or Partner Account Manager.
