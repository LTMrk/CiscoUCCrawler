# ScienceLogic SL1

by ScienceLogic

SL1, ScienceLogic’s AIOps and observability platform, monitors customer data across clouds and on-premises, enabling actionable insights to predict and resolve service-impacting problems faster, powered by machine-driven analytics and automation.

## Overview

### Observability and AIOps Platform

ScienceLogic delivers a comprehensive, AI-driven automation platform tailored for today's complex, distributed IT environments. At its core is SL1, our industry-leading AIOps and Observability solution, supported by Restorepoint for secure configuration and compliance management, PowerFlow for intelligent workflow automation and integration, and Skylar AI for advanced predictive analytics and machine learning. Together, these capabilities break down organizational silos, streamline IT operations, and provide real-time visibility across hybrid cloud, multi-cloud, and on-premises environments. Through automated discovery, accurate service dependency mapping, intelligent data correlation, and predictive insights, ScienceLogic enables IT teams to proactively address issues, enhance service availability, and operate more efficiently. ScienceLogic also delivers native integrations with over 500 technologies, including deep interoperability with Cisco solutions such as ACI, Meraki, DNA Center, and AppDynamics. This integration maximizes the value of Cisco investments, empowering customers to accelerate digital transformation through smarter, more efficient operations.

### Key benefits

- End to End Observability with SL1 ScienceLogic SL1 provides comprehensive visibility across your IT ecosystem, from infrastructure to applications to services. With deep integration into Cisco technologies, SL1 enables real-time monitoring, correlation, and service mapping across hybrid and multi-cloud environments. • Discover and map infrastructure, applications, and services automatically, including Cisco platforms like ACI, Meraki, and AppDynamics • Correlate and contextualize operational data to reduce alert noise and improve incident resolution • Visualize service dependencies and health with real-time topology views enriched with Cisco telemetry

- Configuration and Compliance Control Restorepoint delivers secure, automated configuration and compliance management for network devices, including full support for Cisco hardware. It helps reduce risk, enforce policy adherence, and enable rapid recovery from misconfigurations or failures. • Automatically back up and version Cisco device configurations with full audit trails • Detect unauthorized changes and ensure compliance with internal and industry standards • Restore configurations quickly across Cisco routers, switches, firewalls, and more

- Workflow Integration and Automation PowerFlow enables seamless integration between ScienceLogic and external IT systems such as ITSM, cloud, and DevOps tools. It automates event handling, ticketing, and response workflows to reduce manual effort and improve operational agility. • Automate end-to-end workflows across your IT ecosystem • Connect to platforms like ServiceNow, AWS, Azure, and Cisco DNA Center • Enable closed-loop incident management and auto-remediation

- Predictive Intelligence and AI Guidance Skylar AI delivers actionable insights by applying machine learning to operational data. It predicts incidents, identifies root causes, and prioritizes issues based on business impact, helping IT teams act faster and smarter. • Detect patterns and anomalies before they become critical issues • Accelerate root cause analysis with correlated context • Focus resources on high-impact incidents with intelligent prioritization
