# PagerDuty for Catalyst Center

by Cisco

Catalyst Center with PagerDuty delivers real-time network alerts and AI-driven insights to the right teams. Automatically notify on-call engineers, accelerate triage and root cause analysis, and improve uptime with faster issue resolution.

## Overview

The integration between Cisco Catalyst Center and PagerDuty enables the delivery of event notifications to PagerDuty. The integration enables IT organizations to subscribe to network issues identified by Cisco DNA Center and send notifications to PagerDuty. Customers have a real-time network issues view on the PagerDuty platform, which improves issue resolution and increases network uptime.

### Key benefits

- Identify relevant issues Cisco Catalyst Center's AI-driven insights and anomaly detection quickly identify the most important issues, so IT teams can focus on business critical functions.

- Resolve problems faster Cisco Catalyst Center's Machine Reasoning Engine provides triage and root cause analysis of complex issues and helps IT solve problems faster than previously possible.

- Engage the right people Automatically notify an on-call engineers, automate escalation processes, and keep stakeholders informed with Cisco and PagerDuty integration.
