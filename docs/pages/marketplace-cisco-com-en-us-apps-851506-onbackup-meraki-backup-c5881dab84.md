# OnBackup - Meraki Backup

by Onnen Solutions

Automated configuration backup for MSPs & IT Teams managing Meraki at scale. Every org, every network, every change, captured, versioned, and restorable in minutes.

## Overview

### Automated configuration backup for MSPs & IT Teams managing Meraki at scale

One misconfigured firewall rule. One template change that cascaded further than expected. One engineer who left without documenting anything. For an MSP running dozens of Meraki organisations or conscientious IT Teams, the Dashboard tells you what your networks look like now, ONbackup tells you what they looked like before, and gets you back there fast. 

ONbackup automatically captures your Meraki Dashboard configuration across every organisation and network you manage,  firewall rules, SSIDs, VLANs, Switch Ports, VPN's, and more, on a schedule you control. When something breaks, you don't reconstruct settings from memory or trawl the change log: you see exactly what changed, and restore with confidence.

Built for MSPs and IT Teams: multi-org from day one, per-client separation, and enterprise-grade security on Microsoft Azure. Set it up once, and stop hoping nothing goes wrong.

### Key benefits

- Built for MSPs & IT Teams, not single networks Manage backups across all your client organisations from one pane of glass. Per-client separation, at-a-glance health status, and no per-org juggling of API keys and scripts.

- Recover in minutes, not afternoons When a change goes wrong, restore known-good configuration directly, no rebuilding firewall rules from screenshots or knowledge bases, no reconstructing SSIDs from memory.

- Know exactly what changed Every backup is a point-in-time record. Compare snapshots to pinpoint what changed, when, turning "the Wi-Fi broke sometime last week" into a two-minute answer.

- Set it and forget it Automated, scheduled backups run without anyone remembering to click a button. Your newest client org is protected the day you onboard it, not the day someone gets round to it.

- Security your clients can audit Hosted on Microsoft Azure with encryption throughout, local data residency. When your client's compliance team asks questions, you have answers.

- Protection beyond the Dashboard's memory Meraki's change log shows what happened; it doesn't give the configuration back. ONbackup keeps independent, restorable copies outside the Dashboard, real disaster recovery, not just an audit trail.

- Multi-Organisation Backup ONbackup is built for MSPs and IT Teams from the ground up. Connect every Meraki organisation you manage and see backup health across your entire client base at a glance, no per-org scripts, no juggling API keys, no spreadsheet tracking who's protected and who isn't. New client onboarded? Protected the same day.

- Automated Scheduled Backups Backups run automatically on the schedule you choose — daily, weekly, or aligned to your change windows. No engineer has to remember to click a button, and no client goes unprotected because someone was on leave. You get notified when a backup completes, and more importantly, when one doesn't.

- Deep Configuration Coverage Firewall rules, SSIDs, VLANs, switch port profiles, admin roles, VPN settings and more, captured as structured, versioned configuration data. Community scripts and DIY approaches typically cover a fraction of the Dashboard; ONbackup is maintained as a product, updated as the Meraki API evolves.

- Fast, Targeted Restore When a change goes wrong, restore a known-good configuration directly, the whole network or just the settings that broke. No rebuilding firewall rules from screenshots, no reconstructing an SSID from a colleague's memory, no support ticket that lasts all afternoon.
With our "Snapback" feature, if a restore goes wrong it automatically reverts to the last know good point.

- Point-in-Time Change Visibility Every backup is a timestamped record of your client's network. Compare any two snapshots to pinpoint what changed between them using the "Compare" feature, turning "the guest Wi-Fi broke sometime last week" into a two-minute investigation instead of an archaeology project.

- Enterprise-Grade Security & Local Data Residency Hosted on Microsoft Azure with local residency, with managed identities, secrets held in Azure Key Vault, and encryption in transit and at rest. A ready-made Data Processing Agreement and UK GDPR-aligned processing mean that when your client's compliance team sends the security questionnaire, you already have the answers.
