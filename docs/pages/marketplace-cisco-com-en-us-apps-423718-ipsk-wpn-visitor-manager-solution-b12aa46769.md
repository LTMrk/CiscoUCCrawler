# IPSK / WPN Visitor Manager Solution

by Splash Access

Secure IPSk - WPN Solution with a Fully managed devices that is easy and simple to use . Group or Individual IPSK with QR codes , Encrypted WiFi and automatic traffic segregation for Groups. Options to print out codes directly to individual end users .

## Overview

### Secure IPSK / WPN QR Visitor Management

Secure Onboarding with QR codes in a Managed Or Self managed solution . SplashAccess WPN (Wireless Private Network) offers a secure and reliable Visitor WiFi solution with the added feature of IPSK (Individual Pre-Shared Key) authentication. IPSK authentication allows each visitor to have their own unique and private key for accessing the WiFi network. With SplashAccess WPN and IPSK QR Codes , the WiFi network is protected by encrypting the communication between the user's device and the network. This ensures that only authorized users with their specific pre-shared key can connect to the network. The use of IPSK enhances security by eliminating the shared passphrase or password typically used for WiFi access. Instead, each user is assigned an individual key, which reduces the risk of unauthorised access and strengthens the overall network security. By implementing SplashAccess WPN with IPSK, organisations can provide a secure WiFi  Visitor Guest environment while still offering the flexibility and convenience of WiFi access to their users.
