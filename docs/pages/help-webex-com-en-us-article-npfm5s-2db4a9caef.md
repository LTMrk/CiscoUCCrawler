## Update your headset firmware

To begin your upgrade, perform one of these actions:

The Cisco Headsets app won't upgrade your headset when the headset battery is low. Make sure your
                headset is charged before you upgrade.

- Connect your headset with the USB cable to a Cisco IP phone.

- Connect your headset with the USB cable to a computer with Cisco Jabber.

- Connect your headset with the USB cable to a computer with Webex App.

You can also update your Cisco Headset USB Adapter through Cisco Accessory Hub.

Follow the on-screen instructions.
