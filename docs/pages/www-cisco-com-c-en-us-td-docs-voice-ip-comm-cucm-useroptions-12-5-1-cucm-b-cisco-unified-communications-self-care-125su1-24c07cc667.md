# Instant Message and Presence Status

## Let Others Know That You're Busy

You can set Do Not Disturb status only on your company-provided phones.

From Unified Communications Self Care Portal , choose IM & Availability > Do Not Disturb .

Check the Turn on check box and click Save .

## Share Your Work Status

From Unified Communications Self Care Portal , choose IM & Availability > Status Policy .

Check the Automatically update status when there is a meeting on my calendar check box, click Status Policy , and click Save .
