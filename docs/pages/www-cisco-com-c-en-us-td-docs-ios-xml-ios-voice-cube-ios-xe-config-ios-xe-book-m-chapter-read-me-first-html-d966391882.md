### Important Information about Cisco IOS XE 16

Effective Cisco IOS XE Release 3.7.0E for Catalyst Switching and Cisco IOS XE Release 3.17S (for Access and Edge Routing)
                              the two releases evolve (merge) into a single version of converged release—the Cisco IOS XE 16—providing one release covering
                              the extensive range of access and edge products in the Switching and Routing portfolio.
