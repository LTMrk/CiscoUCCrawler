# Remove and replace the battery

These instructions are provided in order to comply with the EU Battery Replaceability Requirement.

This product was not specifically designed for battery replacement in the field.

It is recommended that, whenever possible, follow the standard repair or return process for battery issues.

Caution

Always wear an ESD-preventive wrist strap and ensure that it makes good contact with your skin when you remove or install
                                          the internal modules. Connect the equipment end of the wrist strap to the metal part of the chassis.

## Remove the battery

Perform this procedure when the battery requires replacement or maintenance on supported hardware.

Follow proper electrostatic discharge (ESD) precautions before handling the battery or device.

Wear an ESD-preventive wrist strap, ensuring that it makes good skin contact. Connect the clip to an unpainted surface of
                                          the chassis frame to channel unwanted ESD voltages safely to ground. To guard against ESD damage and shocks, the wrist strap
                                          and cord must operate effectively.

Warning

Statement 1015 —Battery Handling

To reduce risk of fire, explosion or leakage of flammable liquid or gas:

Replace the battery only with the same or equivalent type recommended by the manufacturer.

Do not dismantle, crush, puncture, use sharp tool to remove, short external contacts, or dispose of in fire.

Do not use if battery is warped or swollen.

Step 1

Before you perform any module replacement, read the Safety Warnings section and disconnect the power supplies.

Step 2

To access the battery, remove the 13 screws (5 from  top and 4 on each side) from the cover and remove the cover.

Step 3

Insert a thin tipped screw-driver in the gap of the holder and gently push the battery away from the screw-driver tip.

Step 4

Rotate the screw tip to release the battery from the holder.

## Replace the battery

Follow proper electrostatic discharge (ESD) precautions before handling the battery or device.

## Before you begin

The battery is a BR2032 type. Batteries should only be replaced with the same or an equivalent type recommended by the manufacturer.

Panasonic BR-2032/BN

Step 1

Read the Safety Warnings instructions and disconnect the power supplies before you perform any module replacement.

Step 2

Orient the battery with positive polarity as shown.

Step 3

Insert the battery into the holder so that one edge is under the overhang.

Step 4

Press the opposite edge down so it snaps into the holder.

Step 5

Replace the cover and secure the screws.
