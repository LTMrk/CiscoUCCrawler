Click your profile picture and then click Settings > Meetings .

Under Meetings , find Default Webex Site URL and then click Edit .

Select your desired Webex site, click Update .

If you have a host license on more than one Webex site, you can select one as your default for Personal Rooms, devices,
                            contact cards, in-app scheduler and more.
