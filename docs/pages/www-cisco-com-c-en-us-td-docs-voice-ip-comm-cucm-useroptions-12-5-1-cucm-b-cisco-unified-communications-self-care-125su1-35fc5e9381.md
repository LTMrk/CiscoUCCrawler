Cisco Unified Communications Self Care Portal User Guide, Release 12.5(1) SU1

# Cisco Unified Communications Self Care Portal User Guide, Release 12.5(1) SU1

Updated: June 19, 2019

Chapter: Get Started

## Chapter: Get Started

# Get Started

## Launch Self Care Portal

### Before you begin

The Self Care Portal URL

Your username and password

Enter the Self Care Portal URL in the web browser address bar:  For example, https://<server_name:portnumber>/ucmuser . Note that the address format may vary depending on how the administrator configures the server.

Enter your username and password and click Sign In .

## Set Your Preferred Language

From Unified Communications Self Care Portal , choose General Settings > Language .

From the Display Language drop-down list, choose your preferred language, and click Save .

## Change Your Phone Display Name

From Unified Communications Self Care Portal , choose Phones > My Phones .

Hover over the name of your phone, click the Settings icon, and choose Edit .

Enter the new name of the phone in the Description field, and then click Save .

## Add Your Additional Phones to Self Care Portal

From Unified Communications Self Care Portal , choose Phones > My Phones .

Click the Add New icon.

Enter the phone number and description in the respective fields.

If you want to enable single number reach (answer your calls from any other device or phones) on your new phone, check the Enable Single Number Reach check box.

If you've added your mobile phone as your new phone, check the Enable Move to Mobile check box.

If you want to enable the Cisco Jabber features on your new phone, check the Enable Extend and Connect check box.

Click Save .

## Activate Your Phone

If your phone is not active, you see the Ready to Activate message on your phone in the Self Care Portal.

From Unified Communications Self Care Portal , choose Phones > My Phones .

Choose your phone, and click View Activation Code .

Use one of the options to activate your phone:

Enter the 16-digit activation code on your new phone.

If your phone has a video camera, you can use the camera to scan the barcode.

Click Save .

## Set Up Phone Services

From Unified Communications Self Care Portal , choose Phones > Phones Settings > Services .

Choose your phone and click Add New Services .

Choose the required services from the Services drop-down list, enter the display name in the Display Name field, and click Save .

## Change Your Portal Password

You can't generate a new password if you have forgotten it. To generate a new password, contact your network administrator.

From Unified Communications Self Care Portal , choose General Settings > Client/Portal Password .

Enter your new password in the New Password field, re-enter it in the Confirm New Password field, and then click Save .

## Change Your Phone Services PIN

The PIN that you enter must meet the credential policy defined in Unified Communications Manager. For example, if the credential
                                          policy specifies a minimum PIN length of 7 digits, the PIN that you enter should be at least 7 digits long and cannot exceed
                                          128 digits. For more information, contact your system administrator.

From Unified Communications Self Care Portal , choose General Settings > Phone Services PIN .

Enter the PIN in the New Phone PIN field, re-enter it in the Confirm New Phone PIN field, and then click Save .

## Schedule a Meeting Using an Access Code

From Unified Communications Self Care Portal , choose General Settings > Conference .

Enter the meeting number in the Meeting Number field.

Enter the access code in the Attendees Access Code field, and then click Save .

## Download the Required Plug-ins

From Unified Communications Self Care Portal, choose Downloads .

Choose the plug-in that you want to download and click the Download button.

## Download Your Phone User Manual

From Unified Communications Self Care Portal , choose Phones > My Phones .

Hover over your additional phone, click the Settings icon, and choose Download Manual .

## Sign out From Self Care Portal

From Unified Communications Self Care Portal , click your display name, and choose Sign Out . Your display name appears at the top right corner of the home page. If you haven't configured your display name, your user
                              ID appears on the screen.
