## About This
                        	 Guide

This guide describes how to install, configure, and use the Cisco
                           		Contact Center Gateway feature. The Contact Center Gateway enables Cisco
                           		Unified Contact Center Enterprise (Unified CCE) to appear as an ACD (Automatic
                           		Call Distributor) to Cisco Unified Intelligent Contact Management in a
                           		Parent/Child deployment.

## Audience

This guide is intended for users who deploy and administer contact
                           		centers that use the Cisco Contact Center Gateway software.
