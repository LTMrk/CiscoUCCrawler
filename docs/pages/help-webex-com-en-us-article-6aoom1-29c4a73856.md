As a participant in a phone call or a Webex hybrid meeting, you can choose to show or hide
        the real-time translation and transcription on the phone screen by simply pressing the
        softkey Captions or tapping the soft button Closed
          captions (depending on your phone model).

When enabled, the closed captions will display just above the softkeys or soft buttons.

The following phone models support this feature:

- Cisco Desk Phone 9861 and 9871

- Cisco Video Phone 8875

If your phone doesn't show the Captions softkey or the soft button during a phone call or a Webex meeting, contact your administrator for
        support.

## Show closed captions

During a phone call or a Webex hybrid meeting, do one of following actions depending on
        your phone model:

To show captions:

Press Captions . Highlight Closed captions , and press On .

Tap Closed captions and toggle on Closed captions .

Select Caption language and choose the preferred caption
            language for translations. The system automatically translates a person's speech into
            transcripts that appear on the bottom of the screen. The default caption language is
            English.

The status (on or off) of closed captions and the selected language will be saved
            automatically for future calls and meetings.

If no one is speaking, the last caption disappears after 5 seconds.

## Limitations

Up to 4 rows can be displayed in the closed captions on the phone screen.

For Webex meetings, maximum of 5 caption languages can be translated from the server. The
        actual supported number varies depending on the backend limit.

For phone calls, the Captions softkey or the soft button is only available on the primary line with a single call.
