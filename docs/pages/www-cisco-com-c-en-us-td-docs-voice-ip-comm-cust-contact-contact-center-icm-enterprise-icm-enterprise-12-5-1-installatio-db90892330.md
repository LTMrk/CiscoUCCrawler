## About This Guide

## Audience

This guide is intended for contact center managers and system support
                           		personnel who are planning and preparing contact center sites for a Unified ICM
                           		system installation. Readers should be familiar with contact center site
                           		planning and preparation issues. They should also have a basic understanding of
                           		the Unified ICM system and the components that are installed as part of the
                           		system.
