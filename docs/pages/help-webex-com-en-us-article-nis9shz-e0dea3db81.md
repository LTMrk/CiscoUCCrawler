A queue is a waiting area where incoming or outgoing phone calls are temporarily stored and
        managed until they can be distributed automatically to agents (or picked manually) for
        handling. Users who receive calls from the queue are known as agents.

## Join or leave a call queue

As an agent, you can join or leave a call queue.

Before you begin

Your administrator configures Call Queue and Customer Assist for your phone. For more
        information on Webex Calling Call Queue configuration, see Configure Call Queue . For more information on Webex Calling
        Customer Assist, see Webex Calling Customer Assist .

Choose one of the following methods to join or leave the call queue.

Single line: select Queues on the phone screen.

Multiple lines: select the primary line, then do one of the following actions
              depending on your phone model:

Press Agents > Queues .

Tap More(…) > Queues .

- If the agent has mulitple phones, any change in the call queue status on one phone
                will be synchronized to other phones.

- The Queues softkey or button is only available with the
                primary line of the phone.

You can see call queue status on the Queues screen. The status
            can be Joined or Not joined .

Select Join to join a call queue.

The status of the call queue changes to Joined . You are able to
            recieve any incoming calls for that queue.

Select Leave to leave the call queue.

The status of call queue changes to Not joined . You are not able
            to recieve any incoming calls for that queue.
