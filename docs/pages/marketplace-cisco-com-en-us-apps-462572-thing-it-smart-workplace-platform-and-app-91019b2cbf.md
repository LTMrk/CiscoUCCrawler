# Thing-it Smart Workplace Platform and App

by Thing Technologies

Do you have to manage hybrid work and ESG on an enterprise scale? 

Thing-it transforms outdated office spaces into efficient and sustainable work environments.

With Thing-it you can easily:
- Rightsize your office space portfolio to the paradigm shift introduced by hybrid work.

- Meet your company's ESG requirements regarding energy consumption, carbon dioxide emissions, employee wellbeing and more.

## Overview

### Space management and workplace experience well-integrated with the Meraki platform

Do you have to manage hybrid work and ESG on an enterprise scale? 

Thing-it transforms outdated office spaces into efficient and sustainable work environments. 

It helps right-sizing your office space portfolio to the paradigm shift introduced by hybrid work and supports your employees coming 'back to the office' in a hybrid work environment with the best possible on-site workplace experience and frictionless use of the available space.

It also enables your company to meet its  ESG requirements regarding energy consumption, carbon dioxide emissions, employee wellbeing and more.

All that leveraging an existing or planned Meraki infrastructure with access points, sensors and cameras for the best transparency on workspace utilization and quality.

### Key benefits

- Optimize the use of your space resources Understand how desks, meeting rooms and parking lots are used by your employees. Get rid of space you do not need and ensure that the remaining space serves the requirements of your teams perfectly - even if those requirements are changing.

- Deliver great workplace experience Help your employees to make the most of their office day. From an easy planning of the office visit to a productive time on site. In a comfortable and efficient work emvironment.

- Connect all hardware and systems you need Flexible by design, with open APIs and hardware independence.

- Satisfy the requirements of your IT and compliance departments Built for enterprise environments, with data protection, security, and unlimited scalability.

- Desk and Space Booking Book meeting rooms and other shared resources like desks, parking lots in seconds.

- Space and Desk Occupancy Helps your employees to find free desks in their preferred areas and your workplace and space managers to right-size the available desks and zones through data analytics. With occupancy and people count measurements through an existing Meraki AP network and Meraki Smart Cameras.

- Indoor Climate and Air Quality Indoor climate/well-being dashboard.
Analytics data cube for Power BI, Tableau etc.
Notifications on CO2 threshold ‘Open window …’
Combined with occupancy, weather data, …
