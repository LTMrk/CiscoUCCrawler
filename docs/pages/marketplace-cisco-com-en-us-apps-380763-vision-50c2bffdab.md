## Features

### Footfall Analysis

Footfall Analysis provides real-time insights into visitor flow across stores, offices, public spaces, and events. It helps optimize resources, enhance security, improve customer experience, and measure the impact of initiatives, all through an intuitive dashboard.

### Customer Analysis

Customer Analysis delivers real-time insights into visitor demographics and behavior, such as age, gender, and emotions, helping optimize marketing, enhance user experience, and tailor services. It enables decision-making based on precise and up-to-date information across various industries.

### Occupancy

Occupancy delivers real-time insights into space usage and traffic patterns, supporting resource optimization, flow management, and strategic planning. It’s ideal for any environment (eg. retail, offices, transport, events, or manufacturing) where safety and efficiency matter.

### Zone Analytics

Zone Analytics offers a clear, visual view of presence and movement within a space, revealing patterns that support space optimization, resource management, and a better user experience. Applicable across sectors, from retail to manufacturing, it drives operational efficiency through data-driven decisions.

### Heatmaps

Heatmaps provide real-time insights into foot traffic and visitor movement, helping businesses understand behavior, optimize spaces, and improve customer experience. Ideal for Retail, Hospitality, Transportation, Museums, Public Administration, and Manufacturing, it supports evidence-based strategies to boost flow and engagement

### Queue Analysis

Queue Analysis provides a clear view of queue behavior, with insights on wait times and queue lengths. Ideal for Retail, Transport, Events, Museums, and Public Offices, it helps improve resource management and customer experience through better operational efficiency.

### Object Detection

Object Detection enhances existing cameras by identifying objects in real time, using AI and Machine Learning to deliver insights without manual monitoring. It enables businesses to extract valuable information from images across various use cases including inventory management, shelf monitoring, object sorting, abandoned object detection, PPE detection, and vehicle detection.
