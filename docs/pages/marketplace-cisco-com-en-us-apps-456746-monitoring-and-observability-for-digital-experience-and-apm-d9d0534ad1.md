## Features

### Quickly and automatically visualise and optimise the whole network

The Highlight platform imperceptibly and continuously collects data from nominated connections throughout a network estate. Create an API key in your Cisco dashboard, put it in Highlight and in less than 5 minutes the platform will discover and start collecting data on your network services and application tests.

That data is then translated and presented in a clear and understandable way, including visualisation of underlay, overlay, LAN and application test services. This enables IT teams and service managers to instantly know exactly what is working smoothly and what needs attention within their network estate.

### Instant status identification with colour-coded heat tiles

Network data is no use if it’s not understandable or actionable. The Highlight platform’s unique heat tiles instantly show the status of services and locations. They indicate the nature and quantity of issues present at that location or service, splitting issues into stability, load and health categories. Location tiles represent all the services in a particular location whilst service tiles can represent any collection of user-defined services, regardless of their physical location.

### Explore categorised service status at any location with grid view

For a more detailed overview of locations, you can use the grid view, which separates services into technology groups. Here you can see locations that are having issues with many services at once or many locations that are suffering from an issue with the same service. You can scan across the columns to see how different service categories are performing at any location or scan down the rows to see the status of different locations.

### FAQs

Read below to get answers to frequently asked questions.

#### How does Highlight integrate with Cisco services?

- Highlight uses the Cisco APIs to understand both the state of the network and to collect real time data on the network. Highlight allows you to configure integration per organisation, so if you have one support account with access to multiple orgs, you can pick and choose which org you want Highlight to integrate with. More info on setting up Highlight is available in our Help and Support site .

#### I already have a technical monitoring tool, why do I need Highlight?

- Technical monitoring tools are great, we use them a lot in Highlight, but they aren’t designed for the people who have to run a service who are typically non-technical, short of time and just want to get to the bottom of an issue quickly. Highlight Service Observability Platform supports a wide range of features designed specifically for service providers selling a commercial networking service to businesses, who need to assure the service for the customer. You can do some of that with technical monitoring tools, but they aren’t designed for the job and it’s much harder (and often a lot more expensive).

#### Can I share the information in Highlight with my end-users and/or customers?

- Absolutely! Highlight is designed from the ground up for unlimited levels of tenancy. For example; a service provider can share visibility with a reseller, who in turn shares with a retail customer, who in turn has region managers and even store managers, every level can be shared with just that ‘branch’ of the network visible for that user or group of users. Security is also hugely important in Highlight, we work very hard to ensure data is never exposed to users that shouldn’t see it.

#### I have a big network, can you scale?

- Absolutely, Highlight is designed for the size and scale of large global service providers, so networks with many thousand locations and tens of thousand devices is tried and tested in our platform, coexisting with other customers and networks of similar size. Our scale isn’t infinite (nothing is infinite) but ultimately scale is our problem as Highlight is a cloud service.

#### How do you collect data on the underlay/uplink connections?

- Underlay connectivity services, such as copper or fibre line broadband, dedicated access (or fibre Ethernet in some regions), cellular or satellite, typically have their own router device. If this device supports SNMP, Highlight can collect data and bring the service into our platform.

#### What server resources are required to run Highlight?

- Highlight is a 100% cloud-based service so no hardware/server/data centre resources are required. That said, collecting data from service provider management networks or private LANs can be tricky when properly secured and behind firewalls, so Highlight also has a software agent that can be used to collect data within a secure environment and post the data back to Highlight's cloud platform. The Highlight agent is incredibly efficient, supporting well over 20,000 devices on a single vCPU 2GB server. More info on our agent is available on our Help and Support site .
