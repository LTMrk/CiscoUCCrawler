## Features

### AI powered space scheduling

GoSpace's proprietary spatial AI, AIDRA learns your preferences and schedule and automatically reserves you space.

#### The only 100% automated solution

- Need to join a different team or find a quiet space for the day?   No problem, simply let GoSpace AI know and your space will be update automatically.

- GoSpace AI learns your weekly schedule so there is no need to schedule in advance.  Space will be ready for you with your team when you show up.

- Changing your weekly schedule or visiting a new office location? Simply tell GoSpace AI which building you'll be attending and space will be ready for you when you arrive.

### Get together with a simple nudge

Nudge your colleagues to join you in the office on any day.  If they accept the nudge they are automatically scheduled space with you in the office.  No more wasted time coordinating schedules and trying to find desks together to book.

#### Simple and fast

- Select a day and choose specific colleagues or a whole team to nudge.  The right amount of space will automatically be reserved for all those who accept.

- Track who has accepted or declined your nudge and those that are still pending,

- Rest assured you will not be bombarded with notifications as you can only be nudged once to join any team.   In addition you can elect to turn off nudges all together.

### Efficiently align in-office schedules

GoSpace AI allows you to quickly search and overlay your in-office schedule with others.  Check which days your colleagues are confirmed to be in-office as well as what days they are likely to be in-office based on their historic preference.

#### End unnecessary commutes and time spent coordinating schedules

- Overlay your colleague's schedule and decide which days to join them.

- Check in real-time to see if your colleague is in-office and where they are in the building

- Maintain privacy.  Decide who can view your schedule or opt out of sharing all together.

### Automated capacity management

Real estate administrators know that when it comes to managing space, actionable analytics are far from a solution.  The hardest and most time consuming tasks involve adapting the team space assignments with the least day to day disruption while meeting strategic objectives.  Not to mention updating desk reservation rules and informing impacted employees of the change.    GoSpace AI is the first solution which automates all of this tedious manual effort.   GoSpace AI automatically re-assigns and closes space to ensure that employees are not faced with empty floors on low demand days which not only improves overall office liveliness but alslo reduces operational costs and CO2 emissions.

#### Daily allocation solutions

- Solutions are optimized to ensure the least day to day disruption, eliminating the stress of looking for and finding a new team space.

- GoSpace AI automatically generates and adapts a rolling 4 weeks of daily allocation solutions.

- Administrators can generate scenarios to accommodate planned headcount changes and schedule GoSpace AI to activate on specific days.

#### Go beyond space usage insights and automatically optimize

- GoSpace AI learns from historic space usage to automatically target and turn off access to floors, zones and even desks that are the least used.

- Unused capacity is grouped together for cost savings and/or monetization as flex space.

#### Leverage your existing technology

- GoSpace AI does not require integration with access control or any other building systems to revolutionize the employee hybrid work experience and to generate massive efficiencies for your real estate administrators.

- GoSpace AI is able to maintain its forecasting models and power its real-time allocations using only Meraki WAP data.
