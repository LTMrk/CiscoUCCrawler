### Experience AI that adapts to the room

Leveraging an industry-first agentic AI director, Board Pro G3 dynamically adapts and chooses the best camera view as people speak or move around the room. No more manual preset changes. Just the best view, every time.

### Customize your deployment

Add cameras and microphones to extend coverage, capture more voices, and unlock a more inclusive experience in large spaces with simple, one-click AV over IP peripherals.

### Let AI handle the details

Embedded note-taking agents take on the heavy lifting, so your teams can focus on the meeting at hand. Notes, summaries, and action items—all automatically captured in the background.

### Take the guesswork out of your spaces

Create perfect ideation and meeting rooms from the start with our Workspace Advisor agent. Get a 3D rendering of your space in Control Hub, so admins can quickly see whether every participant in the room can be seen, heard, and included.

### Hear everyone and be heard.

Enjoy rich, immersive sound powered by an advanced stereo loudspeaker system. Spatial sound makes hybrid meetings feel like you’re sitting in the same room, while a built-in, 14-element mic array and intelligent microphone extensions bring unmatched clarity to speakers’ voices.
