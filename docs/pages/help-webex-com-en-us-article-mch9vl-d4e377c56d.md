Slide the Noise Cancellation switch down to NC to turn on noise cancellation.

Slide the Noise Cancellation switch to OFF to turn noise cancellation off.

| 1 | Slide the Noise Cancellation switch down to NC to turn on noise cancellation. |
|---|---|
| 2 | Slide the Noise Cancellation switch to OFF to turn noise cancellation off. |
