Webex for Government supports end-to-end encrypted meetings in Webex App.

Sign in to your Webex site .

Click Preferences > Scheduling .

From the Meeting type drop-down list, select E2E
            Encryption + Identity .

Click Save .

| 1 | Sign in to your Webex site . |
|---|---|
| 2 | Click Preferences > Scheduling . |

            Encryption + Identity . |
| 4 | Click Save . |
