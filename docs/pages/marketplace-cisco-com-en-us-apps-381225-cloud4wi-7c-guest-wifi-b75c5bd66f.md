# Cloud4Wi | Guest WiFi

by Cloud4Wi

Cloud4Wi Guest WiFi solution makes guest access automatic and secure — and turns every connection into first-party data.

## Overview

### Guest WiFi Solution

Guest WiFi should be the bridge between the digital and physical guest journey. Cloud4Wi offers an AI-powered guest WiFi solution that eliminates the friction of traditional networks and turns guest connectivity into a strategic growth engine.

The journey starts with the captive portal — not just a login screen, but a fully branded onboarding experience. New guests register, give consent, and download a Passpoint/OpenRoaming profile directly through it. From that point on, every device reconnects automatically, with zero friction and no login required. The captive portal isn't replaced by Passpoint — it's the gateway to it.

Beyond connectivity, Cloud4Wi closes the "data desert" problem. That first-visit interaction captures high-quality, first-party guest data in full alignment with global privacy laws. Through the MyData Portal, guests stay in control of their information — building trust while you accumulate actionable insights into their on-premise behavior.

By integrating with your martech stack, Cloud4Wi turns real-time data into location-aware experiences. Trigger personalized messages or in-app content the moment a guest enters your venue, delivering relevant engagement at every stage of their visit.

Underpinning everything is Hedy, Cloud4Wi 's AI engine. Hedy detects network issues before guests notice, proposes precise resolutions, and surfaces actionable insights — reducing IT tickets so your team can focus on outcomes, not firefighting.

Trusted by Campari Group, Ferrari, MSC, Prada Group, Starbucks, and leading global companies across 70,000+ locations in 150 countries, Cloud4Wi is the only guest WiFi solution that combines enterprise-grade security, hardware independence, and AI to turn your WiFi into a strategic growth engine.
