# Auvik Network Management

by Auvik

Auvik’s AI-powered IT operations software maps, monitors, and alerts on network issues so you can keep ahead of potential outages and reduce manual lift on network related tasks.

## Overview

### Manage your network with AI-powered IT operations, from Auvik

Why IT teams choose Auvik Auvik, powered by Auvik Aurora AI agents, enables technicians move from alert to action by providing contextual insights and recommended next steps based on data from the actual networks you monitor and manage. Reduce the hidden costs of service delivery - manual documentation, long ticket investigations, unnecessary escalations, and preventable site visits - with Auvik.

- Troubleshoot faster with live maps, alerts, device context, traffic insights, and user experience data.

- Reduce manual work with automated discovery, documentation, monitoring, and configuration backups.

- Standardize service delivery across multi-vendor networks, different device types, and distributed sites.

- Scale more efficiently by helping technicians support more end users with less operational friction.

- Automate network mapping & IT asset management Complete your network picture with automated network discovery, inventory, mapping and documentation. Find every asset connected to your network - Cisco, Cisco Meraki, or any vendor.
