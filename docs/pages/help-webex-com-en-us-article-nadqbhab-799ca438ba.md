Slide the Noise Cancellation switch on the right ear cup up to AMB to turn on ambient mode.

Slide the Noise Cancellation switch to OFF to turn  ambient mode off.

| 1 | Slide the Noise Cancellation switch on the right ear cup up to AMB to turn on ambient mode. |
|---|---|
| 2 | Slide the Noise Cancellation switch to OFF to turn  ambient mode off. |
