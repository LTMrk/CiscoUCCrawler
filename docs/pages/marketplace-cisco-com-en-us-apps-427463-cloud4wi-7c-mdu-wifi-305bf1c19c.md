# Cloud4Wi | MDU WiFi

by Cloud4Wi

Cloud4Wi offers an AI-powered MDU WiFi solution that gives every resident their own private network — automatically, on the infrastructure you already own.

## Overview

### Cloud4Wi | MDU WiFi

In multi-tenant environments, WiFi is as foundational as electricity — and just as invisible when it works. The problem: most property networks weren't built for today's connected residents. Shared SSIDs create security gaps. Manual onboarding at move-in creates IT backlogs. Inconsistent hardware across a growing portfolio makes centralized management nearly impossible. Cloud4Wi was built to solve all three.

At the core of the resident experience is the Personal Area Network (PAN). Instead of dropping every resident onto a shared building network, Cloud4Wi gives each one their own private, isolated network — automatically, from day one. iPSK, easy PSK and Passpoint authenticate devices securely without shared passwords or manual credential distribution. Residents get a home-router-level experience on an enterprise-grade backbone, plus a self-service portal to manage their own devices and grant guest access without ever calling the front desk.

For IT, the operational impact is immediate. Move-in becomes a zero-touch process. iPSK and easy PSK provisioning, rotation, and revocation run automatically, tied to your existing resident identity system. When a resident moves out, access is revoked instantly across every access point on the property — with no manual cleanup.

Cloud4Wi is hardware-independent. Unlike single-vendor solutions, Cloud4Wi normalizes access management across Aruba, Cisco, Extreme, Meraki, Ruckus, and more — so you can standardize the resident WiFi experience across your entire portfolio without a single forklift upgrade.

Underpinning everything is Hedy, Cloud4Wi's AI engine. Hedy detects issues before residents notice them, proposes precise resolutions, and delivers actionable insights — keeping IT focused on outcomes, not tickets.

Cloud4Wi is the only multi-tenant WiFi solution that combines AI-powered operations, automatic secure authentication, and true hardware independence to turn property WiFi from a maintenance burden into a competitive amenity.
