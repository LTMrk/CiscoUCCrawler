## Overview

### Splash Access Overview

SplashAccess Enhancing the student experience through high-performance WIFI . For Students require quick, easy, and secure IPSK wireless private networks (WPN) .

Splash Access’ aim is to simply ensure students get connected and stay connected in the easiest, user friendly way possible. Splash Access manages the deployments of all IPSK KEYS and Wireless Private Networks (WPN) Delivering the best student residential network experience.

SplashAccess - That Home away from experience !!

Splash Access the creation of a custom splash page that can be integrated with the Education Module through the Meraki Portal, allowing providers the ability to control access and allocate personal VLANS in one easy to use system.

The Education solution focuses on the ‘ Student experience ’.

With a simple Captive portal, using their unique SSID credentials, students can login quickly and efficiently into the network using any device required (mobile, desktop etc).

A Device Registration Portal gives each student the ability to login to a guest SSID. Giving them a personal virtual network, providing a simpler and more secure device interaction environment, providing them with a ‘home away from home experience’.

Each student will also be able to self-register their own devices, onboarding and managing devices with no captive portal capabilities, such as PlayStation, Xbox etc using their student credentials via the device registration portal. Students will can even pre-register and onboard devices weeks before arrival to their location.

A Tier 1 management portal gives administrators the ability to control access and allocate student details and VLANS in one easy to use system. Administrators will have the ability to securely manage users and devices via the Tier 1 Management portal. Reporting/Logs enables admin users to track lost or stolen devices.
