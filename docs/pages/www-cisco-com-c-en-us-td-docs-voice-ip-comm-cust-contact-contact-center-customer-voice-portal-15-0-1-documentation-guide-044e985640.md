## Plan

### Solution Design Guide for Cisco Unified Contact Center Enterprise

This document provides a solution-level perspective on designing your contact center enterprise solution for Unified Contact
                     Center Enterprise Reference Designs. This guide combines design information from Unified CCE, Unified CVP, Unified Intelligence
                     Center, and several other products. We created this guide with extensive input from our architects. It represents our best
                     current thought on contact center enterprise designs.

To view the latest Solution Design Guide for Cisco Unified Contact Center Enterprise, see https://www.cisco.com/c/en/us/support/customer-collaboration/unified-contact-center-enterprise/products-implementation-design-guides-list.html .
