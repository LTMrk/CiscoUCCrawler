If a connected device attempts to draw more power than the USB port's maximum output, a warning message will pop up, prompting you to unplug the device. When you see the message, disconnect the device to protect your phone.

## Best practices for device charging

- Only charge devices with a rated input power that doesn't exceed the phone's maximum output power.

- Connect the headset to the phone via the USB HD Adapter.

- Fully charge the headset before connecting it to the phone or the attached KEM with a USB cable.

- Connect your phone to a power socket, as the USB port over PoE provides less power.

- Avoid forcefully inserting plugs into the USB ports and ensure they match correctly.

- Never cover charging devices to prevent overheating.

- Unplug the device if it's already fully charged.
