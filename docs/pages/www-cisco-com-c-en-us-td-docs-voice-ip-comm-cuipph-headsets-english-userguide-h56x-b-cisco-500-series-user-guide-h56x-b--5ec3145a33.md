# Cisco Headset 500 Series Call Controls

## Cisco Headset 520 Series and Cisco Headset 530 Series Call Controls

### Make and Answer Calls

On the inline controller, press Call .

### Adjust Your Volume

Press Up or Down on the inline controller.

### Mute Yourself on a Call

Press Mute on the inline controller.

### Place a Call on Hold

Press Call once on the inline controller.

If you have an incoming call, the headset answers automatically.

### End a Call

Hold Call on the inline controller until you hear a tone.

### Reject a Call

Press Call twice on the inline controller.

### Place a Call on Hold and Resume a Held Call

Press Call on the inline controller.

Select the call you want to resume.

Press Call .

### End a Call and Answer an Incoming Call

Hold Call until you hear a tone.

Press Call once.

### End a Call and Resume a Held Call

Hold Call until you hear a tone.

On your phone, select the call that you want to resume.

Press Call once.

## Cisco Headset 560 Series Call Controls

Cisco Headset 561 and 562 with Standard Base and Cisco Headset 561 and 562 with Multibase use similar call controls to the
                              wired headset models with a few important differences. The call control buttons are located on the headset ear cup and call
                              status feedback is provided on the Standard Base and Multibase.

### Turn Your Headset On and Off

Hold Call on your headset for 4 seconds. You hear a tone indicating headset status.

Do one of these actions.

- Hold Call for 1 second to turn the headset back on.

- Place the headset on the base.

### Make and Answer  Calls

Press Call on your headset.

### Adjust Your Volume

Use Volume on your headset.

### Mute Yourself on a Call

Do one of these actions.

- Press Mute on your headset.

- You can also mute yourself with on the multibase.

### Place a Call on Hold

While on a call, press Call on your headset.

- If you have an incoming call, the headset answers automatically.

### End a Call

Do one of these actions.

- Hold Call on your headset until you hear a tone.

- Place your headset on its base.

### Reject a Call

Press Call on your headset twice.

### Put a Call on Hold and Resume a Call

Press Call on your headset.

Select the call that you want to resume.

Press Call .

### End a Call and Resume a Held Call

Hold Call on your headset until you hear a tone.

Select the call that you want to resume.

Press Call .

### End an Active Call and Answer an Incoming Call

Hold Call until you hear a tone.

Press Call once.

### Answer a Call From a Different Source

Do one of these actions:

- Press Call on your headset.

- On the multibase, press the source button for the incoming call.

## Cisco Headset Multi-app Feature

All Cisco Headset models can retain call control features when there is more than one open calling client on your Windows
                              or Mac desktop. Instead of broadcasting a button press to all open applications, headset controls only impact one calling
                              application at a time.

For example, you can mute an active call in the Webex app and then answer an incoming Cisco Jabber call through the Jabber
                              UI. While you’re on the Jabber call, call controls through the headset only impact Jabber. When the Jabber call is complete,
                              you can resume your Webex call and maintain call control through your headset.

Keep the following in mind when you have multiple calling apps open on your desktop:

Active calls don't automatically hold when you answer an incoming call on a different application. Be sure to hold or mute
                                                a call on one application before you answer an incoming call on a different calling app.

Multi-app control is only available with Webex (version 41.8 or later) and Cisco Jabber (version 14.1 or later) over Bluetooth
                                                or with the USB cable.
