# LogicMonitor | LM Envision

by LogicMonitor

LogicMonitor integrates with Meraki, Catalyst Center, Catalyst SD-WAN, ISE, Splunk, ThousandEyes, and ServiceNow to help you resolve complex issues fast.

## Overview

### LogicMonitor | Hybrid Observability Powered by AI

LogicMonitor differentiates between signals and noise, resulting in actionable insights that lead to better service levels and efficient use of resources, while offering official integrations with Cisco Meraki, Catalyst Center, Catalyst SD-WAN, ISE, Splunk, and ThousandEyes. Our agentic AI powered platform can reduce ServiceNow noise with up to 90-95% efficiency.

### Key benefits

- Agentic AIOps Edwin AI offers a single pane of glass for event intelligence, troubleshooting and automation that correlates thousands of events from multiple sources like LogicMonitor, Splunk, and Datadog to quickly identify the true root cause of alert storms so only what's relevant goes to ServiceNow.  This results in 90% net reduction in incidents.

- Cisco Meraki Integration Enables small and simple to large and complex customers and service providers to monitor their Cisco Meraki environments in the context of their broader hybrid and multi-cloud infrastructures.  LogicMonitor's Meraki integration monitors the most critical elements of Security Appliances (MX), Switches (MS and Catalyst), Access Points (MR and CW), Cellular Gateways (MG), Environmental Sensors (MT), Smart Cameras (MV), and Telegateways (Z).  The integration uses the Cisco Meraki Dashboard API and Cisco Meraki Webhooks and if desired can layer-on additional telemetry via SNMP and Syslog.

- Cisco Catalyst Center Integration Enables small and simple to large and complex customers and service providers to monitor their Cisco Catalyst Center environments in the context of their broader hybrid and multi-cloud infrastructures.  This integration helps customers that are new to Cisco and Catalyst Center (formerly DNA Center) and those who are migrating from Prime Infrastructure.  LogicMonitor leverages the Catalyst Center API to discover the site hierarchy and all of the devices Catalyst Center manages, so you can start monitoring them in minutes.

- Cisco Catalyst SD-WAN integration Enables small and simple to large and complex customers and service providers to monitor their Cisco Catalyst SD-WAN environments in the context of their broader hybrid and multi-cloud infrastructures.  Environments can be all Cisco, new and old technologies, or multi-vendor.

LogicMonitor integrates with the Catalyst SD-WAN Bulk API for SD-WAN specific metrics and alarms and layers on available SNMP, Syslog, and Netflow data to provide access to rich telemetry for capacity planning, troubleshooting, and root cause analysis.

- Cisco ISE Integration Enables small and simple to large and complex customers and service providers to monitor their Cisco ISE environments in the context of their broader hybrid and multi-cloud infrastructures. Monitors server and user sessions, ISE deployment status, licensing, certificates, syslog events, and system performance.

- Cisco ThousandEyes Integration The LogicMonitor Edwin AI ThousandEyes Integration enables Edwin AI to receive alert events from your ThousandEyes environment in real time using custom webhooks. This facilitates automated response, alert enrichment, and cross-platform incident correlation.

- Splunk Cloud Integration The LogicMonitor Edwin AI Splunk Integration enables Edwin AI to receive and normalize event data from your Splunk Cloud environment in real time. This integration transforms incoming Splunk events into the Edwin Common Event Format (CEF) using a configurable YAML-based mapping structure.

The integration supports field extraction, enrichment, normalization, and default fallback logic to ensure clean and consistent event ingestion into Edwin AI.

- Cisco Intersight Integration for UCS X-Series Modular System This integration uses the Cisco Intersight Telemetry API to retrieve metrics and inventory data to provide out-of-the-box monitoring for Cisco UCS X-Series Chassis, Servers, and Fabric Interconnects.
