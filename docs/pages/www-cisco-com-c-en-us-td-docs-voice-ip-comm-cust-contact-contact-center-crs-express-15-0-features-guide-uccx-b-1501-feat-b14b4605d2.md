# Customer Smart Account

A Customer Smart Account provides the repository for the products that are enabled with Smart Licensing. Users must create
                        a Smart Account so that the licenses for each product can be deposited into users accounts. The Smart Account creation is
                        a one-time setup activity. Users can activate, deactivate, monitor, and track the usage of licenses. Customers, Channel partners,
                        or any authorized users can directly manage the licenses.

## Customer Smart Account Request

### Before you begin

Step 1

On the Cisco Smart Workplace page, hover over Administration tab and click Create Smart Account .

Step 2

Select Yes, I will be the Approver for the account .

Approver must have the authority to enter legal agreements, will serve as the primary owner, and nominates account administrators.

Click Edit for Account Domain Identifier .

Enter appropriate Proposed Domain Identifier .

Enter appropriate Contact Phone Number .

Click OK .

Enter appropriate Account Name .

Click Continue .

Step 3

Select No, the person specified below will be the Approver for the Account .

Click Edit for Account Domain Identifier .

Enter appropriate Proposed Domain Identifier .

Click OK .

Enter appropriate Account Name .

Enter Message to Approver .

Click Continue .

Click Complete Smart Account Setup link in the email.

Select Yes, I accept the role of Account Approver .

You can also decline or nominate another person as approver.

Click Continue .

Select the required Account Domain Identifier .

Enter the Account Name .

Click Continue .
