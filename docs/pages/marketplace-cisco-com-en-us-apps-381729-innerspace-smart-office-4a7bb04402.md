# InnerSpace Smart Office

by Innerspace

Wifi based people analytics and occupancy insight platform

## Overview

### Overview

The InnerSpace platform uses WiFi signals from smart devices (maintaining full individual privacy) to capture your indoor reality by tracking movement throughout the space. With always-on data collection, we capture patterns in behaviour over time to tell, but not limited to:

Showcasing different work styles of employees within the same team

When mobile employees of each team come into the office and how often vs. on-site employees of each team and how often

Where certain teams spend most of their time and inform efficient redesign to the space to put it where they need it most

How teams interact or gather and where they don’t go at all

These insights are used by our customers to inform decisions for their operations and to improve employee experience and optimize their real estate footprint. These decisions include:

Determining an accurate sharing ratio to make better decisions about how much office space you need

Addressing return-to-work concerns about crowding, health and safety protocols, BCP, and ever changing government regulations

Change office layout for greater employee productivity

Deliver new digital experiences like desk reservation solutions to enable a hybrid workspace
