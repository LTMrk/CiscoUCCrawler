# Desk Booking | PlaceOS

by PlaceOS

Hot desking can create a few challenges. 

As an employee, I would like to know: Where can I work today? Where can I work in the future? Can I work in proximity to my team? Will I have a monitor? Is there good coffee on the available floor? 

As the employer, I would like to know: how much of my workplace is utilized? Am I providing the right mix of space for my employees to be happy and productive? What teams need more or less space? 

Finding an available and suitable desk without friction is the workplace experience to get right. The integrations from PlaceOS provide real-time context that helps make this process easy and flexible. 

PlaceOS desk booking allows users to search for desks based on requirements, book for a team or group, check in at the desk with a QR Code or enable auto check-in by leveraging PlaceOS integrations.

## Overview

### Flexible & functional

Simplify desk booking by providing real-time availability, flexible configurations, workflow automation, and customizable rules.

### Key benefits

- Booking Users can book available desks for a specific time slot through the platform.

- Group Bookings Users can book multiple desks for a group or team, making it easy to collaborate and work together.

- QR Code Check-In Users can use a QR code provided through the platform to check-in to their desk, making the process quick and easy.

- Auto check-in With this feature, users can automatically check-in to their desk based on their location, eliminating the need for manual check-ins.

- Utilization Reporting: Provides data on desk usage, allowing you to make informed decisions on resource allocation and space planning.

- Real-time Map Interface Displays the current availability of desks, helping users to easily find and book a desk that meets their needs.
