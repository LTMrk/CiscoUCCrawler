# Viana™ People Counting

by meldCX

Analyze and monitor the occupancy of your physical spaces in real time by tracking entries and exits. Gain insights into foot traffic patterns and optimize space utilization.

## Overview

### Automatically capture footfall traffic and occupancy of your spaces

Set capacity for your physical spaces. Track traffic at entrances and different zones to let you know how many people have entered or are present at various zones. Understand footfall traffic, dwell times across different areas of your store including screens. 

Viana collects streams of non-identifiable data from cameras and sensors, blurring faces and turning each individual into a token. This means that individual privacy is protected and any data processed at the edge are anonymous.

### Key benefits

- Count individuals at entrance and exits Capture anonymous visitor metrics such as gender, age category, and dwell time.

- Anonymous Vision Analytics High-performance face mapping extracts predefined human data without keeping any identifiable information. Data is captured and processed in line with various global standards.

- A Comprehensive Data Story Make every sensor a data point. Viana allows you to stack multiple AI models to deliver better insights and less overall cloud consumption.
