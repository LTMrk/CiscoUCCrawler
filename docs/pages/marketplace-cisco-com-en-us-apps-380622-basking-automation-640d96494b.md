### Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Find quick answers about how Basking integrates with Cisco Meraki, ensures data security, delivers actionable insights, and supports seamless setup and ongoing optimization.

#### 1. How does Basking.io transform my Cisco Meraki network into a powerful occupancy analytics tool?

- Basking leverages your existing Cisco Meraki MR Access Points and MV Cameras to deliver real-time, anonymized occupancy analyticswithout the need for additional hardware. By connecting via the Meraki Scanning API v3, Basking collects data like client device counts, dwell times, and peak usage trends, offering actionable insights to optimize office space, reduce operational costs, and enhance the workplace experience.

#### 2. What makes Baskings data collection secure and GDPR-compliant?

- Basking.io is designed with privacy-first architecture, ensuring full GDPR and SOC 2 Type II compliance. Data is encrypted in transit (TLS 1.3) and at rest (AES-256), providing enterprise-grade security. Only essential, anonymized metrics (like device counts and dwell times) are collected, and no personal data (e.g., MAC addresses) is stored or traceable.

#### 3. What are the key business insights Ill gain from integrating Basking.io with Meraki?

- Basking offers portfolio-level analytics to compare data across multiple locations, helping make strategic, enterprise-wide decisions. It provides hybrid work metrics by tracking visit frequency, dwell times, and peak usage hours. AI-driven recommendations suggest ways to optimize space and reduce costs, while real-time heatmaps help identify underutilized or overcrowded areas for smarter space management.

#### 4. How quick and seamless is the integration with Cisco Meraki?

- Baskings integration with Cisco Meraki is fast and frictionless, typically completed in minutes. It involves enabling the Scanning API in the Meraki Dashboard, providing the validator code to Basking, entering the integration URL and secret key for Wi-Fi and Bluetooth data streams, and validating the connection. No additional hardware is needed, and the system is scalable across multiple locations.

#### 5. How does Basking.io provide ongoing value after integration?

- Beyond setup, Basking ensures long-term optimization through automated reports and alerts, customizable dashboards, and 24/7 technical support. Easily expand analytics across new locations as your business grows, and receive tailored insights by location, timeframe, or metric to continuously improve workplace efficiency.
