## Important
                        	 Information

Cisco Virtual
                           		Office (CVO) and Cisco Emergency Responder do not include the capability to
                           		automatically locate remote devices that are not on the customer premises. It
                           		is your responsibility before using an off-premises device to provide your
                           		correct and current location data. If you place an emergency call from an
                           		off-premises device without providing your location, your emergency call may be
                           		delivered to an inappropriate emergency service responder, or with an incorrect
                           		location.
