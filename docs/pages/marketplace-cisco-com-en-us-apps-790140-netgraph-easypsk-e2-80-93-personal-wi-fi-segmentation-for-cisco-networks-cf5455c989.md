# Netgraph EasyPSK – Personal Wi-Fi Segmentation for Cisco Networks

by Netgraph

Netgraph EasyPSK enables a home-like WiFi experience in shared environments by automating the creation and lifecycle management of personal WiFi credentials.
The solution supports both Cisco Meraki and Cisco Catalyst (including WLC 9800) environments, using API-based integration for Meraki Wi-Fi Personal Network (WPN) and RADIUS-based integration for scalable EasyPSK deployments.
This provides consistent user isolation, centralized control, and operational visibility across different Cisco network architectures.

## Overview

### Home-Like WiFi with Personal PSK Access

Netgraph EasyPSK enables a home-like WiFi experience in shared and multi-tenant environments across Cisco wireless platforms. Each user, apartment, room, or tenant receives a personal WiFi password (PSK) that creates an isolated network experience similar to a private home router. Devices connected under one personal PSK cannot see devices connected under another, providing built-in separation without complex network redesign. For Cisco Meraki environments, EasyPSK integrates via the Meraki Dashboard API to automate Wi-Fi Personal Network (WPN). For Cisco Catalyst (including WLC 9800), the solution operates through RADIUS-based EasyPSK, enabling scalable deployment of personal WiFi credentials. Administrators can define lifecycle policies such as validity, grouping, and access rules, while delegated administration and self-service workflows enable distributed management across co-living, student housing, hospitality, and multi-tenant office environments.

### FAQ

Common questions about deployment, user experience, and policy management for Netgraph EasyPSK across Cisco wireless environments.

#### Q: What does “home-like WiFi” mean in this context?

- Each user or unit receives a personal WiFi password that creates a logically isolated network environment, similar to a private home router. Devices connected under one personal PSK cannot access devices connected under another.

#### Q: How does Self-Service work for end users?

- Users can access a Self-Service portal where they can view their assigned WiFi password, rotate or update it, and see which devices are connected under their personal network. They can also grant shared visibility and management rights to trusted individuals such as family members or co-working participants.

#### Q: Can administrators apply different policies to different users?

- Yes. Netgraph EasyPSK supports both individual Group Policy assignment and shared policy models. This allows different network characteristics or access parameters depending on tenant, building, or user role.

#### Q: Does the solution support single-site and multi-site deployments?

- Yes. Netgraph EasyPSK supports management of single Meraki networks, distributed multi-site environments, and template-based Meraki deployments, with centralized policy governance across all locations.

#### Q: Is additional hardware required?

- No. Netgraph EasyPSK operates entirely within Cisco Meraki environments using secure API integration with the Meraki Dashboard.

#### Q: Do I need one access point per apartment or unit?

- No. Netgraph EasyPSK and Meraki Wi-Fi Personal Network provide logical network isolation through personal PSKs. Multiple apartments or rooms can share access point coverage in a hotel-style wireless design while maintaining full separation between users. Per-unit access point deployments are also supported when required by RF or capacity design.

#### Q: How does EasyPSK work with Cisco Catalyst (WLC 9800)?

- EasyPSK integrates with Cisco Catalyst wireless networks (including WLC 9800) using standard RADIUS-based authentication. Each device or user is assigned a unique pre-shared key (PSK), which is validated centrally while access policies are applied dynamically during authentication.

#### Q: Can EasyPSK scale to large deployments on WLC 9800?

- Yes. The solution supports large-scale environments with tens of thousands of personal PSKs on a single SSID, making it suitable for high-density deployments such as multi-tenant housing, hospitality, and enterprise environments.

#### Q: How are policies applied in a Catalyst EasyPSK deployment?

- Policies are applied dynamically via RADIUS during authentication. This enables assignment of VLANs, access rules, or other network characteristics per user, device, or group, without requiring multiple SSIDs.

#### Q: Can EasyPSK be combined with Meraki deployments?

- Yes. Netgraph EasyPSK supports both Cisco Catalyst (WLC 9800) and Cisco Meraki environments, enabling consistent management of personal WiFi credentials across different network architectures.
