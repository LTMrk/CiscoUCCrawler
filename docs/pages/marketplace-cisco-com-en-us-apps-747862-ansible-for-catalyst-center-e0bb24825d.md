# Ansible for Catalyst Center

by Cisco

Catalyst Center Ansible integration automates device provisioning, config, and compliance for wired and wireless networks. Accelerate onboarding, ensure secure and consistent setups, automate image management, and enforce policies, no onsite visits needed.

## Overview

### Ansible for Catalyst Center

Streamline Catalyst Center provisioning with ready-to-use Ansible playbooks. Automate configurations and simplify network management tasks.

The Ansible playbook collection is certified by RedHat and supported by Cisco TAC/Engineering, compatible with Catalyst Center version 2.3.7.6 and later. It leverages REST APIs and integrates with Cisco's ecosystem to provide a robust automation framework for modern network operations.

Download the Ansible playbook from: Ansible playbook

### Key benefits

- Automated Device Provisioning and Configuration The Catalyst Center Ansible playbook enables seamless provisioning of wired and wireless devices by automating site assignments and configuration deployments. This reduces manual effort and accelerates network onboarding, allowing network engineers to provision new devices in minutes without onsite visits. The playbook supports zero-touch provisioning workflows, ensuring consistent and error-free device setup across multiple sites.

- Software Image Management and Compliance Automation With integrated workflows for software image import, tagging, distribution, and activation, the Ansible playbook simplifies lifecycle management of device software images. It automates compliance checks by identifying devices with outdated or non-golden images and facilitates scheduled or on-demand upgrades. This ensures network devices remain secure and up to date, reducing operational risks and manual intervention.

- Policy and Network Settings Automation The playbook supports automation of network policies, templates, and settings across the Cisco Catalyst infrastructure. It translates business intent into consistent network configurations, enabling policy-based segmentation and access control. This automation enhances security posture by enforcing zero-trust principles and streamlines network operations by applying policies uniformly across wired and wireless environments.
