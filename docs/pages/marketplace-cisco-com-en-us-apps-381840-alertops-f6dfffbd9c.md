# AlertOps

by ALERTOPS

AlertOps incident response orchestration platform optimizes On-Call Scheduling, Omni-Channel Notifications, Escalations, and Automation.

## Overview

### Overview

AlertOps Integration with Cisco Meraki integration allows End-To-End automation of Incident Response and ticket lifecycles. Smart notifications provide enhanced visibility across the board. AlertOps helps simplify managing multiple SLA's, managing audit-trails, and keeping all tools in sync across the organization. The platform is a SaaS-based software solution, collaboration and automation hub that enables an organization to dramatically improve the issue notification, escalation, and time to resolution process. As incidents occur that impact business-critical processes and revenue streams, the platform alerts the right people at the right time and with the right data to enable rapid incident resolution.
