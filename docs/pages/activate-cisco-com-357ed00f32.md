# Cisco Software Central

Access everything you need to activate and manage your Cisco Smart Licenses.

## Cisco Software License Tools

Cisco License Central

Cisco's premier end-to-end platform for managing the lifecycle of licenses, devices, subscriptions, and accounts—from fulfillment to renewal.

Smart Software Manager

Cisco's legacy license management tool for license transactions and consumption reporting.

Cisco Download and Upgrade

Cisco software downloads for new software and Cisco upgrades for your current software.

Traditional Licenses

Generate and manage PAK-based and other device licenses, including demo licenses.

EA Workspace

Generate and manage licenses purchased through a Cisco Enterprise Agreement.

Network Plug and Play

Automate device discovery and activation on-prem or from the cloud.
