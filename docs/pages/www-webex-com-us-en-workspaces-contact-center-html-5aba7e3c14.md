### High-definition audio calling

The Desk Phone 9800 Series comes with an enhanced speaker, a new bass-enhancing acoustic enclosure, and AI for noise removal. Adding the Headset 560 helps to further reduce background noise with unidirectional microphones and a boom arm that isolates sound so even in a room filled with commotion, your agents stay focused, and your customers will always get a great experience.

A button made for action

Each Desk Phone 9800 model comes equipped with a customizable action button that can be configured for anything that’s important for your organization. Top use cases include emergency calls, security, access to IT support, broadcasting announcements, and more.

Best-in-class security

Ensure unparalleled end-to-end encrypted security with the Desk Phone 9800 Series which are the first phones in the industry to feature Trusted Platform Module (TPM) 2.0 hardware, plus top-of-the-line features including TLS 1.3, Secure Boot, Image Authentication, and network access control. Ideal for government, finance, service workers, and customer service agents, our phones set the standard for secure communications.

Flexible microphone connectivity

With the Headset 560, you can equip contact center workstations with a wireless DECT 6.0 headset available in mono or stereo ear that includes an option for a standard base with 1 USB connection or a multi base with 2 USB and 1 Bluetooth® connection.
