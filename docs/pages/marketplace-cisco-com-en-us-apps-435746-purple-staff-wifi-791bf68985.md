# Purple Staff WiFi

by Purple

Secure and hassle-free Purple Staff WiFi. Staff log in once with their existing corporate credentials and their devices connect automatically from then on. When they leave the company, access stops. Secure and simple, using Purple's Identity-Based Networks.

Each user receives unique WPA3-Enterprise encryption keys, creating isolated network segments that prevent lateral traffic sniffing even on shared access points. Perfect for organizations with hybrid workforces, multiple locations, or contractor management requirements. 

Purple is the leading global connectivity, with a presence in 89 countries and serving nearly 500 million users. Purple's solutions are trusted by over 80,000 venues worldwide including McDonald's, Harrods, and AGS Airports.

Compatible with Cisco Meraki, Catalyst, and other major Cisco platforms. ISO 27001 certified. Full audit trails. GDPR and CCPA compliant data handling.
