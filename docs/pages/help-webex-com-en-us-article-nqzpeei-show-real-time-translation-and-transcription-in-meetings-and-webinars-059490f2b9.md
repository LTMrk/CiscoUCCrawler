This article is about Webex meetings and webinars. For information on Webex devices, see Closed captioning on Board, Desk, and Room
        Series .

When real-time translation is on, the system automatically translates a person's speech into
      captions that appear above the meeting or webinar controls. The captions panel on the right side shows the transcription for the selected
      spoken language not the translation, (the 16 supported spoken languages are for transcription
      only, not translation).

Real-time translation is a paid add-on license,
          without this license, translation into multiple caption languages is not available.

We support 16 spoken languages for transcription only, in Webex Meetings, Webex Webinars (not available for webinars in webcast view), and meetings started from Webex App. These 16 spoken languages do not include translation capabilities and are available
          without requiring a Real-time Translation license.

The spoken language for transcription can be selected only by the host or automatically
      changed with auto-detection of spoken language . On Webex App, cohosts who join a meeting from a device can select the spoken language for transcription. Each
      and every participant can select their own preferred caption language for translations.

The translation doesn't include all dialects. A meeting or webinar can use a maximum of 15
      unique caption languages at the same time.

For free Webex account users, Webex Assistant or automated closed captions only include 5 spoken languages
      for transcription.

Webex for Government provides the default 5 spoken languages translated to 120+ caption languages.

In a meeting or webinar, select Show closed captions .

Select Caption language and choose the desired language.

Each participant can select their own preferred caption language for translations. The
              translation doesn't include all dialects. A meeting or webinar can use a maximum of 15
              unique caption languages at the same time.

Tap More options > Closed Captions .

Toggle on Closed Captions .

Tap Choose Language and select the language you want to translate speech.

Each participant can select their own preferred caption language for translations. The
              translation doesn't include all dialects. A meeting or webinar can use a maximum of 15
              unique caption languages at the same time.

Exit out of the options to save these settings.
