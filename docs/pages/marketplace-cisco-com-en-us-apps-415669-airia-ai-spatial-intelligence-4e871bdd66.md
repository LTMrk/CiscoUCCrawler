# AIRIA, AI Spatial Intelligence

by AIRIA

AIRIA is a spatial intelligence software solution that gives universities a clear, data-backed view of how facilities are used through real-time occupancy insights. By analyzing campus-wide occupancy patterns, AIRIA identifies underutilized, overburdened, or misaligned spaces. This data visualization enables facilities and energy teams to optimize space, align operations, reduce costs, and support sustainability goals—without additional hardware.

## Overview

### AIRIA, AI Spatial Intelligence

AIRIA enables digital transformation by turning existing network infrastructure into actionable spatial intelligence for large, dynamic facilities. Our AI-powered software platform provides 24/7 occupancy insights to assist with identifying underutilized spaces, automating energy optimization, streamlining operations and delivering measurable cost savings - helping universities maximize financial and sustainability goals, with seamless, data-driven integration and minimal deployment effort.

### Key benefits

- Optimize Space Utilization AIRIA Provides 24/7 occupancy insights to identify underused classrooms, labs, and buildings, helping universities repurpose, consolidate and maximize existing space without costly new construction.

- Advance Sustainability Goals Reduces energy waste and emissions by aligning building operations and planning with dynamic, real-time occupancy levels, supporting ESG targets and carbon reduction.

- Reduce Operating and Energy Costs Aligns HVAC, lighting, and services with actual occupancy, cutting wasted energy, lowering maintenance and utility expenses, and boosting building efficiency.

- Avoid Unnecessary Capital Projects Validates real space needs with campus-wide occupancy data, preventing overbuilding, costly renovations, or unnecessary leased space.

- Improve Planning and Scheduling Supports smarter classroom, lab, and office scheduling by revealing true usage patterns and peak demand, reducing conflicts and enhancing operational efficiency.

## Features

### Campus-wide Occupancy Visualization

AIRIA visualizes 24/7 occupancy patterns across buildings, floors, and rooms, revealing underused and overburdened spaces so teams can consolidate, repurpose, or right-size facilities based on real utilization.

### Data-driven Space and Capital Planning

The platform supports smart capital planning by proving where existing space is sufficient, prioritizing renovations, and helping delay or avoid unnecessary expansions, leases, and deferred-maintenance liabilities.

### HVAC and Energy Optimization

AIRIA aligns HVAC and lighting runtimes with real-time and predictive occupancy, enabling 10–40% energy savings, reduced peak charges, and measurable progress toward carbon-neutrality and sustainability targets.

### Automated Reporting and Analytics

Machine-speed analytics and built-in reports replace manual studies, supplying defensible data for budget decisions, grants and compliance while giving facilities, energy, and finance leaders a shared, trusted source of truth.

### Fast, Interoperable, Privacy-first Deployment

AIRIA is interoperable with existing IT and building systems and designed privacy-first, using no PII and no individual tracking while still delivering actionable insights.
