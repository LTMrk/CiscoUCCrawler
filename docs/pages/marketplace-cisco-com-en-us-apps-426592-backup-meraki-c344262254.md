## Features

### Scheduled & On-Demand Backups

Set it and forget it. Ensure the latest snapshot of your Cisco Meraki Dashboard configuration is always securely stored with our automatic scheduled backups or triggered instantly with on-demand backups. Choose daily, weekly, or monthly schedules, or back up at any moment when changes are made. Our service covers the entire Dashboard organization, providing comprehensive protection for all your critical data.

### Granular Restore

Recover exactly what you need, when you need it. Roll back an individual network or your  organization settings to any snapshot in your backup history with a single click. Perfect for fast recovery from misconfigurations, audits, or disaster recovery scenarios.

### Restore & Backup History

Keep track of every backup and restore operation. Browse and restore from a complete history of encrypted backup snapshots. Whether you’re auditing changes, troubleshooting, or recovering from misconfigurations, you can instantly roll back to any previous point. Restore reports capture exactly which settings were changed during each operation, ensuring full visibility and traceability.

### Data Residency Control

Your data, your choice. With our new Data Residency Control feature, you can choose where your backup data is stored from multiple global regions. This ensures compliance with local data residency requirements, enhances performance by reducing latency, and provides greater control over your data. Available regions include Africa, Asia Pacific, Australia, Canada, Europe, the Middle East, South America, and the United States.
