# isofy

by isofy

isofy provides a simple and cost-effective network management solution tailored for coworking spaces, flex spaces, and multi-tenant office buildings.

## Overview

### Simplified Network Management for Workspaces

isofy simplifies network management for coworking spaces, flex offices, and multi-tenant buildings—delivering powerful automation and seamless Cisco Meraki integration.

Designed for commercial real estate operators, isofy streamlines network setup and administration across single or multiple sites. Our platform empowers you to create secure, private networks for tenants and guests, while dramatically reducing IT overhead and complexity.

Through deep integration with Cisco Meraki, isofy automates VLAN provisioning, bandwidth allocation, and switch port configuration—eliminating the need for manual network changes when new members or companies join your space. With just a few clicks, operators can manage Wi-Fi access, monitor network usage, and ensure every user has the right level of access, all through an intuitive interface.

Key features include:
-Automated network provisioning and Meraki API integration
-Dynamic data port configuration for Meraki switches
-Time-bound Wi-Fi access tied to member bookings
-Real-time usage analytics and reporting
-24/7 customer support and hands-free onboarding

Whether managing one location or many, isofy and Meraki work together to deliver a flexible, secure, and scalable solution—helping you grow your business without network headaches.

Experience network management made simple with isofy + Cisco Meraki.

### Key benefits

- Simplified Network Management Provision users, authorize devices, create private networks, and assign bandwidth tiers in real-time with a simple web-based dashboard, deeply integrated with your Meraki stack.

- Automated Private VLAN Provisioning Seamlessly provision dedicated, secure VLANs per workspace company when members or guests onboard through isofy. Reduces manual configuration and ensures network separation and security by default.

- Smart Bandwidth Tier Allocation Assign bandwidth tiers to user networks based on membership plan or daypass access. Upsell bandwidth as a service and apply unique bandwidth tiers to private networks in just a few clicks.

- No-Code Automations via 5,000+ App Integrations Link Meraki network provisioning with platforms like OfficeRnD, Nexudus, Zapier, Mailchimp, Salesforce, and more—without writing code. Ideal for workspace operators who need workflow automation at scale.

- Live Network Usage & Reporting Dashboard Get granular insights on client session counts, data consumption, and device types connected to your Meraki infrastructure directly within the isofy dashboard. Exports available for deeper analysis.

- Dynamic Port-Based Configuration for Switches For Meraki MS switches, automatically enable dynamic “data port” provisioning tied to user and workspace changes, eliminating manual switch port assignments. Connected devices are automatically placed into their private VLAN.

- Full Meraki API Integration & Device Management Securely connect your Meraki organization via API to isofy during site setup. Once linked, you can view equipment inventories, provision networks, and manage switches and access points—all inside a unified web-based application.

- Branded Wi-Fi Captive Portal Present a white-labeled captive portal on your connected Meraki access points, providing a wireless experienced tailored to your brand logos and colors.
