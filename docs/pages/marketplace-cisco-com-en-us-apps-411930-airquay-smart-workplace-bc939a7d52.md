# AirQuay Smart Workplace

by AIRQUAY

AirQuay Smart Workplace is supporting your work efficiency improvement by checking users' presence via API-based location tracking. 
Major features include information utilization for workplace environment optimization and useful ideas creation for office remodeling. It enables you to check employee's location by floor/region when the collaboration among employees is required. In addition, this solution can also help minimize the spread of contagious disease.

## Overview

### Improve your work efficiency with user presence information!

AirQuay Smart Workplace solution improves your work efficiency by checking users’ presence via API-based location tracking. 
The major features include 
1) Information utilization : It provides information necessary for workplace environment optimization and creates useful ideas for office remodeling.
2) Anti-infection : Applicable for helping minimize the spread of contagious disease 
3) Collaboration : It enables you to check employee location by floor/region when the collaboration among employees is required.
