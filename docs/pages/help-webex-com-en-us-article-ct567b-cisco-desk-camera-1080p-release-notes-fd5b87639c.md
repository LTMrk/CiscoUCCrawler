## July 10, 2025

Version: 1.1.0101.37

Secured Cisco Desk Camera 1080p

If you are a Cisco Desk Camera 1080p user working with Microsoft Windows 10 or Windows 11, your access to the camera can now
        be secured. After you connect a secured camera to your computer for the first time, this
        camera is permanently assigned to this computer and works with this computer
        exclusively.

To work with this feature, you need to install the Cisco Accessory Hub desktop app (version 4.0 or later) as a system service first. Contact your IT administrator for more
        information.

## July 9, 2025

Version: 1.1.0101.37

Fixed issues

- Fixed the audio clipping issue by adding new mechanisms.

- Fixed the issue that Cisco Desk Camera 1080p with auto-focus frequently attempts to focus.

- Fixed the issue that you cannot downgrade a secured Cisco Desk Camera 1080p to an eralier version with the secure camera feature enabled.

- Fixed the issue that you cannot factory reset a secured Cisco Desk Camera 1080p.
