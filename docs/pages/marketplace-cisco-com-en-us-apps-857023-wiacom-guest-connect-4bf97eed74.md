# Wiacom | Guest Connect

by Wiacom

Wiacom Guest Connect provides branded, self-service WiFi registration through URL or QR code, before arrival or on-site. Capture consented first-party data and automatically deliver secure access through iPSK, Passpoint profiles, or other configured methods, with device limits, bandwidth and expiration policies based on location defaults or data from connected business systems such as PMS, CRM, booking platforms, APIs, or other external sources, plus real-time guest engagement and analytics across registrations, visits, returning users, locations, and access activity.

## Overview

### Guest WiFi Registration, Anywhere, iPSK and Passppoint profiles

Give guests a permanent, branded way to register for WiFi before arrival or while on-site through a shareable URL or QR code. Guest Connect separates onboarding from access, allowing validated users to receive WiFi Pass, Key Access, Passpoint Connect or another configured access method.

### Key benefits

- Register Guests Beyond the Captive Portal Capture registrations before guests connect to WiFi, before they arrive, or through QR codes and links placed anywhere in the customer journey.

- Simplify Guest WiFi Access Give users one clear registration journey and automatically provide the WiFi access method configured for that location.

- Build First-Party Guest Relationships Turn registration into a measurable customer touchpoint with consent, registration history, returning-guest information, and integration with external business systems.

- Support Pre-Arrival Onboarding Include Guest Connect links in pre-arrival communications, booking journeys, event communications, websites, or other customer touchpoints.

- Connect Guest Data With Existing Systems Push registration, revisit, and status events to PMS, CRM, CDP, or other platforms through APIs, webhooks, and supported native integrations.

- MSP Multi-Tenant Management Centralize Guest Connect deployments for MSPs, distributors, resellers, customers, and large distributed organizations.

- Cisco Meraki API Integration Connect Wiacom Guest Guest Connect with Cisco Meraki infrastructure to add advanced onboarding, identity, engagement, analytics, and customer intelligence while retaining the existing wireless environment.

- User Troubleshooting & Support Tools Give administrators visibility into user registration, authentication, access, and session activity to help identify and resolve WiFi issues without relying only on network-side troubleshooting.

## Features

### URL & QR Guest Registration

Provide a permanent Guest Connect registration page that guests can access through a shareable URL or QR code. Registration can happen before arrival, while on-site, or from any customer communication channel without requiring the user to already be connected to the guest WiFi network.

#### Shareable Registration URL

- Provide a permanent registration link that can be used across websites, emails, messaging, booking journeys, or other digital channels.

#### QR Code Onboarding

- Allow guests to open the registration journey directly by scanning a QR code from signage, printed material, or other venue touchpoints.

#### Pre-Arrival Registration

- Allow guests to register before reaching the venue and prepare their WiFi access in advance.

#### Independent From Captive Portal

- Guest Connect operates as a standalone onboarding channel and does not depend on the captive portal connection flow.

### Flexible WiFi Access Delivery

Separate guest onboarding from WiFi access. After registration, Guest Connect can trigger the access method configured for the location, including WiFi Pass, Key Access, Passpoint Connect or other supported Wiacom access workflows.

#### WiFi Pass

- Deliver a unique personal WiFi passphrase and connection QR code using supported iPSK, MPSK, DPSK, or equivalent technologies.

#### Key Access

- Deliver shared secure SSID credentials and connection information to validated users.

#### Passpoint Connect

- Use Guest Connect as an onboarding channel for profile-based Passpoint connectivity on supported deployments.

#### Independent Onboarding and Access

- Choose the registration channel and WiFi access method independently for each location.

### Branded Guest Registration

Provide a branded Guest Connect journey that gives venues a permanent customer-facing WiFi registration touchpoint outside the traditional captive portal flow. Guest Connect can complement pre-arrival communications, venue signage, websites, and other customer journeys.

#### Branded Registration Experience

- Present Guest Connect using the venue or customer's branding and customer-facing identity.

#### Permanent Registration Page

- Provide a reusable customer-facing WiFi registration destination rather than relying only on a portal triggered during network connection.

#### Multi-Channel Onboarding

- Use Guest Connect alongside captive portal, PMS, API, staff-assisted, application-based, or other supported onboarding channels.
