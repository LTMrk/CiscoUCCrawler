## Entering the Era of Hybrid Work

### Understanding How the Workplace Must Evolve

The era of hybrid work is here with 68% of knowledge workers already working from both the home and the office. But what do hybrid teams need to be successful? New global research uncovers the challenges and benefits of hybrid work, as well as the technology and experiences employees desire to improve collaboration and productivity at home and in the office.

Gain practical, data-backed insights into:

- The challenges and benefits of hybrid work, according to employees.

- Concerns employees have about returning to the office and how to solve them.

- The top work policies and technology employees want to help reduce burnout and fatigue.

- How to create a thriving hybrid work culture.
