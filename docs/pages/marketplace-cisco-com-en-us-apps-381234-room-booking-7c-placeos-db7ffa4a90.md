# Room Booking | PlaceOS

by PlaceOS

With intelligent workflows, our booking feature automates scheduling, room selection, and confirmation notifications. Our intuitive interface makes it easy for users to quickly find and reserve the perfect meeting space for their needs. Users can also customize their booking preferences and set up recurring meetings with just a few clicks. Our meeting room booking feature works harmoniously with our suite of productivity tools to create a seamless and efficient workplace experience for all users.

## Overview

### Exceptional booking experience

Easily book and manage meeting rooms and spaces, as well as access real-time availability and resource information.

### Key benefits

- Booking Allows users to reserve meeting rooms or workspaces in advance.

- Room Display Displays real-time availability and booking information outside the room.

- Check-In Quick check-in: Allows users to quickly check in to their reserved room or workspace. Auto check-in: Automatically checks in users when they arrive at the room, using Cisco Meraki integrations.

- Configuration Allows administrators to set rules and configurations for room booking, such as which rooms are available to which groups or departments, and what types of meetings can be held in which rooms.
