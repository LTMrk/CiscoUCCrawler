### FAQ

Frequently asked questions for Smart Track.

#### Who is Smart Track for?

- Smart Track is designed for Enterprises and Channel Partners who have signed a Cisco Enterprise Agreement (EA).

#### How does Smart Track account for existing license credits when setting up a new Enterprise Agreement?

- When transitioning to an Enterprise Agreement, many customers already have Meraki licenses with varying expiration dates across their environments. Smart Track factors in these existing "credits" by calculating the remaining value on those unexpired license terms. It then applies that credited amount as a discount to the new EA's total cost. Furthermore, Smart Track shows the credited license values at a granular level - detailing which specific locations and networks those credits are originating from. This ensures the EA cost is properly adjusted, while also enabling accurate chargebacks by allocating each group's credited amount appropriately.

#### What level of granularity does Smart Track provide for tracking license consumption?

- Smart Track provides visibility down to the organization, network, and even individual device level. This allows customers like franchised businesses to allocate and track license consumption accurately across different locations, brands, franchisees, etc. under the same Enterprise Agreement.

#### We need granular cost breakdowns for budgeting and internal chargebacks across our distributed offices/branches. How does Smart Track help?

- Smart Track provides multi-dimensional cost allocation reporting tailored to your organization's needs. It breaks down the total EA cost based on the licenses consumed by each location, department, cost center, etc. For example, Smart Track can generate a cost breakdown that shows the EA expenditure for your San Francisco office separately from your Chicago branch. Or it can allocate costs by business unit, franchise, etc. This aligns with how you need to manage budgets and perform chargebacks internally. The reports can be generated globally for overall budgeting, or filtered down to the individual location or department level for direct cost transparency and accountability. Smart Track becomes your central system of record for EA cost management.
