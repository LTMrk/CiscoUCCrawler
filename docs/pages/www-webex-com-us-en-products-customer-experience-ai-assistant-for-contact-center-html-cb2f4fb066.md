# Elevate the agent and supervisor experience.

The AI Assistant automates tasks across your contact center so your customer-facing teams can focus on delivering exceptional experiences.

### AI Agent Transfer Summaries

Provide accurate, automated summaries from AI Agents and IVR transfers so customers don’t have to repeat themselves.

### Dropped Call Summaries

Ensure continuity when communication is interrupted by capturing interaction details to seamlessly resume a conversation with all the previous context.

### Topic Analytics

Uses historical data to identify common reasons for customer contact and organize conversations between agents and callers by topic—making it easy to track trends and gain customer insights.

### Agent Wellbeing

Automatically identify signs of burnout and take action by re-routing calls to other agents, switching agents to a less demanding channel, or adding a break to their schedule.

### Automatic CSAT

Get insights on customer satisfaction for every voice interaction with automatically generated customer satisfaction (CSAT) scores through survey data, operational metrics, and call recordings.

### Real-time Assist

Improve agent efficiency and reduce onboarding time by providing agents with instant AI-suggested responses in the chat window that are contextual and tailored to the customer’s query.

### Real-time Transcription

Provide instant, accurate, and continuous transcription of conversations during live calls.
