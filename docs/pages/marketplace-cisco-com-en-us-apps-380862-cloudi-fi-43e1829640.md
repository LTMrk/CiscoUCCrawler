# Cloudi-Fi

by Cloudi-Fi

Cloudi-Fi is a global cloud-based platform offering a solution for enterprises to secure their guests, BYOD and IoT devices accessing the Internet.
It offers worldwide compliance with local regulations, personalized user journey features and enhanced internet security.

## Overview

### Customizable, secure, and compliant cloud-based Wi-Fi captive portal for guests and BYOD

Cloudi-Fi is a 100% cloud-based Captive Portal solution that manages Internet access for guest Wi-Fi users at corporate offices, public hotspots, retail locations, or various transportation means. The solution addresses key challenges unique to Wi-Fi networks, such as compliance with all existing local regulations, data privacy, users’ identification and authentication, and personalized digital services.

### Identify and secure your users for a personalized experience

- • Cloud-based solution combining guests' identification and authentication for secure guest onboarding.

- • Multiple authentication modes for a seamless experience: employee sponsorship, SMS, social networks (15+, including WeChat), QR code, and corporate directories: ADFS, Microsoft Azure, Google, Okta,...

- •  Ability to connect to the existing security solution to create specific security policies.

- • User profiling for personalized user's onboarding based on dedicated Wi-Fi experience and usage.

- • Automatic user's recognition for future visits and targeted marketing and analytics

### Onboard your IoT efficiently and securely

- • IoT's categorization: IoT access is managed with an innovative cloud-based DHCP service. This Cloudi-Fi feature provides discovery, identification, and segmentation of all IoT devices.

- • IoT security: Identified IoTs are classified and least privileges access security rules are applied for a Zero-Trust access strategy.

### Ensure compliance

- • Compliance and Internet Providers laws: One single platform to manage global compliance and comply with Internet Providers laws requiring logs to be stored for the length of time requested by governments and made available on demand.

- • Data privacy friendly with various rules worldwide (GDPR, CCPA, PIPA, Cybersecurity law, etc ...).

- • User data privacy: Encryption of users' identifiers with Individual opt-in/ opt-out.

### Deploy a plug-and-play solution in all your locations

- • Rapid & frictionless deployment using existing infrastructure : Cloudi-Fi is a SaaS solution that is deployed instantly, remotely, across the world regardless of any existing infrastructure.

- • Multiple SSID support: This is ideal for corporations or retailers looking for a single solution for their guests while differentiating by subsidiary or brand with a differentiated captive portal for each SSID.

- • 100% Cloud solution for environments with distributed local internet breakouts.

### Key Features

- • One platform for Guests, IoT and BYOD onboarding and management

- • Compliance with local regulations, Internet Provider's laws, and user Data privacy

- • Cloud-based security for unmanaged devices

- • Automated deployment on any infrastructure

- • Personalized and customizable captive portals for guests

- • One solution for all types of verticals

### FAQs

Please find below our FAQs. For any other questions, do not hesitate to contact our teams sales@cloudi-fi.com

#### What is the pricing model?

- We have a license base model. We charge per site and per number of users on a pay-per-use model.

#### Is it direct or indirect sales?

- We sell our solutions through our partners. We have a partner program to onboard and assist our customers in their cloud transformation.

#### Is there any hardware or infrastructure to deploy?

- Cloudi-Fi is 100% cloud based and infrastructure agnostic.

#### How does Cloudi-Fi integrate with Cisco Meraki ?

- Cloudi-Fi integrates with Cisco Meraki MR and MX with the Cisco Meraki Dashboard API. The configuration is fully automated through a wizard.
Please visit our Knowledge Base (link in Overview) or the Cisco Meraki page in the Ecosystem/Technology Partners section of our website

#### Is it compliant with the law ?

- We comply with local regulations, data privacy regulations as well as internet Service providers' regulations (local log storage).
Check our compliance map and report on our Technology/Security & compliance webpage.
