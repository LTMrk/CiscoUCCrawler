# Setyl — IT Asset and License Management Platform (ITAM)

by Setyl

Get the who, what, why, where, when of your IT assets and software licenses — with the Setyl IT Asset and License Management Platform.

## Overview

### Get the who, what, why, where, when of your IT assets and software licenses — with the Setyl IT Asset & License Management Platform.

Setyl is a cloud-based IT asset and license management (ITAM) software, which integrates with your existing tech stack. 

The platform gives you full visibility and control over your IT assets, licenses, software applications, users, vendors and spend in one centralized register. 

Use Setyl to: 
Manage all your assets, licenses, usage and spend in one place. 
Automate and scale daily IT operations, including employee onboarding and offboarding. 
Cut wasted IT spend by up to 30%. 
Be audit-ready and stay compliant, including with SOC 2 and ISO 27001 standards.

### Key benefits

- Add context to your Meraki assets Connect Cisco Meraki to Setyl to automatically import discovered assets. You can then add valuable lifecycle details to each asset in Setyl — such as purchase price and dates, warranty details, location, and disposal information — and receive notifications when support is coming to an end (LDOS), along with other key insights.

- Gain full visibility over your IT in one place Create a source of truth for your organization by tracking and managing all your IT in one unified, centralized platform, including through out-of-the-box integrations with Meraki and your other systems.

- Automate and scale your IT operations Alleviate the complexity of running IT in your organization with comprehensive inventory and asset lifecycle management, structured employee onboarding and offboarding processes, actionable insights, and increased automations.

- Cut wasted IT spend Up to 30% of IT spend is wasted on unnecessary equipment and subscriptions. Use our license and renewal management workflows, asset recovery services, shadow IT detection features, and spend reporting to make quantifiable savings and optimize your budget.

- Be audit-ready and stay compliant Navigate your SOC 2, ISO 27001, Cyber Essentials, and other audits with ease, and minimize compliance risks with our dedicated features. Build a comprehensive information asset register, conduct robust vendor due diligence, identify shadow IT, keep a log of activities and change history, and more.

- Collaborate with stakeholders across your organization Setyl’s clear interface and intuitive design make it easy to use for everyone in your organization, while dedicated roles and permissions allow you to delegate tasks across departments, without losing control.

- Get up and running quickly Native integrations with your existing tech stack combined with intuitive platform functionality ensure a speedy and straightforward implementation of the Setyl platform.

- Hardware asset management Gain complete visibility and control over your organization’s hardware assets, devices and equipment. 

Features include: hardware asset inventory, asset lifecycle management, warranty tracker, device auto-detection, asset management workflows, asset labels and recovery, and more.

- Software asset management Map all your SaaS subscriptions, software licenses, and service agreements, including costs and renewals. 

Features include: software asset inventory, subscription renewal management, license rightsizing, shadow IT detection, vendor audits and due diligence, admin management, and more.

- User management Effectively manage the allocation of IT assets, licenses, and admin roles to employees from onboarding to offboarding.

Features include: HR tool integrations, onboarding and offboarding workflows, requests and approvals, IT cost per employee reports, employee asset surveys, roles and permissions, and more.

- IT spend management Get a full understanding of what is driving IT spend, and identify opportunities for cost savings.

Features include: IT spend tracking, financial reports, license renewals and reminders, license rightsizing, asset tracking and recovery, and more.

- Compliance management and IT safeguarding Mitigate security and compliance risks, and breeze through your ISO 27001, SOC 2 and more audits.

Features include: comprehensive information asset register, effective asset tracking and recovery, shadow IT detection, vendor audits and management, access controls, activity logs and change history, and more.

- Out-of-the-box integrations Connect Setyl to your existing tech stack to gain complete visibility over your IT in one place, automate your workflows, and speed up implementation.
