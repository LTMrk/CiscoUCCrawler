Unified Contact Center Express

Cisco Unified Contact Center Express is a highly secure, sophisticated, and easy to deploy customer interaction management solution for up to 400 agents. Customers can integrate UCCX into existing applications, build reporting applications, or even customize the customer experience with scripting.
