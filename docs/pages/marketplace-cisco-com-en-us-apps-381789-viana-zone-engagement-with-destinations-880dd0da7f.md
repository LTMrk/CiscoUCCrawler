## Overview

### Make every sensor a data point

Viana Zone Engagement with Destinations uses anonymous vision analytics to measure human activity as they move within your spaces. Divide your spaces into specific zones and see how people move and engage, and capture visitor journeys to better understand behaviours of different audience personas.

### Key benefits

- Monitor Visitor Movement Across Your Spaces Analyze how visitors move between different zones of your spaces, capturing their interactions and behaviours to better understand engagement and optimize space usage.

- Anonymous Vision Analytics High-performance face mapping extracts predefined human data without keeping any identifiable information. Data is captured and processed in line with various global standards.

- A Comprehensive Data Story Make every sensor a data point. Viana allows you to stack multiple AI models to deliver better insights and less overall cloud consumption.
