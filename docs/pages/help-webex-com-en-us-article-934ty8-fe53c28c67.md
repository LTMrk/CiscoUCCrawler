## Closed caption: Real-time translation (RTT) and
        Transcription (ASR) in meetings and webinars

Real-time translation and
        transcription allow a more inclusive and engaging meeting and webinars. The host can select
        from multiple spoken languages for a meeting or webinar, which you can later translate into
        over 120+ other languages in closed captions. See Show or hide
          automated closed captions during a Webex meeting or webinar for details.

We
        support 16 spoken languages for Webex Meetings, Webex Webinars (not available for webinars
        in webcast view), and meetings started from Webex App, without requiring a Real-time
        Translation license. However, if you want access to the 120+ caption languages, you'll need
        to have a Real-time translation license .

For free Webex
        account users, only 5 spoken languages are available for transcription.
