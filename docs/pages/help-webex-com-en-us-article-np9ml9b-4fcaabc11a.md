If you’re working in a shared workspace that has a Cisco phone with the hot desking feature enabled, you can sign in and book the phone to use it as your personal device.

Once booked, the phone automatically syncs your contacts, Webex calendar, and call history,
        making it easy to access and manage your communications. You can also connect the Webex
        desktop app to the phone to place or answer calls, and control features such as hold/resume,
        transfer, and conference directly from the app.

If the workplace phone is configured as a hot-desk-only device, it operates in the host mode by default and can only be used to make emergency calls. To access all features, sign in and book the phone.

## Sign in and book a shared phone

The Top 360 LED and Front Arc LED on the shared phone indicate the availability status.
        When the phone is available, the LEDs light in solid green. After you sign in and book the
        device, the LEDs will light in solid white.
        The LEDs in solid amber
        indicate that the phone has been reserved. Only the person who reserved it can sign
        in.

After signing in, you’ll be prompted to choose the end time for your booking. By default, 5 p.m is selected, but you can select a different end time during the same day. Make sure to press Book to confirm your booking. Otherwise, the booking screen times out in 2 minutes, then the phone signs you out and goes back to the QR code screen.

Once the booking duration expires, the system signs you out automatically. If needed, you can also end your booking by signing out from the phone or from your Webex App. This terminates the booking and free up the resource for other users.

On a booked device, you can't upgrade the software or do a factory reset.

## Sign out of the booked phone

If you need to end your booking earlier, simply sign out of the phone to make the workspace available for other users. Otherwise, the system will automatically sign you out when your booking duration expires. If you happen to be on a call when the booking expires, the phone will keep the call connected and sign you out after the call ends.

For privacy reasons, your personal data will be removed from the device once you sign out.

Active calls prevent automatic sign-out even after your booking duration expires. Make sure that you end all calls on the phone before you leave the desk, as this prevents you from being able to sign out remotely or from the phone.

- Phone

- Webex App

Before you begin

End all calls on the phone before you sign out.

Follow the way that applies to your phone settings:

On the phone screen header, tap > Sign out .

Press Sign out .

You can sign yourself out of a booked device using Webex App either on your mobile phone or on your computer. This option is helpful when you leave the workspace forgetting to sign out.

Before you begin

End all calls on the phone before you sign out.

On your Webex App, choose the Connect to device icon .

Locate your booked phone in the Booked devices list and choose Sign out .

Confirm the sign-out in the pop-up window.
