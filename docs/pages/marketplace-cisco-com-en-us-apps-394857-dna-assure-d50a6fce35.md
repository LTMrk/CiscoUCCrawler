# DNA Assure

by DevNetApps.io

Providing a service assurance wrapper around the Cisco Meraki platform by reducing downtime and the restoration process from days/weeks to minutes.

## Overview

### Solution Overview

Governed by cloud service assurance best practice that complements the Cisco Meraki platform, we offer a Cloud BC/DR managed service or SaaS product-only to achieve the best possible recovery time objective for your business.

Operational business challenges solved with DNA Assure:

- Continuous protection of critical operational data – with 24/7 Disaster Recovery Support

- Built-in preventative service outage intelligence – mitigating risk against future downtime

- Design authority governance – ongoing configuration compliance checking

In a scenario of data loss on the Cisco Meraki platform that causes a service outage incident, rebuilding deleted devices and networks requires significant manual intervention, product expertise, and the availability of up-to-date documentation.

If we then also consider the ramifications of such activity where thousands of networks and devices are affected, along with the increased likelihood of incorrectly configuring or omitting key parameters this can all further extend the period of impact and prolongs business downtime.

Innovative Business Solution

Our solution complements the Cisco Meraki Cloud platform and provides customers with peace of mind. Our DNA Assure managed service focuses on:

- Increasing uptime and reducing business restoration downtime – from days and weeks to hours and minutes

- Never missing an operational change – by securely capturing them as and when they happen, in real-time

- Proving the restoration process with your IT team – by providing ongoing biannual testing and tabletop exercising

- Mitigating future outages – through ongoing real-time service intelligence, to provide additional risk removal

- Increasing control level – for potentially better cyber insurance coverage and help with minimizing insurance costs

- Providing 24/7 access – Disaster Recovery expertise available around the clock
