# Webex App | Spaces and Teams
![list-menu](https://help.webex.com/images/feedback.svg)Feedback?
In Webex App, all your work takes place within spaces and teams. Here you can send messages, make calls, have video meetings, and share files and whiteboards. 
## Direct messages
![](https://cisco-api.ingeniuxondemand.com/DITA/content/en/us/td/i/400001-500000/410001-420000/417001-418000/417481.jpeg)
You [ send a message ](https://help.webex.com/en-us/zi4dpc/) directly to one person. The conversation that you started remains between the two of you, and you can't add other people to the conversation later.
## Spaces
![](https://cisco-api.ingeniuxondemand.com/DITA/content/en/us/td/i/400001-500000/410001-420000/417001-418000/417482.jpeg)
[Spaces](https://help.webex.com/en-us/hk71r4/) work well for a group of people working on a specific topic. You come together to handle this _thing_ (which can be something quick or long-term), but the focus is more narrow and specific. You could also hear these referred to as standalone spaces.
You can [join a space](https://help.webex.com/en-us/fyufh4/) when someone who's already in the space adds you to it.
Spaces start out unmoderated, which means anyone can do things like [add](https://help.webex.com/en-us/n35gqwcb/) or [remove](https://help.webex.com/en-us/n161k3p/) people, or [change the name and picture](https://help.webex.com/en-us/ktsmt5/). But if you need more control over who's joining, you can [make yourself a moderator of a space](https://help.webex.com/en-us/gw1w6c/). Then, only you can add people to the space, and you get other privileges like being able to [delete people's messages or files](https://help.webex.com/en-us/article/rt2wbp/).
Non team members/guests can't moderate a space.
## Space sections
Organize and manage all your spaces into sections that you create. For example, easily drag and drop spaces to sections, to keep specific project work spaces together. Expand and collapse sections to give your space list a clean look. See [Webex App | Space sections](https://help.webex.com/en-us/uaayuo/) if you need help with organizing your spaces.
## Public spaces
[Public spaces](https://help.webex.com/en-us/article/r78evy/) are spaces created within your organization which are easily discoverable. Discover, join or create public spaces and connect with people with similar interests, or keep up with the latest news. [Public spaces](https://help.webex.com/en-us/article/r78evy/) are different from other spaces, as they aren't tied to a specific team. Anyone in your organization can find a public space that you create. If you change your mind, you can make a public space private again at any time.
## Space actions
Hover over a space, and right click or click More ![More Options button](https://cisco-api.ingeniuxondemand.com/DITA/content/en/us/td/i/400001-500000/450001-460000/450001-451000/450687.jpg) to access more actions for the space.
  * **Peek** —See messages in the space without a read receipt.
  * **Add to favorites** —Add the space to the favorites section, or in your filtered list of favorite spaces.
  * **Add people** —Add others to the space.
  * **Open in new window** —Open the space in a separate window in the app.
  * **Notifications** —Brings you to the notification settings in the app.
  * **Copy space link** —Copies the link to that space.
  * **Edit space information** —If you have permission, you can edit the space information, including the name of the space, and a cover image.
  * **Move to section** —Move the space to any space section that you've created.
  * **Create section** —Creates a section with that space in the section.
  * **Hide** —Hides the space from your list of spaces.
  * **Leave** —Leave that space, and no longer see messages in the space.


## Teams
![](https://cisco-api.ingeniuxondemand.com/DITA/content/en/us/td/i/400001-500000/410001-420000/417001-418000/417480.jpeg)
If your original intention changes and that project outgrows its original purpose, you can [ create a team ](https://help.webex.com/en-us/n3kbxbf/) and then [ move a space into a team ](https://help.webex.com/en-us/nfzbl3p/).
Teams help you keep everything organized by putting multiple spaces under a common theme. Teams are great for a consistent group of people who are going to be working on _something_ for a long time. That _something_ can involve many activities, or could be subprojects happening at the same time.
To [ join a team ](https://help.webex.com/en-us/fr4sm5/), a moderator must add you to it.
You can also [ connect the team with a Microsoft 365 group ](https://help.webex.com/en-US/article/fyyrxq/Webex-%7C-Connect-a-Microsoft-365-Group-to-a-Team) when you're creating the team. You can then manage people in the team from Microsoft 365 and use Microsoft Sharepoint folders to save your files.
Every team has at least one team space,  General . You're automatically added to the  General  space when you're added to the team. As a team member, you can view and [ join any unmoderated team space ](https://help.webex.com/en-us/l0jhr4/) within the team. You can also [ create team spaces ](https://help.webex.com/en-us/nha7emp/) for others to join.
