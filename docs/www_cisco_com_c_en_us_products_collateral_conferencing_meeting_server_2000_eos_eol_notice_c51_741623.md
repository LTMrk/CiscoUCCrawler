EOL12677 - Amended
Cisco announces the end-of-sale and end-of-life dates for the Cisco Meeting Server 2000. The last day to order the affected product(s) is February 18, 2019. Customers with active service contracts will continue to receive support from the Cisco Technical Assistance Center (TAC) as shown in Table 1 of the EoL bulletin. Table 1 describes the end-of-life milestones, definitions, and dates for the affected product(s). Table 2 lists the product part numbers affected by this announcement. For customers with active and paid service and support contracts, support will be available under the terms and conditions of customers' service contract.
**Table 1.** End-of-Life Milestones and Dates for the Cisco Meeting Server 2000  
|  Milestone  |  Definition  |  Date   |  
| --- | --- | --- |

|  End-of-Sale Date:  
HW   |  The last date to order the product through Cisco point-of-sale mechanisms. The product is no longer for sale after this date.  |  February 18, 2019  |  
|  Last Ship Date:  
HW   |  The last-possible ship date that can be requested of Cisco and/or its contract manufacturers. Actual ship date is dependent on lead time.  |  May 19, 2019  |  
|  End of SW Maintenance Releases Date:  
HW   |  The last date that Cisco Engineering may release any final software maintenance releases or bug fixes. After this date, Cisco Engineering will no longer develop, repair, maintain, or test the product software.  |  February 18, 2020  |  
|  End of Vulnerability/Security Support:  
HW   |  The last date that Cisco Engineering may release a planned maintenance release or scheduled software remedy for a security vulnerability issue.  |  February 17, 2022  |  
|  End of Routine Failure Analysis Date:   
HW   |  The last-possible date a routine failure analysis may be performed to determine the cause of hardware product failure or defect.  |  February 18, 2020  |  
|  End of New Service Attachment Date:

HW   |  The last date to extend or renew a service contract for the product.  |  May 16, 2023  |  
|  Last Date of Support:
