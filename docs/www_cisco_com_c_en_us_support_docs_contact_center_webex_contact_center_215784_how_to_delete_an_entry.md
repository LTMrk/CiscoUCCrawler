This document describes how to delete an Entry Point or Queue in WebEx Contact Center.

##  Steps to create Entry Point and Queue
**Step 1.** Log in to Portal via _<https://portal.ccone.net>_ and click on Provisioning.
**Step 2.** Configure an Entry Point (EP) and Queue under Provisioning. Click on New Entry Point and New Queue tabs respectively.

**Note** : Entry Point - Cisco_Test_EP and Queue - Cisco_Test_Q has been created

## Problem
Routing Strategy for the Cisco_Test_Q has been deleted but when we try to delete the Queue - Cisco_Test_Q, it gives an error**"Cannot deactivate - Virtual Team is referenced by other entities"**
Click on the "i" icon seen in the above error
[![](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/contact-center/webex-contact-center/215784-how-to-delete-an-entry-point-or-queue-08.png)](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/contact-center/webex-contact-center/215784-how-to-delete-an-entry-point-or-queue-08.png "Related image, diagram or screenshot.")
**Note** : System is designed in a way that deleted Routing Strategy is retained under "Deleted Strategies" to avoid human error or needs to re-activate the Routing Strategy, Therefore the queue deletion operation fails with the error "the Routing Strategy existence in the system"
## Solution:
Step 1. Go to Routing Strategy under **Portal** >>**Click on Deleted Strategies** >> **Select the Queue** - Cisco_Test_Q. Referenced Routing Strategy is available
[![](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/contact-center/webex-contact-center/215784-how-to-delete-an-entry-point-or-queue-09.png)](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/contact-center/webex-contact-center/215784-how-to-delete-an-entry-point-or-queue-09.png "Related image, diagram or screenshot.")
Step 2. Delete the Routing Strategy "Cisco_Test_Q_RS" under "Deleted Strategies"
[![](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/contact-center/webex-contact-center/215784-how-to-delete-an-entry-point-or-queue-10.png)](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/contact-center/webex-contact-center/215784-how-to-delete-an-entry-point-or-queue-10.png "Related image, diagram or screenshot.")
Step 3. Go to **Provisioning** >> **Queue** >> Select "Cisco_Test_Q", click on Delete and the operation is successful
[![](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/contact-center/webex-contact-center/215784-how-to-delete-an-entry-point-or-queue-11.png)](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/contact-center/webex-contact-center/215784-how-to-delete-an-entry-point-or-queue-11.png "Related image, diagram or screenshot.")
Delete the Routing Strategy for Cisco_Test_EP, and try to delete the Queue - Cisco_Test_EP.
Routing Strategy for the Cisco_Test_EP has been deleted but when we try to delete the Queue - Cisco_Test_EP, it gives an error**"Cannot deactivate - Virtual Team is referenced by other entities"** as the Routing Strategy is referenced. (See below)
[![](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/contact-center/webex-contact-center/215784-how-to-delete-an-entry-point-or-queue-12.png)](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/contact-center/webex-contact-center/215784-how-to-delete-an-entry-point-or-queue-12.png "Related image, diagram or screenshot.")[![](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/contact-center/webex-contact-center/215784-how-to-delete-an-entry-point-or-queue-13.png)](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/contact-center/webex-contact-center/215784-how-to-delete-an-entry-point-or-queue-13.png "Related image, diagram or screenshot.")
Follow Step 1 to Step 3 in order to delete the Entry Point.
[![](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/contact-center/webex-contact-center/215784-how-to-delete-an-entry-point-or-queue-14.png)](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/contact-center/webex-contact-center/215784-how-to-delete-an-entry-point-or-queue-14.png "Related image, diagram or screenshot.")[![](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/contact-center/webex-contact-center/215784-how-to-delete-an-entry-point-or-queue-15.png)](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/contact-center/webex-contact-center/215784-how-to-delete-an-entry-point-or-queue-15.png "Related image, diagram or screenshot.")
