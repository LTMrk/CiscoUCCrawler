Join the ranks of over 23,000 professionals who are shaping the future of collaboration through the [Webex Insider User Group Program](https://community.cisco.com/t5/cisco-insider-user-group/ct-p/ccp-home "Webex Insider User Group Program"), a vibrant community powered by Cisco Insider. This initiative is not just a program; it’s a movement of collaboration enthusiasts, industry professionals, and thought leaders all driven by a shared passion for improving the way we communicate and work together using Webex.
## Contribute to product evolution
Formerly known as Webex Experts, the User Group Program is a collaborative hub where the most dedicated Webex users come together to offer valuable product feedback and insights. This collective intelligence is what fuels the continuous evolution and improvement of Webex solutions, ensuring that our solutions meet the needs of a diverse and dynamic user base.
## Unlock the benefits of Webex User Group
By joining the Webex Insider User Group Program, you’ll unlock a variety of benefits designed to supercharge your Webex experience:
  * **Network Expansion** : Connect with a global network of professionals who share your dedication to excellence in collaboration.
  * **Shape innovation** : Influence the trajectory of Webex by sharing your insights and contributing to product development.
  * **Insider Knowledge** : Receive exclusive updates and be the first to explore new features before they hit the market.
  * **Utilization Mastery** : Learn advanced strategies to enhance how your company leverages Webex, directly from the experts.


## Your exclusive insider benefits
As an Insider, your engagement goes beyond mere participation. You’ll have the opportunity to
  * **Collaborate Closely** : Interact with Webex product, engineering, and go-to-market teams to exchange best practices and experiences.
  * **Access Exclusive Content** : Delve into technical briefings, roadmap sessions, and live demos reserved exclusively for members.
  * **Direct Product Influence** : Offer direct feedback to the product teams and play an active role in guiding the future of Webex solutions.


## Join the Elite Experts Group
Within the User Group Program, the Elite Experts group is a select group of customers representing the top 1% of the community. These members are chosen annually in November. Elite Experts are crucial in directing our product roadmap, participating in focus groups, surveys, and forums, and providing invaluable insights to our Business Units and Product teams. Keep an eye out for the application later this year.
### Exclusive Expert Perks
  * **Elite Circle** : Be one of the first-class Webex enthusiasts with direct influence on product direction.
  * **Unparalleled Access** : Engage directly with Business Units through dedicated Webex spaces.
  * **Industry Recognition** : Serve as panelists and speakers at prestigious Cisco events.
  * **Feedback Forums** : Participate in focus groups and surveys to refine and enhance Webex solutions.
  * **Community Leadership** : Drive discussions and engagement in community forums, becoming a beacon for others.


## Join the Webex Insider User Group Today
The Webex Insider User Group Program is where your voice can truly help shape the future of Webex, and your expertise can lay the foundation for the next generation of collaboration tools. If you’re passionate about making a difference and eager to contribute to the growth of Webex, this program awaits your unique perspective.
Join us today to become an integral part of a community that’s not just using technology but actively defining it. Visit the [User Group page](https://community.cisco.com/t5/customer-connection/ct-p/ccp-home "User Group page") and scroll down to the “Log in/Apply to the Cisco Insider User Group“ button to take the first step into a larger world of collaboration and influence.
