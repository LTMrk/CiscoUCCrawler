## Objective
The Cisco IP Phone 7800 and 8800 Series Multiplatform Phones comprise a set of full-featured Voice-over-Internet Protocol (VoIP) phones that provide voice communication over an IP network. The phones provide all the features of traditional business phones, such as call forwarding, redialing, speed dialing, transferring calls, and conference calling. The Cisco IP Phone 7800 and 8800 Series Multiplatform Phones are targeted for solutions that are centered on third-party Session Initiation Protocol (SIP)-based IP Private Branch Exchange (PBX).
The Cisco IP Phone 7800 and 8800 Series Multiplatform Phones allows you to customize settings such as the phone name, wallpaper, logo, screen saver, brightness display, back light setting, and so on.
This article provides instructions on how to configure the brightness display settings through the web-based utility or the Graphical User Interface (GUI) of your Cisco IP Phone 7800 or 8800 Series Multiplatform Phone.
### Applicable Devices | Firmware Version
  * IP Phone 7800 Series | 11.0.1 ([Download latest](https://software.cisco.com/download/home/284883944))
  * IP Phone 8800 Series | 11.0.1 ([Download latest](https://software.cisco.com/download/home/284729655))


## Configure Brightness Display Settings
### Through the Web-based Utility
Step 1. Access the web-based utility of your IP Phone then choose **Admin Login > Advanced**.
[![](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/smb/collaboration-endpoints/cisco-ip-phone-7800-series/images/ijgm-05232017-brightness-display-settings-web-step1.png)](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/smb/collaboration-endpoints/cisco-ip-phone-7800-series/images/ijgm-05232017-brightness-display-settings-web-step1.png "Related image, diagram or screenshot.")
Step 2. Choose **Voice > Phone**.
[![](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/smb/collaboration-endpoints/cisco-ip-phone-7800-series/images/ijgm-05232017-brightness-display-settings-web-step2.png)](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/smb/collaboration-endpoints/cisco-ip-phone-7800-series/images/ijgm-05232017-brightness-display-settings-web-step2.png "Related image, diagram or screenshot.")
Step 3. Scroll down to the Screen area, then enter a value from 1 to 15 in the _Display Brightness_ field. The higher the number, the greater the brightness on the IP phone screen.
[![](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/smb/collaboration-endpoints/cisco-ip-phone-7800-series/images/ijgm-05232017-brightness-display-settings-web-step3.png)](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/smb/collaboration-endpoints/cisco-ip-phone-7800-series/images/ijgm-05232017-brightness-display-settings-web-step3.png "Related image, diagram or screenshot.")
**Note:** In this example, 15 is used.
Step 4. Click **Submit All Changes**.
[![](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/smb/collaboration-endpoints/cisco-ip-phone-7800-series/images/ijgm-05232017-brightness-display-settings-web-step4.png)](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/smb/collaboration-endpoints/cisco-ip-phone-7800-series/images/ijgm-05232017-brightness-display-settings-web-step4.png "Related image, diagram or screenshot.")
You should now have successfully configured the brightness display settings on your Cisco IP Phone 7800 or 8800 Series Multiplatform Phone through the web-based utility.
### Through the GUI of the IP Phone
Step 1. Press the **Settings** button on your IP Phone.
[![](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/smb/collaboration-endpoints/cisco-ip-phone-7800-series/images/ijgm-05232017-brightness-display-settings-gui-step1.png)](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/smb/collaboration-endpoints/cisco-ip-phone-7800-series/images/ijgm-05232017-brightness-display-settings-gui-step1.png "Related image, diagram or screenshot.")
Step 2. Choose **User preferences** using the circle navigation [![](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/smb/collaboration-endpoints/cisco-ip-phone-7800-series/images/ijgm-05232017-brightness-display-settings-gui-step2.png)](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/smb/collaboration-endpoints/cisco-ip-phone-7800-series/images/ijgm-05232017-brightness-display-settings-gui-step2.png "Related image, diagram or screenshot.") button.
[![](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/smb/collaboration-endpoints/cisco-ip-phone-7800-series/images/ijgm-05232017-brightness-display-settings-gui-step2a.png)](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/smb/collaboration-endpoints/cisco-ip-phone-7800-series/images/ijgm-05232017-brightness-display-settings-gui-step2a.png "Related image, diagram or screenshot.")
Step 3. Choose **Screen preferences**.
[![](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/smb/collaboration-endpoints/cisco-ip-phone-7800-series/images/ijgm-05232017-brightness-display-settings-gui-step3.png)](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/smb/collaboration-endpoints/cisco-ip-phone-7800-series/images/ijgm-05232017-brightness-display-settings-gui-step3.png "Related image, diagram or screenshot.")
Step 4. Select **Display Brightness**. 
Step 5. Press the left or right arrows of the navigation button on your IP Phone to adjust the brightness display.
[![](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/smb/collaboration-endpoints/cisco-ip-phone-7800-series/images/ijgm-05232017-brightness-display-settings-gui-step4.png)](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/smb/collaboration-endpoints/cisco-ip-phone-7800-series/images/ijgm-05232017-brightness-display-settings-gui-step4.png "Related image, diagram or screenshot.") [![](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/smb/collaboration-endpoints/cisco-ip-phone-7800-series/images/ijgm-05232017-brightness-display-settings-gui-step4a.png)](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/smb/collaboration-endpoints/cisco-ip-phone-7800-series/images/ijgm-05232017-brightness-display-settings-gui-step4a.png "Related image, diagram or screenshot.")
**Note:** ​In this example, the display brightness is set to 20. Some models my show a brightness display instead of entering a number. The GUI will adjust the brightness display accordingly.
Step 5. Press the **Set** or **Save** softkey to save the settings.
[![](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/smb/collaboration-endpoints/cisco-ip-phone-7800-series/images/ijgm-05232017-brightness-display-settings-gui-step5.png)](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/smb/collaboration-endpoints/cisco-ip-phone-7800-series/images/ijgm-05232017-brightness-display-settings-gui-step5.png "Related image, diagram or screenshot.")
You should now have successfully configured the brightness display settings on your Cisco IP Phone 7800 or 8800 Series Multiplatform Phone through the GUI.
