## Introduction
This document describes how to troubleshoot Unpartitioned SD cards error on Flexflash Controller.
## Prerequisites
### Requirements
There are no specific requirements for this document.
### Component Used
The information in this document is based on these software and hardware versions:
  * Standalone C series server
  * Flexflash Controller FX3S
  * Two SD cards of identical size
  * CIMC version of 3.X or higher


The information in this document was created from the devices in a specific lab environment. All of the devices used in this document started with a cleared (default) configuration. If your network is live, ensure that you understand the potential impact of any command.
## **Problem**
**Error messages and fault code**
**Fault Code: F1260**
**Description** : Flex Flash Virtual Drive 1 (Hypervisor) Degraded: please check the flash device or the controller
**Reason** : Equipment_Degraded
**Fault Code: F1262**
**Description** : Flexflash controller Flexflash-0 configuration error: Configure the Flexflash controller correctly
**Reason:** Equipment_Unhealthy
**Note** : Scenario where the issue is with 1 SD card Present.
[![](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/servers-unified-computing/ucs-c-series-rack-servers/215930-troubleshoot-unpartitioned-sd-cards-in-c-00.png)](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/servers-unified-computing/ucs-c-series-rack-servers/215930-troubleshoot-unpartitioned-sd-cards-in-c-00.png "Related image, diagram or screenshot.")
After you navigate to **Storage > Cisco Flexflash**, it is seen that SD card 1 as unpartitioned and Virtual drive Hypervisor degraded as expected
[![](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/servers-unified-computing/ucs-c-series-rack-servers/215930-troubleshoot-unpartitioned-sd-cards-in-c-01.png)](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/servers-unified-computing/ucs-c-series-rack-servers/215930-troubleshoot-unpartitioned-sd-cards-in-c-01.png "Related image, diagram or screenshot.")
The flexflash controller status is shown in the image here.
[![](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/servers-unified-computing/ucs-c-series-rack-servers/215930-troubleshoot-unpartitioned-sd-cards-in-c-02.png)](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/servers-unified-computing/ucs-c-series-rack-servers/215930-troubleshoot-unpartitioned-sd-cards-in-c-02.png "Related image, diagram or screenshot.")
**Note** : Unpartitioned SD card has been degraded and can proceed with the RMA? No, replacement might not resolve the issue.
## **Solution**
Step 1. Under **General** Tab, click **Configure cards** , as shown in this image.
[![](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/servers-unified-computing/ucs-c-series-rack-servers/215930-troubleshoot-unpartitioned-sd-cards-in-c-03.png)](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/servers-unified-computing/ucs-c-series-rack-servers/215930-troubleshoot-unpartitioned-sd-cards-in-c-03.png "Related image, diagram or screenshot.")
Step 2. Select Primary card as **SLOT 2**(because Slot 1 is used for the unpartitioned card and it is assumed that all the data is stored in SD card 2). Check **AUTO-SYNC** , click **SAVE** , as shown in this image.
**[![](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/servers-unified-computing/ucs-c-series-rack-servers/215930-troubleshoot-unpartitioned-sd-cards-in-c-04.jpeg)](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/servers-unified-computing/ucs-c-series-rack-servers/215930-troubleshoot-unpartitioned-sd-cards-in-c-04.jpeg "Related image, diagram or screenshot.")**
Step 3. After you click on **SAVE,** a window appears, as shown in this image, click **OK.**
**[![](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/servers-unified-computing/ucs-c-series-rack-servers/215930-troubleshoot-unpartitioned-sd-cards-in-c-05.jpeg)](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/servers-unified-computing/ucs-c-series-rack-servers/215930-troubleshoot-unpartitioned-sd-cards-in-c-05.jpeg "Related image, diagram or screenshot.")**
Step 4. After you click **OK,** notice that in Physical drives Tab, that Slot 1 which had unpartitioned card is to be configured as **MIRROR_SECONDARY** but **UNHEALTHY** , this is an expected behavior
[![](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/servers-unified-computing/ucs-c-series-rack-servers/215930-troubleshoot-unpartitioned-sd-cards-in-c-06.jpeg)](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/servers-unified-computing/ucs-c-series-rack-servers/215930-troubleshoot-unpartitioned-sd-cards-in-c-06.jpeg "Related image, diagram or screenshot.")
Step 5. Under the **Virtual Drives** tab and you see that Virtual Drive's **Auto-Sync** is initiated (in step 2, **auto-sync** is checked). Wait for the auto-sync to complete 100%
[![](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/servers-unified-computing/ucs-c-series-rack-servers/215930-troubleshoot-unpartitioned-sd-cards-in-c-07.jpeg)](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/servers-unified-computing/ucs-c-series-rack-servers/215930-troubleshoot-unpartitioned-sd-cards-in-c-07.jpeg "Related image, diagram or screenshot.")
Step 6. After **Auto-Sync** is complete, as shown in this image, you see Virtual drive as **Healthy**.
**[![](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/servers-unified-computing/ucs-c-series-rack-servers/215930-troubleshoot-unpartitioned-sd-cards-in-c-08.jpeg)](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/servers-unified-computing/ucs-c-series-rack-servers/215930-troubleshoot-unpartitioned-sd-cards-in-c-08.jpeg "Related image, diagram or screenshot.")**
The card in Slot no. 1, which is seen healthy in step 4 is now **HEALTHY** after **AUTO-SYNC.**
**[![](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/servers-unified-computing/ucs-c-series-rack-servers/215930-troubleshoot-unpartitioned-sd-cards-in-c-09.jpeg)](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/servers-unified-computing/ucs-c-series-rack-servers/215930-troubleshoot-unpartitioned-sd-cards-in-c-09.jpeg "Related image, diagram or screenshot.")**
Step 7. The Virtual Drive is still not host-accessible if you observe here, which means you cannot see it in the boot option and cannot boot your OS from there or install the OS on it.
[![](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/servers-unified-computing/ucs-c-series-rack-servers/215930-troubleshoot-unpartitioned-sd-cards-in-c-10.png)](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/servers-unified-computing/ucs-c-series-rack-servers/215930-troubleshoot-unpartitioned-sd-cards-in-c-10.png "Related image, diagram or screenshot.")
Step 8. In order to make it host accessible, under **Virtual drive** , click **ENABLE VIRTUAL DRIVE.**
**[![](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/servers-unified-computing/ucs-c-series-rack-servers/215930-troubleshoot-unpartitioned-sd-cards-in-c-11.png)](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/servers-unified-computing/ucs-c-series-rack-servers/215930-troubleshoot-unpartitioned-sd-cards-in-c-11.png "Related image, diagram or screenshot.")**
Step 9. Now it is observed that the Virtual drive is connected to the host and visible on the boot menu as shown in this image.
[![](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/servers-unified-computing/ucs-c-series-rack-servers/215930-troubleshoot-unpartitioned-sd-cards-in-c-12.png)](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/servers-unified-computing/ucs-c-series-rack-servers/215930-troubleshoot-unpartitioned-sd-cards-in-c-12.png "Related image, diagram or screenshot.")
[![](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/servers-unified-computing/ucs-c-series-rack-servers/215930-troubleshoot-unpartitioned-sd-cards-in-c-13.png)](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/servers-unified-computing/ucs-c-series-rack-servers/215930-troubleshoot-unpartitioned-sd-cards-in-c-13.png "Related image, diagram or screenshot.")
### When you Forget to Check AUTO-SYNC in Step 2?
The virtual drive does not sync automatically and SD card 1 continues to show as **UNHEALTHY.** Under **VIRTUAL DRIVES** tab, check on the Virtual drive and click **SYNC VIRTUAL DRIVE.** This is called **MANUAL SYNC**. Quite self-explanatory.
After Sync is completed, the Virtual drive and SD card 1 becomes healthy as shown in **step 6.**
**[![](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/servers-unified-computing/ucs-c-series-rack-servers/215930-troubleshoot-unpartitioned-sd-cards-in-c-14.jpeg)](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/servers-unified-computing/ucs-c-series-rack-servers/215930-troubleshoot-unpartitioned-sd-cards-in-c-14.jpeg "Related image, diagram or screenshot.")**
