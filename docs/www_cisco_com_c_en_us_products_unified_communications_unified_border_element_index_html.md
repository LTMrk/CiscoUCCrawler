## Session border control for any company size
Get highly secure voice and video connectivity from the enterprise IP network to service provider SIP trunks. Cisco Unified Border Element (CUBE) performs the four critical functions of a session border controller: session control, security, interworking, and demarcation. Migrate to SIP in a way that works for your business by controlling the pace and strategy.

## Features and benefits
### Take advantage of deployment options
Deploy virtually on Cisco UCS servers or integrated into a Cisco router. Choice of deployment includes support for centralized, distributed, or hybrid deployments—often with little to no hardware investment.
### Enjoy flexibility, scalability, redundancy
Provide multiple services on the same routing platform and use router features to improve network interworking. CUBE scales to 64,000 sessions with box-to-box and in-box redundancy.
### Enable more capabilities and options
With CUBE, it’s easier to enable capabilities including simplified recording features, voice policy for better security, WebEx Cloud Connected Audio to reduce costs, and line-side capabilities to simplify support. 
### Protect your investment
Repurpose network equipment assets as the voice network evolves. CUBE features built-in interoperability with third-party IP PBXs and service providers.
