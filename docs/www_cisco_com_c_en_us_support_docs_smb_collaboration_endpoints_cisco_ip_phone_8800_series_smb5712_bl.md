## Objective
Blocking anonymous calls on the Cisco IP Phone 8800 Series Multiplatform Phone lets you filter incoming calls that do not show caller information. If enabled, this feature automatically rejects specific calls.
This article aims to show you how to enable anonymous call blocking on the Cisco IP Phone 8800 Series Multiplatform through its web-based utility and phone GUI.
### Applicable Devices | Firmware Version
  * IP Phone 8800 Series | 11.0.1 ([Download latest](https://software.cisco.com/download/home/284729655))


The 8800 series phones referenced in this article are not Enterprise phones that use a specific call controller. If you would like to compare the two different types of phones, check out [Compare & Contrast: Cisco IP MPP Phones & Cisco Unified IP Phones](https://www.cisco.com/c/en/us/support/docs/smb/collaboration-endpoints/cisco-ip-phone-8800-series/1574-Compare-and-Contrast-Cisco-IP-MPP-Phones-and-Cisco-Unified-IP-Phones.html). 
## Block Anonymous Call
### Through the Web-based Utility
Step 1. Log in to the web-based utility of the IP phone and click **Admin Login > a****dvanced**.
[![](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/smb/collaboration-endpoints/cisco-ip-phone-8800-series/images/rjs-08312017-blockaweb-step1.png)](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/smb/collaboration-endpoints/cisco-ip-phone-8800-series/images/rjs-08312017-blockaweb-step1.png "Related image, diagram or screenshot.")
Step 2. Click **Voice > User**.
[![](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/smb/collaboration-endpoints/cisco-ip-phone-8800-series/images/rjs-08312017-blockaweb-step2.png)](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/smb/collaboration-endpoints/cisco-ip-phone-8800-series/images/rjs-08312017-blockaweb-step2.png "Related image, diagram or screenshot.")
Step 3. Under Supplementary Services, choose **Yes** from the Block ANC Setting drop-down menu.
[![](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/smb/collaboration-endpoints/cisco-ip-phone-8800-series/images/rjs-08312017-blockaweb-step3.png)](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/smb/collaboration-endpoints/cisco-ip-phone-8800-series/images/rjs-08312017-blockaweb-step3.png "Related image, diagram or screenshot.")
Step 4. Click **Submit All Changes**.
[![](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/smb/collaboration-endpoints/cisco-ip-phone-8800-series/images/rjs-08312017-blockaweb-step4.png)](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/smb/collaboration-endpoints/cisco-ip-phone-8800-series/images/rjs-08312017-blockaweb-step4.png "Related image, diagram or screenshot.")
You should now have successfully blocked anonymous calls on your phone through the web-based utility.

Step 1. Press the **Applications** button on the IP Phone.

Step 2. Using the Navigation cluster [![](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/smb/collaboration-endpoints/cisco-ip-phone-8800-series/images/rjs-08312017-blockgui-step2.png)](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/smb/collaboration-endpoints/cisco-ip-phone-8800-series/images/rjs-08312017-blockgui-step2.png "Related image, diagram or screenshot.") button, navigate to **User****preferences** then press the**Select** softkey.
[![](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/smb/collaboration-endpoints/cisco-ip-phone-8800-series/images/rjs-08312017-blockgui-step2b.png)](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/smb/collaboration-endpoints/cisco-ip-phone-8800-series/images/rjs-08312017-blockgui-step2b.png "Related image, diagram or screenshot.")
Step 3. Choose **Call preferences** then press the**Select** softkey.
[![](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/smb/collaboration-endpoints/cisco-ip-phone-8800-series/images/rjs-08312017-blockgui-step3.png)](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/smb/collaboration-endpoints/cisco-ip-phone-8800-series/images/rjs-08312017-blockgui-step3.png "Related image, diagram or screenshot.")

Navigate to **Block a****nonymous****call** and then press the Navigation cluster [![](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/smb/collaboration-endpoints/cisco-ip-phone-8800-series/images/rjs-08312017-blockgui-step4.png)](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/smb/collaboration-endpoints/cisco-ip-phone-8800-series/images/rjs-08312017-blockgui-step4.png "Related image, diagram or screenshot.") right button to set it to**On**.

Step 5. Press the **Set** softkey.
[![](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/smb/collaboration-endpoints/cisco-ip-phone-8800-series/images/rjs-08312017-blockgui-step5.png)](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/smb/collaboration-endpoints/cisco-ip-phone-8800-series/images/rjs-08312017-blockgui-step5.png "Related image, diagram or screenshot.")
