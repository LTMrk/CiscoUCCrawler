Configure TR-069 on the Cisco IP Phone 7800 Series or 8800 Series Multiplatform Phone
## Objective
TR-069 defines the Customer Premise Equipment Wide Area Network Management Protocol (CWMP). It describes the interaction between an auto-configuration server (ACS) and one or more endpoints that supports CWMP. TR-069 makes “no-touch” installation, deployment of new services, firmware maintenance, and troubleshooting possible. Configuring TR-069 on the Cisco IP Phone 7800 or 8800 Series Multiplatform Phones allows the device to communicate with the ACS and be able to request updates. It will also allow the ACS to instantly reach the phone in case new services are to be deployed.
This article aims to show you how to configure TR-069 on the Cisco IP Phone 7800 Series or 8800 Series Multiplatform Phone. It assumes that the TR-069 settings on the ACS has already been configured. To complete the configuration, the following details are needed:
  * ACS Username
  * ACS Password
  * ACS URL
  * Connection Request Username
  * Connection Request Password
  * (Optional) Backup ACS URL
  * (Optional) Backup ACS Username
  * (Optional) Backup ACS Password


## Applicable Devices
  * 7800 Series
  * 8800 Series


## Software Version
  * 11.0.1


## Configure TR-069
Step 1. Log in to the web-based utility of the phone and click **Admin Login > a****dvanced**.
[![](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/smb/collaboration-endpoints/cisco-ip-phone-7800-series/images/ell-08112017-tr09-step1.png)](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/smb/collaboration-endpoints/cisco-ip-phone-7800-series/images/ell-08112017-tr09-step1.png "Related image, diagram or screenshot.")
Step 2. Click the **Voice** tab.
[![](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/smb/collaboration-endpoints/cisco-ip-phone-7800-series/images/ell-08112017-tr09-step2.png)](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/smb/collaboration-endpoints/cisco-ip-phone-7800-series/images/ell-08112017-tr09-step2.png "Related image, diagram or screenshot.")
Step 3. Click the **TR-069** sub-tab.
[![](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/smb/collaboration-endpoints/cisco-ip-phone-7800-series/images/ell-08112017-tr09-step3.png)](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/smb/collaboration-endpoints/cisco-ip-phone-7800-series/images/ell-08112017-tr09-step3.png "Related image, diagram or screenshot.")
Step 4. Under TR-069, choose **Yes** from the Enable TR-069 drop-down list.
[![](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/smb/collaboration-endpoints/cisco-ip-phone-7800-series/images/ell-08112017-tr09-step4.png)](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/smb/collaboration-endpoints/cisco-ip-phone-7800-series/images/ell-08112017-tr09-step4.png "Related image, diagram or screenshot.")
**Note:** The default value is No. In this example, Yes is chosen.
Step 5. Enter the ACS URL in the _ACS URL_ field. The ACS URL is provided by the Internet Service Provider (ISP).
[![](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/smb/collaboration-endpoints/cisco-ip-phone-7800-series/images/ell-08112017-tr09-step5.png)](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/smb/collaboration-endpoints/cisco-ip-phone-7800-series/images/ell-08112017-tr09-step5.png "Related image, diagram or screenshot.")
**Note:** In this example, [www.cpeacs.com](http://www.cpeacs.com) is entered.
Step 6. Enter the user name in the _ACS Username_ field. This is the username that would allow the phone to connect to the ACS.
[![](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/smb/collaboration-endpoints/cisco-ip-phone-7800-series/images/ell-08112017-tr09-step6.png)](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/smb/collaboration-endpoints/cisco-ip-phone-7800-series/images/ell-08112017-tr09-step6.png "Related image, diagram or screenshot.")
**Note:** In this example, CPE1 is entered.
Step 7. Enter the password in the _ACS Password_ field. This is the password that would allow the phone to connect to the ACS.
[![](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/smb/collaboration-endpoints/cisco-ip-phone-7800-series/images/ell-08112017-tr09-step7.png)](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/smb/collaboration-endpoints/cisco-ip-phone-7800-series/images/ell-08112017-tr09-step7.png "Related image, diagram or screenshot.")
Step 8. Enter the request username in the _Connection Request Username_ field. This will be used by the ACS to authenticate the CPE.
[![](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/smb/collaboration-endpoints/cisco-ip-phone-7800-series/images/ell-08112017-tr09-step8.png)](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/smb/collaboration-endpoints/cisco-ip-phone-7800-series/images/ell-08112017-tr09-step8.png "Related image, diagram or screenshot.")
**Note:** In this example, CPEUser is entered.
Step 9. Enter the request password in the _Connection Request Password_ field. This will be used by the ACS to complete the authentication process with the CPE.
[![](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/smb/collaboration-endpoints/cisco-ip-phone-7800-series/images/ell-08112017-tr09-step9.png)](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/smb/collaboration-endpoints/cisco-ip-phone-7800-series/images/ell-08112017-tr09-step9.png "Related image, diagram or screenshot.")
Step 10. (Optional) Enter the inform interval value in the _Periodic Inform Interval_ field. The interval value is measured in seconds. It determines the interval at which the phone contacts to the ACS.
[![](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/smb/collaboration-endpoints/cisco-ip-phone-7800-series/images/ell-08112017-tr09-step10.png)](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/smb/collaboration-endpoints/cisco-ip-phone-7800-series/images/ell-08112017-tr09-step10.png "Related image, diagram or screenshot.")
**Note:** The default value is 20. In this example, 20 is used.
Step 11. (Optional) Verify that Periodic Inform Enable is set to Yes. This allows the phone to communicate with the ACS periodically. This can be changed by choosing an option from the Periodic Inform Enable drop-down list.
[![](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/smb/collaboration-endpoints/cisco-ip-phone-7800-series/images/ell-08112017-tr09-step11.png)](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/smb/collaboration-endpoints/cisco-ip-phone-7800-series/images/ell-08112017-tr09-step11.png "Related image, diagram or screenshot.")
**Note:** The default value is Yes.
Step 12. (Optional) From the TR-069 Traceability drop-down list, choose **Yes** to activate TR-069 transaction traceability. Choose **No** to disable the feature.
[![](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/smb/collaboration-endpoints/cisco-ip-phone-7800-series/images/ell-08112017-tr09-step12.png)](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/smb/collaboration-endpoints/cisco-ip-phone-7800-series/images/ell-08112017-tr09-step12.png "Related image, diagram or screenshot.")
**Note:** The default value is No.
Step 13. (Optional) From the CWMP V1.2 Support drop-down list, choose **Yes** to enable the feature. Choose **No** to deactivate.
[![](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/smb/collaboration-endpoints/cisco-ip-phone-7800-series/images/ell-08112017-tr09-step13.png)](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/smb/collaboration-endpoints/cisco-ip-phone-7800-series/images/ell-08112017-tr09-step13.png "Related image, diagram or screenshot.")
**Note:** The default value is Yes.
Step 14. (Optional) From the TR-069 VoiceObject Init drop-down list, choose **Y****es** to activate the feature. Choose **No** to deactivate.
[![](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/smb/collaboration-endpoints/cisco-ip-phone-7800-series/images/ell-08112017-tr09-step14.png)](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/smb/collaboration-endpoints/cisco-ip-phone-7800-series/images/ell-08112017-tr09-step14.png "Related image, diagram or screenshot.")
**Note:** The default value is Yes.
Step 15. (Optional) From the TR-069 DHCPOption Init drop-down list, choose **Yes** to activate the feature. Choose **No** to deactivate.
[![](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/smb/collaboration-endpoints/cisco-ip-phone-7800-series/images/ell-08112017-tr09-step15.png)](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/smb/collaboration-endpoints/cisco-ip-phone-7800-series/images/ell-08112017-tr09-step15.png "Related image, diagram or screenshot.")
**Note:** The default value is Yes.
Step 16. (Optional) From the TR-069 Fallback Support drop-down list, choose **Yes** to enable the feature. Choose **No** to deactivate.
[![](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/smb/collaboration-endpoints/cisco-ip-phone-7800-series/images/ell-08112017-tr09-step16.png)](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/smb/collaboration-endpoints/cisco-ip-phone-7800-series/images/ell-08112017-tr09-step16.png "Related image, diagram or screenshot.")
**Note:** The default value is No.
Step 17. (Optional) Enter the backup ACS URL in the _BACKUP ACS URL_ field.
[![](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/smb/collaboration-endpoints/cisco-ip-phone-7800-series/images/ell-08112017-tr09-step17.png)](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/smb/collaboration-endpoints/cisco-ip-phone-7800-series/images/ell-08112017-tr09-step17.png "Related image, diagram or screenshot.")
**Note:** By default, this field is left blank.
Step 18. (Optional) Enter the backup ACS username in the _BACKUP ACS User_ field.
[![](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/smb/collaboration-endpoints/cisco-ip-phone-7800-series/images/ell-08112017-tr09-step18.png)](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/smb/collaboration-endpoints/cisco-ip-phone-7800-series/images/ell-08112017-tr09-step18.png "Related image, diagram or screenshot.")
**Note:** By default, this field is left blank.
Step 19. (Optional) Enter the backup ACS password in the _BACKUP ACS Password_ field.
[![](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/smb/collaboration-endpoints/cisco-ip-phone-7800-series/images/ell-08112017-tr09-step19.png)](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/smb/collaboration-endpoints/cisco-ip-phone-7800-series/images/ell-08112017-tr09-step19.png "Related image, diagram or screenshot.")
**Note:** By default, this field is left blank.
Step 20. Click **Submit All Changes**.
[![](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/smb/collaboration-endpoints/cisco-ip-phone-7800-series/images/ell-08112017-tr09-step20.png)](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/smb/collaboration-endpoints/cisco-ip-phone-7800-series/images/ell-08112017-tr09-step20.png "Related image, diagram or screenshot.")
