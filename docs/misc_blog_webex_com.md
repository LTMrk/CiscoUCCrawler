##### The Workplace Paradox
Here’s what keeps me up at night: we’re building workplaces for people who don’t work the way we think they do anymore.
I’ve spent the better part of three decades in technology, and I’ve never seen a moment quite like this. The office isn’t dead, but it’s not what it was. Remote work isn’t a pandemic relic; it’s a preference. And AI isn’t coming to transform work. It’s already here. It’s in our meetings, summarizing our calls, and quietly rewriting the rules of productivity. Yet for all this change, most organizations are still operating with frameworks built for a world that no longer exists.
