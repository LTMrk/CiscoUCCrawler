##### The Workplace Paradox
Here’s what keeps me up at night: we’re building workplaces for people who don’t work the way we think they do anymore.
I’ve spent the better part of three decades in technology, and I’ve never seen a moment quite like this. The office isn’t dead, but it’s not what it was. Remote work isn’t a pandemic relic; it’s a preference. And AI isn’t coming to transform work. It’s already here. It’s in our meetings, summarizing our calls, and quietly rewriting the rules of productivity. Yet for all this change, most organizations are still operating with frameworks built for a world that no longer exists.
That’s the disconnect. Companies are trying to solve yesterday’s problems with outdated thinking. They’re deploying AI without strategy, sprinkling it across products like seasoning without understanding the recipe. They’re investing in devices without understanding how spaces actually get used. They’re chasing innovation without asking the most important question:  _what are we actually trying to do?_
At [Cisco Live EMEA](https://www.ciscolive.com/emea.html), we’re not just showcasing products. We’re presenting a vision. One where Connected Intelligence becomes the operating system for how work happens. Where people and AI don’t compete, they each make the other better. Where physical and digital spaces blend seamlessly. Where customer experiences aren’t transactional, they’re transformational. 
This is about building the workplace of today for the workforce of tomorrow. And it starts with understanding that intelligence isn’t just in the cloud. It’s everywhere: at the edge, in the room, on the desk, in every conversation, and across every interaction.
## Intelligent Workspaces – Where Distance Becomes Zero
For years, we’ve talked about “bringing the remote participant into the room.” Noble goal. Wrong framing. The real challenge isn’t making remote people feel included. It’s making _every_ space intelligent enough to adapt to how people naturally collaborate, whether they’re across the table or across the world.
![](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns=%22http://www.w3.org/2000/svg%22%20viewBox=%220%200%201672%201028%22%3E%3C/svg%3E)
This is what we call**Distance Zero:** the idea that intelligence starts at the edge, where devices capture people, space, and context in real time, so AI can improve the work experience as it happens. Not after the fact. Not in some cloud data center. Right there, in the moment, adapting to what’s unfolding. 
**The Room Kit Pro G2 and Desk Pro G2** : Cinematic Quality, Simplified. Walk into any executive boardroom and you’ll find a paradox: incredible technology that’s incredibly complicated. The **Cisco Room Kit Pro G2** changes that equation. This is a powerful room system for large rooms with multi-camera support, one-click setup, and AI-powered video intelligence that frames speakers dynamically. Designed to simplify large rooms and the most demanding AV scenarios with advanced multi-camera intelligence, scalable AVoIP room orchestration, single-click setup, NVIDIA-powered AI workflows, and seamless meetings on any platform. This is collaboration that feels effortless.

From a single building to your entire global portfolio.

## Architecting the desk phone for modern work.
Enterprise desk phones were once designed and evaluated as fixed, single-purpose voice devices. But as hybrid work becomes standard, organizations need endpoints that can support flexible workspaces, personalized user experiences, and seamless cloud or on-premises operations.
The 9800 Series was built for this new reality. By bringing calling, workspace management, emergency notifications, and centralized administration into one architecture, Cisco helps IT teams reduce complexity while giving employees a more intuitive workplace experience.
## Five innovations that set Cisco apart, according to Frost & Sullivan.
#### 1. End-to-end security: hardware, privacy physical safety
The 9800 Series protects organizational privacy, infrastructure, and physical employee safety within a single hardware platform:
  * **Hardware-based root of trust:** The first desk phone portfolio featuring **TPM 2.0** as a dedicated hardware chipset, defending devices against supply-chain tampering and physical breach attempts.
  * **Private & secure communications: **Guarantees data privacy and network integrity through **TLS 1.3 encryption** , Secure Boot, image authentication, runtime defenses, and a built-in stateful firewall to prevent unauthorized access.
  * **Physical safety emergency escalation:** Includes a dedicated, high-visibility **[red Action Button](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=C7rzX3jSIvM)** directly on the device. IT teams can configure it for instant emergency calls, silent alerts, or custom facility dispatch with a single press.


#### 2. AI-powered audio & automated call capabilities
Instead of just routing voice calls, the 9800 Series actively enhances how teams communicate—removing background noise and language barriers while automatically capturing key action items and sending follow-up summaries straight to your inbox after the call:
  * **Distraction-free audio quality:** [Bidirectional AI noise suppression](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IMNfFYsycgc) filters out background chaos—like open-office chatter or home noise—on both ends of the line, keeping conversations clear and professional without requiring specialized headsets.
  * **Inclusion accessibility in real time:** Live closed captions and real-time transcription display spoken words directly on screen as people talk, helping hard-of-hearing team members stay fully engaged and easing comprehension in fast-moving discussions.
  * **Global team alignment:** Instant language translation breaks down cross-border communication barriers on the fly, allowing international teams and clients to connect naturally across different languages.


#### 3. Simpler deployment across cloud and on-premises environments
Deploying and maintaining thousands of devices can create significant administrative overhead. Cisco simplifies operations by bringing management into [Cisco Cloud Control](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BlyNwKSpxn4) for a consistent platform and streamlined provisioning.
  * **One operating system across deployment models:** PhoneOS supports both Cisco Unified Communications Manager and Webex Calling, helping organizations move between on-premises and cloud environments without complex firmware migrations.
  * **Zero-touch NFC provisioning:** IT teams can configure and onboard devices through Collaboration Control Hub using built-in NFC tags—without removing each phone from its box.
  * **Centralized management & observability:** Managed centrally through [Cisco Collaboration Control Hub](https://www.webex.com/us/en/solutions/cross-platform/control-hub.html), within the single [Cisco Cloud Control](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BlyNwKSpxn4) platform, IT teams get one central place to manage all their Cisco tools, alongside built-in Cisco ThousandEyes intelligence for real-time diagnostics.


#### 4. Intelligent workspace management and desk reservation
The 9800 Series integrates with Cisco Spaces and Webex to make shared workspaces easier to [find, reserve, and use](https://video.cisco.com/detail/video/6384149070112).
> Cisco helps employees address a real and persistent frustration: arriving at an office only to waste time finding a suitable workspace, and solves it with a friction-free, technology-enabled workflow.”
> **— Chris Pennell, Industry Principal, Frost & Sullivan**
  * **At-a-glance availability:** Front and top LED indicators make workspace status easy to see.
  * **Flexible reservations:** Employees can reserve a desk in advance or directly from the phone when they arrive.
  * **Personalized sign-in:** Users can securely sign in from the phone display to access their settings and services.


#### 5. Measurable sustainability and greater ESG visibility
The 9800 Series is designed with sustainability in mind. Models use more than 74% post-consumer recycled plastics, include low-power modes, and are ENERGY STAR certified.
IT teams can also view estimated device-level carbon emissions in Collaboration Control Hub, helping organizations better understand and report on the environmental impact of their device estate.

# Zero Touch Provisioning: Instant Collaboration
On Aug 11, 2026Aug 11, 2026By [Rajesh Patwardhan](https://blog.webex.com/contributors/rpat/)2 Min Read
[ ](https://twitter.com/intent/tweet?url=https://blog.webex.com/workspaces/zero-touch-provisioning-instant-collaboration) [ ](https://www.linkedin.com/shareArticle?mini=true&url=https://blog.webex.com/workspaces/zero-touch-provisioning-instant-collaboration&title=Zero%20Touch%20Provisioning:%20Instant%20Collaboration) [ ](https://www.facebook.com/sharer/sharer.php?u=https://blog.webex.com/workspaces/zero-touch-provisioning-instant-collaboration)
[ ](https://blog.webex.com/workspaces/zero-touch-provisioning-instant-collaboration/ "Copy Link") [ ](https://blog.webex.com/workspaces/zero-touch-provisioning-instant-collaboration/ "Print")
![](https://blog.webex.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/workspace-ztp-1.jpg)
Imagine a workspace that self-configures, authenticates to the network automatically, and becomes instantly functional the moment it is plugged in. No activation codes, no manual scripts, and no back-and-forth verification. With Zero Touch Provisioning (ZTP) becoming generally available for all RoomOS 26 devices, this is now the reality.
By planning the onboarding process in Collaboration Control Hub, service owners can ensure that when an installer plugs in a device, the room comes to life immediately. It is the seamless transition from “ordered” to “operational” that modern IT teams require. 
## From Ordering to Operational: Eliminating Friction
Traditional deployments are often labor-intensive, requiring in-person setup, manual wizard navigation, and the entry of individual activation codes. For large-scale rollouts, this creates several bottlenecks:
  * Operational Complexity: Manually creating hundreds of activation codes and tracking device models is prone to error.
  * Communication Silos: Without a centralized plan, IT, partners, and on-site installers often struggle to stay aligned.
  * High Costs: The need for on-site technicians to visit every room to manually onboard devices are both time-consuming and expensive.


ZTP addresses these challenges by automating the entire lifecycle. In the Control Hub, you can now bulk-create workspaces, assign devices, and apply configuration templates before the hardware even arrives on-site. Once the plan is set, the installer simply connects the device, and the room becomes fully functional.

# How Estes Is Building Smarter Customer Care with Webex AI
On Aug 5, 2026Aug 5, 2026By [Krishna Tyagi](https://blog.webex.com/contributors/ktyagi/), [Jill Townsend](https://blog.webex.com/contributors/jill-townsend/)5 Min Read
[ ](https://twitter.com/intent/tweet?url=https://blog.webex.com/innovation-ai/how-estes-is-building-smarter-customer-care-with-webex-ai) [ ](https://www.linkedin.com/shareArticle?mini=true&url=https://blog.webex.com/innovation-ai/how-estes-is-building-smarter-customer-care-with-webex-ai&title=How%20Estes%20Is%20Building%20Smarter%20Customer%20Care%20with%20Webex%20AI) [ ](https://www.facebook.com/sharer/sharer.php?u=https://blog.webex.com/innovation-ai/how-estes-is-building-smarter-customer-care-with-webex-ai)

![](https://blog.webex.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/estes-truck-hero.jpg)
As North America’s largest privately held freight transportation company, Estes has earned a reputation for delivering high levels of customer care across all 50 states, Canada, Mexico, Puerto Rico, and the Caribbean. They consider every pickup request, tracking question, delivery instruction, and billing inquiry as an opportunity to keep freight moving and build customer confidence and loyalty.
Behind every Estes delivery is a contact center team focused on quality, consistency, coaching and agent productivity. Their 95-year-old company already had a strong quality process involving call reviews, agent report cards, performance tracking, and personalized coaching. But as with many large contact centers, their team faced a familiar challenge. Traditional quality management typically depends on manually assessing less than 2% of calls. Meanwhile, agents still must resolve their after-call effort in addition to focusing on customers.
The result is that valuable signals could be hidden inside everyday conversations. For example, what customers were asking, where agents needed support, which processes created friction, and where coaching could improve the next interaction.
Estes addressed the challenge through their close collaboration with Cisco. Together, the teams used [Webex Contact Center](https://www.webex.com/us/en/products/customer-experience/contact-center.html), [](https://www.webex.com/content/dam/www/us/en/aag/webex-ai-am-at-a-glance-january2026.pdf)[Webex AI Quality Management](https://www.webex.com/us/en/products/customer-experience/ai-workforce-engagement-management.html), and [Cisco AI Assistant](https://www.webex.com/us/en/products/customer-experience/ai-assistant-for-contact-center.html) capabilities to expand visibility, strengthen coaching, reduce repetitive work, and create a more connected view of customer care.
> Partnering with Cisco has been a great experience. From the beginning, we had regular opportunities to share ideas, learn from each other, and work together to improve our operations. We thought we had a good quality audit program in our call center, but we realized we were only scratching the surface of what was possible. Our managers and agents now have real-time access to actionable insights, allowing us to coach more effectively, respond faster, and continuously improve the customer experience.”
> **— Jill Townsend, Director, Corporate Customer Experience, Estes Express Lines**
![](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns=%22http://www.w3.org/2000/svg%22%20viewBox=%220%200%201672%201028%22%3E%3C/svg%3E)
## Overcoming Quality Visibility Gaps 
Estes moved to [Webex Contact Center](https://www.webex.com/us/en/products/customer-experience/contact-center.html) in 2023, creating the cloud foundation for a more modern customer care environment. The AI journey began with a focused question: Could AI help evaluate repeatable parts of more customer interactions while keeping supervisors in control of judgment?
Estes team members John Hoy, Chandler Fuller, Jill Townsend, Jason Amick, and Kyle Sais worked under the leadership of Vice President, Customer Experience and Innovation Carrie Estes Johnstone. Through early-access trials and design-partner sessions, they collaborated with Krishna Tyagi, Shivani Kolala, Mags Moran, Katie NicGabhann, Kirsten Esplin, Karen Sharpe Smith, and other Cisco contributors. The Cisco FDE-led engagement connected Estes directly with AI practitioners as well as Cisco product and engineering. The results turned real-world operational feedback into requirements and coordinated follow-up.
> Choosing the right technology partner is a critical business decision. Our experience with the Cisco AI Team has validated our choice at every turn. Cisco acts as a true strategic partner rather than a traditional software vendor. They took the time to understand our specific operational pain points before recommending solutions.”
> **— John Hoy, Network Engineer** , **Estes Express Lines**
## Building Quality Intelligence and Agent Assistance
Using [Webex AI Quality Management](https://www.webex.com/us/en/products/customer-experience/ai-workforce-engagement-management.html), Estes began expanding from a narrow manual sample toward broader quality intelligence. At the same time, Webex AI Assistant capabilities helped gather intelligence for agents through post-call summaries, mid-call summaries, and auto wrap-up codes as well as visibility into customer sentiment. And the combination mattered. Supervisors gained a broader view of quality while agents were assisted with the work surrounding each interaction.
For the target lines of business, automation became the primary evaluation path for completed quality records. Automated QM activity grew by roughly 80% from March – June 2026.
AI could help evaluate procedural elements consistently, such as whether a required step was followed or whether a customer was placed on hold appropriately. Supervisors could then spend more time on the areas that require experience and context: whether the agent gave the right information, showed empathy, set realistic expectations, and helped move the customer forward.
That distinction is especially important in freight transportation. Customer care often depends on the service center’s practices and regional differences as well as the knowledge agents build from working across teams. Estes did not use AI to remove people from the process. Instead, it used AI to give supervisors better evidence and agents better support so they could focus on improving customer experience while supporting continued business growth.
![](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns=%22http://www.w3.org/2000/svg%22%20viewBox=%220%200%201672%201028%22%3E%3C/svg%3E)
## Turning Insight Into Coaching and Agent Productivity 
As AI-generated quality data scaled, Estes began seeing encouraging signs of adoption across the supervisor workflow. Unique users visiting the Agent Performance grid increased 165% quarter over quarter, and the system recorded hundreds of coaching runs in the last quarter. 
Those signals matter because AI value is realized when people use the insight. Estes wanted quality teams to move faster from a score to the story behind it — all to identify the interactions, patterns, and agents that needed closer attention. Post-call summaries also became part of the agent’s workflow, helping agents document interactions more consistently, reduce repetitive after-call effort, and return their attention to the next customer.
## Measuring Impact
Wrap-up time declined from 15.3 seconds in March 2026 to 10.9 seconds in June 2026 — a reduction of about 28%. This is an early capacity signal; that less time in wrap-up means more time for customer-facing work, coaching, and higher-value follow-through.
## Connecting Quality, Agent Assistance, and Operations
Using near-real-time Topic Analytics, Estes identified more than 480 customer-contact topics, including delivery inquiries, dispatch-location issues, driver-location questions, dock-pickup requests, and pickup rescheduling. Supervisors could focus on the topics relevant to their queues and use near-real-time views when conditions change.The team also explored [Webex AI Workforce Management](https://www.webex.com/us/en/products/customer-experience/ai-workforce-engagement-management.html), testing handle-time anomalies, adjustments, and occupancy assumptions.
> Cisco is on a very positive track towards being able to offer great QM/Auto-QM and WFM solutions for customers that may not require all the capabilities and resources that a call center with thousands of representatives would. They’re creating simpler solutions that are more manageable for supervisors and team leaders. Furthermore, it’s always a plus when you can have all your tools available to you in one suite or interface. It’s clear that Cisco has put lots of work into making a seamless and smooth experience for the end-user to navigate. While there are some enhancements that we’re still looking forward to, ‘I’m personally pleased with the products and am very appreciative of Cisco’s willingness to take and action feedback of real users.”
> **— Chandler Fuller, Analyst, Customer Experience** , **Estes Express Lines**
## A Foundation for What’s Next
Together, these results show AI moving from trial into the daily workflow — expanding quality visibility, supporting agents, and turning insight into action. Through the collaboration of Estes, Cisco, and our partner ePlus, the team is building a disciplined learning loop around adoption, productivity, capacity, and customer outcomes.
For Estes and Cisco, the next chapter is to continue the transformation across AI QM, AI Assistant, and AI Agent and validate the outcomes that matter most: supervisor time redirected from manual assembly to coaching, AI and human evaluation alignment, repeat-contact and resolution improvements, forecasting accuracy, plus the real-world impact of routing, language translation, and AI memory. The destination is a contact center where every interaction can help improve the next customer’s experience.

On Aug 3, 2026Aug 3, 2026By [Amit Barave](https://blog.webex.com/contributors/amitbarave/)7 Min Read

![](https://blog.webex.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/gartner-hero.jpg)
I am incredibly excited and proud to announce that Gartner has recognized **Cisco as a Leader inthe **[**2026 Gartner Magic Quadrant for our Unified Communications as a Service (UCaaS)**](https://www.webex.com/us/en/gp/gartner-magic-quadrant-ucaas.html) for the 8th consecutive year! To us, this positioning continues to be the result of our unwavering vision and proven ability to execute. We are truly honored by this recognition.
Webex has always been about connection — people, devices, tools. This past year, we’ve taken that further: connecting people with AI, and AI with AI. This recognition reflects what drives us every day: meeting customers where they are with innovation, security, and reliability — so they can focus on the work that matters most.
I believe these three areas are core to our continued acknowledgement:
#### 1. Delivering mission critical, high trust collaboration solutions.
Offering advanced reliability and resiliency, flexible deployment options (on-premises, hybrid, cloud), data sovereignty, and complete control and governance over AI.
![](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns=%22http://www.w3.org/2000/svg%22%20viewBox=%220%200%201672%20830%22%3E%3C/svg%3E)
  * **Reliability and resiliency are foundational.** With a 99.999% Webex Calling SLA and enhanced Survivability for Calling and Meetings, the mission keeps running regardless of network state — ensuring that an essential set of collaboration capabilities remain available if connectivity to the cloud is disrupted.
  * **Sovereignty is prioritized by design,** baked into the architecture from the ground up. Your organization stays in full control of its data and encryption, backed by a global footprint of more than 110 data centers, with residency in the US, Canada, the EU, Australia, New Zealand and the Pacific Islands, Saudi Arabia, and India, with Japan and the UK expansion underway.
  * Every organization has different control requirements, and Webex is built to meet them. Keys can live fully on premises in your own data center, in the cloud with you retaining exclusive control over who can access them, or anywhere in between. However your organization needs to manage them, no one outside your organization holds the keys to your conversations.
  * **Security is the engine of our AI strategy**. Cisco governs intelligence by default at every layer. Our Zero Trust architecture extends across every surface where collaboration happens — meetings, messages, recordings, whiteboards — with end-to-end encryption that’s tamper-proof today and quantum-safe for tomorrow.
  * **AI that runs without ever touching the cloud.**[Cisco AI PODs for Collaboration](https://blog.webex.com/collaboration/cisco-ai-pods-collaboration-generally-available-delivering-secure-premises-ai/), now generally available, mean organizations can have AI-powered transcription and summarization running fully on-premises. Deployed against Cisco Meeting Server, AI PODs run on-premises or air-gapped with zero external connectivity. The data never leaves the defined boundary.
  * **Regulatory and corporate compliance requirements are met.** Webex Compliance Hub captures, retains, monitors, and governs communications across calling, meetings, messaging, and AI interactions. It simplifies eDiscovery, legal hold, compliance recording, and AI-powered surveillance, enabling organizations to reduce risk and respond more efficiently to audits and investigations.


#### 2. **Connected Intelligence Collaboration Platform**
We are seeing a fundamental shift in collaboration. AI is now embedded in every interaction — every call, every meeting, every chat. Webex Suite has evolved into a Connected Intelligence Platform, where Context, Conversations, and Outcomes are the engine driving workplace efficiency.
Today, context is continuous and everywhere. It doesn’t just flow across every interaction — it flows across People, Places, and Devices too. Every interaction makes the next one smarter. Webex is built on a platform that’s open, extensible, and AI-native — designed to support interoperability at scale while delivering the resiliency, security, flexibility, and observability our customers need.
We started with a standard AI Assistant to help users with daily tasks. In the past eleven months, we’ve seen a 9x increase in AI Assistant adoption in meetings. That tells us two things: it’s solving a real problem, and customers are building lasting habits around it.
We’ve also announced a suite of coordinated Productivity Agents that can handle routine work autonomously: capturing discussions, scheduling meetings, translating conversations in real time, and managing front-desk operations around the clock. Working in the background, these capabilities return time and attention to the work that actually matters.
Beyond productivity, we’re taking this further: Webex is transforming enterprise engagement by extending our comprehensive, secure AI Agent Studio across our entire Collaboration portfolio. The ability to build, deploy, and manage voice and digital multi-modal AI agents across any internal or external use case allows IT and admins to prioritize consistency.
And lastly, we have a robust Agentic Ecosystem of integration partners and MCPs, with integrations across Microsoft Copilot, Google Gemini, Anthropic Claude, Amazon Quick, and so much more — all governed through the same security architecture as every other Webex AI capability.
Taken together, this is what a Connected Intelligence Platform looks like in practice — not a single feature, but a system where context, agents, and partners work hand in hand to make every interaction better than the last.
![](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns=%22http://www.w3.org/2000/svg%22%20viewBox=%220%200%201672%20943%22%3E%3C/svg%3E)
#### 3. Delivering highly connected and unified management experiences across UCaaS, CCaaS, CPaaS, and Collaboration Hardware (Devices)
This approach delivers more innovative, faster, and more intuitive employee and customer experiences. From automating complex processes and enhancing customer journeys to providing actionable intelligence about Devices or office spaces, our unified innovations such as Cisco AI Assistant, and Collaboration Agents work effortlessly across hardware and software to deliver seamless experiences that don’t just help our customers innovate but make their lives and work easier.
**Everything comes together in Control Hub for IT**. Delivering exceptional user experiences is at the heart of what we do— but IT is the unsung hero who makes all of this possible in the real world. Webex is built to supercharge IT with [Collaboration Control Hub](https://www.webex.com/us/en/solutions/cross-platform/control-hub.html), a single-pane-of-glass experience for managing Suite, Calling, Contact Center, and Collaboration Devices and providing a comprehensive, platform-wide approach to security and sustainability.
![](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns=%22http://www.w3.org/2000/svg%22%20viewBox=%220%200%201672%201028%22%3E%3C/svg%3E)
This year, we announced [Cisco Cloud Control](https://cloud.cisco.com/), Cisco’s unified platform for administration and AgenticOps. This AI-native management platform reimagines how organizations manage, operate, and optimize their IT infrastructure and gives teams one destination to access their complete Cisco estate from a secure, single sign-on experience.
[Control Hub](https://www.webex.com/us/en/solutions/cross-platform/control-hub.html) is now part of the [Cisco Cloud Control](https://cloud.cisco.com/) experience. The powerful tools teams rely on to manage users, devices, workspaces, and collaboration services are now part of Cisco’s unified platform for AgenticOps, bringing collaboration management into a broader, AI-native operating experience.
#### 4. Unmatched Cloud Calling global availability.
Webex enterprise-grade cloud Calling delivers market-leading availability to over 19 million cloud calling users worldwide with three flexible options for PSTN connectivity and domestic calling in over 200 markets.
![](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns=%22http://www.w3.org/2000/svg%22%20viewBox=%220%200%201671%20751%22%3E%3C/svg%3E)
We continue to deliver enterprise calling and AI innovations to a [global customer base](https://www.webex.com/products/calling-global-availability.html), such as AI Receptionist, Cisco AI Assistant for Webex Calling, Collaboration Voice Agents and more.
Cisco also supports customers across both on-premises and cloud deployments, with [Cisco Unified Communications Manager](https://www.webex.com/us/en/products/suite/enterprise-cloud-calling/CUCM.html) serving more than 30 million users worldwide, including organizations in government, defense, critical infrastructure, retail, manufacturing, healthcare, and financial services.

It’s a depth of integration few UCaaS providers can match, because few build the room and the software running inside it.
