#### NovelVox Agent Accelerator for Automotive
Integration by [NovelVox NA Inc](https://www.novelvox.com/)
* * *
Govern and execute dealership sales and service workflows across CDK, Cox Automotive, and Reynolds & Reynolds — in real time, inside Webex Contact Center. 
[ Learn more ](https://www.novelvox.com/unified-agent-desktops/webex-automotive/)
Integration by [NovelVox NA Inc](https://www.novelvox.com/)
NovelVox Agent Accelerator is the execution layer between Webex Contact Center and your dealership systems of record, including CDK, Cox Automotive, and Reynolds & Reynolds. Agents execute complete sales and service workflows from one orchestrated workspace: 
  * confirm, reschedule, or cancel appointments with real-time DMS write-back 
  * validate warranty coverage and claims with complete audit trails 
  * run VIN-based recall outreach through approved workflows 
  * manage financing and trade-in inquiries synchronized with CRM systems


PCI-DSS-compliant payment capture automatically pauses recording during card entry. Every action is executed under role-based permissions to support GDPR and CCPA requirements. Every interaction completes — no dropped steps or manual workarounds.
Click "<https://www.novelvox.com/contact-us/>" to get started. Webex Contact Center license and a NovelVox account required. For pricing and subscription information, contact us at marketing@novelvox.com.
