Are you trying to say that your colleague installed a Linux XCP OS on the CUCM?
It’s essential to understand that ‘Cisco Business Edition 6000’ is not the name of a standalone software. These are tailored solutions based on user count. Both BE6000 and BE7000 employ the same suite of applications, with the maximum device capacity differing according to the chosen solution.
CUCM: Cisco Unified Communications Manager for phone registration  
CUC: Cisco Unity Connection for voicemail  
IMP: Instant Messaging and Presence
  
Additionally, other applications can co-reside with the ones mentioned above.
Please note, it’s not feasible to install two operating systems on the CUCM, similar to how a PC operates
