VRConnect for Webex is a truly one-of-a-kind, purpose-built solution that provides a comprehensive and immersive virtual event experience. The solution covers the user journeys from the Conference Center auditoriums to the Exhibition Floor Booths to Social Lounges, Private Meetings Room and Offices.
A 500 person internal training event or a 50,000 Annual Tradeshow it makes no difference, its modularity allows for continuous extensions and customizations that fit extremely well the nature and dynamics of the virtual event and education world. 
The solution can be used on desktop, mobile and tablets with no special hardware requirement.
