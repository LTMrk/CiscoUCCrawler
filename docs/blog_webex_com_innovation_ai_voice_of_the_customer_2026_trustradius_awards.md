At Cisco, we talk a lot about customer success. But the true measure of our product’s value comes from the people who use our technology every day to connect teams, engage customers, and drive their businesses forward.
That’s why we are proud to announce that we have been honored with [**2026 TrustRadius Top Rated Awards**](https://solutions.trustradius.com/vendor-blog/2026-top-rated-award-winners/) for [Webex Suite](https://www.webex.com/suite/collaboration-suite.html), [Webex Customer Experience](https://www.webex.com/us/en/products/customer-experience.html) solutions, and [Cisco Collaboration Devices](https://www.webex.com/us/en/devices.html).
These awards are especially meaningful because they’re based entirely on feedback from verified users. TrustRadius, a buyer intelligence platform acquired by HG Insights in 2025, has established itself as one of the most trusted sources for independent B2B technology reviews. Its Top Rated Awards reflect the experiences and outcomes customers achieve with the technologies they use every day.

## How the Scoring Works
TrustRadius has awarded Top Rated distinctions since 2016, selecting winners based on customer sentiment, satisfaction, and market relevance. You can explore the detailed [TrustRadius scoring methodology and criteria](https://www.trustradius.com/static/about-trustradius-scoring) to learn how these transparent, unbiased winners are determined.
## Why this Customer Recognition Matters
Awards are meaningful, but customer trust matters even more.
Because these accolades are driven entirely by feedback from verified users, this recognition represents something especially valuable: the voice of our customers. Your experiences, insights, and recommendations help shape our innovation and guide the evolution of our portfolio.
Being recognized across 14 categories—from UCaaS and Collaboration to Video Conferencing and Employee Engagement—underscores the impact Cisco collaboration solutions are having for organizations around the world. Whether enabling hybrid work, improving customer engagement, or delivering more immersive meeting experiences, we remain committed to helping teams do their best work.
To everyone who took the time to log onto TrustRadius, answer those detailed questions, and share your experiences—thank you. Your insights don’t just help other businesses make informed technology decisions; they directly shape our product roadmap. We read your reviews, we listen to your challenges, and we use your feedback to continuously improve.
