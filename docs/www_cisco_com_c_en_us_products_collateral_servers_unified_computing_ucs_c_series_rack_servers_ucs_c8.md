Product Migration Options
As part of our product lifecycle management, the NVIDIA H200 and AMD MI300X GPU-based offerings are being transitioned out of the portfolio. Customers evaluating or currently planning deployments with these platforms are encouraged to consider the latest generation of accelerated computing solutions designed to deliver enhanced AI performance, scalability, and efficiency. 
Customers currently evaluating NVIDIA H200 or AMD MI300X based deployments should consider the following next-generation GPU platforms based on workload requirements, architectural preferences, and commercial considerations: 
●**UCS C880A M8 with NVIDIA HGX Blackwell Ultra B300 GPUs** , delivering significant advancements in AI training and inference performance, memory bandwidth, and scalability for large-scale accelerated computing environments. 
●**UCS C885A M8 with AMD Instinct MI350X GPUs** , offering enhanced compute density, improved performance per watt, and strong capabilities for modern AI, HPC and data-intensive workloads. 
These next-generation platforms provide customers with flexible upgrade options while enabling adoption of the latest innovations in GPU architecture and accelerated infrastructure design. 
For detailed specifications and architectural considerations, please refer to the latest product datasheets: 
●UCS C880A M8 with NVIDIA HGX Blackwell Ultra B300 GPUs
●UCS C885A M8 with AMD Instinct MI350X GPUs
