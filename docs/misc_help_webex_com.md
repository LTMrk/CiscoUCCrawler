Webex Instant Connect
![list-menu](https://help.webex.com/images/feedback.svg)Feedback?
Webex Instant Connect allows healthcare staff and patients to connect using simple, easy-to-use video telehealth consultations, scheduled directly from their Electronic Medical Records (EMR) portal. These browser-based consultations are highly secure, and no private health information is collected at any point. There is nothing for patients to install nor are there any login details to remember: the patient simply clicks the provided link. As a fully flexible platform, Webex Instant Connect now powers other healthcare use cases as well, such as in-patient teleconsultation, hourly virtual rounding, family visitation, and others. The general flow of Instant Connect can be used for any B2C meetings across industries like financial services, retail, real estate, government and much more. 
![](https://cisco-api.ingeniuxondemand.com/DITA/content/en/us/td/i/400001-500000/460001-470000/464001-465000/464513.jpg)
Patients are able to view their upcoming appointments in their EMR portal and click on a link that starts their browser-based telehealth visit. While they’re waiting for the healthcare professional to join, patients can test their video, audio and speaker hardware. The healthcare professional also just needs to click the link provided, and they are instantly connected with the patient.
Visit [instant.webex.com](https://instant.webex.com/) to get started with Webex Instant Connect today!
[Webex Virtual Rounding](https://help.webex.com/en-us/article/sv0h2ab/Webex-Instant-Connect#reference-template_a7caa81a-f172-448d-82c7-323b4a91df98)
The Webex Virtual Rounding solution builds on the flexibility of Webex Instant Connect to drive hourly rounding and in-patient virtual consultation use cases. Hourly rounding patients can be scheduled directly in the Electronic Medical Records (EMR) portal, and the provider just needs to go to the EMR portal’s patient context to launch the visit. The Cisco device facing the patient will join the call directly and the providers will be able to control the device’s camera, thus, simulating a physical visit.
**Advantages**
  1. Providers can join from anywhere using their laptop/mobile.
  2. Through Far End Camera Control (FECC), the providers can maneuver the patient’s device camera so that they can focus on the right area for diagnosis, essentially simulating a physical visit.
  3. Reduces Provider burden and increases overall efficiency resulting in millions of dollars of operational cost savings.


**Things to Note**
  1. We currently provide native integration with Epic EMR for this capability, but any EMR or healthcare system can integrate with Webex Virtual Rounding to unlock this workflow.
  2. The patient-to-device mapping must be maintained by the EMR or healthcare system.
  3. We currently support a no-knock scenario where the patient is notified on device that they will join the call in 15 seconds.
  4. Devices can be configured for this workflow by following the steps mentioned [here](https://help.webex.com/en-us/article/6vsdoi/Configure-Virtual-Rounding).
  5. FECC and the supported devices list can be found [here](https://help.webex.com/en-us/article/nuwutmx/Control-remote-cameras-with-Instant-Connect).

[Instant Connect Integration](https://help.webex.com/en-us/article/sv0h2ab/Webex-Instant-Connect#reference-template_2e099afb-818c-4283-b4ae-f15bb985470b)
See the following sections to get started integrating Instant Connect with your own system. If you encounter any issues, please contact your Cisco AM.
### Integrate with Epic for Telehealth
To integrate Instant Connect with Epic for Telehealth:
  1. Go to [https://instant.webex.com](https://instant.webex.com/) and select the **Epic Integration Page**.
  2. Select **Begin Integration with Epic** and then login as Webex administrator for your organization.
  3. Select **Generate Secret Key** , and save the **Secret Key** , **URL** , and **Parameters** entries to a temporary location.
  4. Login to your Epic environment and paste the secret key into the **CryptKey** field, the URL into the **CryptURL** field, and the parameters into the **PatientOpenURL** field in the Epic configuration page within your FDI records for each Epic application you intend to use.
  5. Create a **Device Integration** for each Epic application you intend to use.
  6. Create an **Appointment Type** within Epic.
  7. Make sure access to the domain, `instant.webex.com,` is enabled in your Epic configuration.


This is a one-time configuration process.
### Integrate with Epic for Virtual Rounding (device integration)
To integrate Instant Connect with Epic for Virtual Rounding (device integration):
  1. Go to [https://instant.webex.com](https://instant.webex.com/) and select the **Epic Integration Page**.
  2. Select **Begin Integration with Epic** and then login as Webex administrator for your organization.
  3. Select **Generate Secret Key** , and save the **Secret Key** , **URL** , entries to a temporary location. 
The Provider Parameters entry for this integration is: `visitID=%CSN%&userType=1&userID=%EPICUSERID;;; ;                 ;;NONE;%&flow={"id":"sip-no-knock","data":[{"uri":%EXTENSION;101336%}]}` where `%EXTENSION;101336%` is the parameter which contains SIP address of the Webex Device that is in front of the patient.
  4. Login to your Epic environment and paste the secret key into the **CryptKey** field, the URL into the **CryptURL** field, and the parameters into the **PatientOpenURL** field in the Epic configuration page within your FDI records for each Epic application you intend to use. You’ll only need to configure the **Provider Record** in this case.
  5. Create a **Device Integration** for each Epic application you intend to use.
  6. Create an **Appointment Type** within Epic.
  7. Make sure access to the domain, `instant.webex.com,` is enabled in your Epic configuration.
  8. A Webex administrator from your customer organization needs to set up Webex Devices in Control Hub to work with this flow. See [Configure Virtual Rounding](https://help.webex.com/en-us/article/6vsdoi/Configure-Virtual-Rounding).
  9. Most new Webex devices also support Far End Camera Control with this workflow. Based on your devices and your Room OS version, some configurations might need to be updated in Control Hub. See [Control remote cameras with Instant Connect](https://help.webex.com/en-us/article/nuwutmx/Control-remote-cameras-with-Instant-Connect).


This is a one-time configuration process.
### Integrate other systems with Instant Connect
To integrate Instant Connect with other systems:
  1. Go to [https://instant.webex.com](https://instant.webex.com/).
  2. Select **Begin Integration** and then login as Webex administrator for your organization.
  3. Select **Activate Instant Connect**.
  4. Go to this Webex Developer Portal [link](https://developer.webex.com/docs/instant-connect-meeting-links) and follow the steps to integrate Webex Instant Connect with your EHR or any other clinical healthcare system. 
You’ll need to add `verticalType=”gen”` (for generic) in the API for non-healthcare workflows. The default vertical is `hc` (healthcare).
  5. Once complete, your customers can leverage Webex Instant Connect links constructed by the endpoint in any way they desire.
  6. Make sure access to the domain, `instant.webex.com,` is enabled to avoid firewall issues.


This is a one-time configuration process.
### Integrate other systems with the Instant Connect device integration workflow
To integrate other systems with the Instant Connect device integration workflow:
  1. Go to [https://instant.webex.com](https://instant.webex.com/).
  2. Select **Begin Integration** and then login as Webex administrator for your organization.
  3. Select **Activate Instant Connect**.
  4. Go to this Webex Developer Portal [link](https://developer.webex.com/docs/instant-connect-meeting-links) and follow the steps to integrate Webex Instant Connect with your EHR or any other clinical healthcare system.
  5. Follow the section **Complete Sample JSON Request Payload Including Device Support** to understand parameters required for integrating Webex Device with your workflow. 
**NOTE** : You’ll need to add `verticalType=”gen”` (for generic) in the API for non-healthcare workflows. The default vertical is `hc` (healthcare).
  6. A Webex administrator from your customer organization needs to set up Webex Devices in Control Hub to work with this flow. See [Configure Virtual Rounding](https://help.webex.com/en-us/article/6vsdoi/Configure-Virtual-Rounding).
  7. Most new Webex devices also support Far End Camera Control with this workflow. Based on your devices and your Room OS version, some configurations might need to be updated in Control Hub. See [Control remote cameras with Instant Connect](https://help.webex.com/en-us/article/nuwutmx/Control-remote-cameras-with-Instant-Connect).
  8. Once complete, your customers can leverage Webex Instant Connect links constructed by the endpoint in any way they desire.
  9. Make sure access to the domain, `instant.webex.com,` is enabled to avoid firewall issues.


This is a one-time configuration process.

# Sign-in issues with Webex
![list-menu](https://help.webex.com/images/feedback.svg)Feedback?
Having trouble signing into Webex Meetings or Webex App? Here's your one-stop shop for the help you need to get up and running. We've included some links that can help you access the app or figure out why you can't. 
If you're having issues signing in to Webex either through your desktop, mobile device, or through your browser, there may be issues with your password or the email address registered with Webex. Try the following troubleshooting steps to address some of the more common sign-in issues. You can also look through any of the articles below for help with more specific issues.
**Reset your password.**
Go to [https://web.webex.com](https://web.webex.com/), enter your email address, click Next, and select Forgot password?. You receive an email with a Reset Password link. Follow the instructions to reset your password.
**Check your spam folder for a verification email during sign-in.**
You need to verify your email address when you're added to your Webex organization. If you don't receive a verification email, confirm that messenger@webex.com is allowed in your inbox and check your spam folder if you still can't find the email.
**Use a different sign-in method**
It's possible your administrator has disabled email sign in and are enforcing a single sign-on method. Enter your company email address and click Next.
You’ll be directed to your single sign-on provider to sign in. After you sign in, you'll be redirected to Webex.

# Configure Virtual Rounding
![list-menu](https://help.webex.com/images/feedback.svg)Feedback?
You can integrate your Webex Devices with Instant Connect by configuring Virtual Rounding in Webex Control Hub. 
To configure Virtual Rounding:  
| 1  |  Make sure that you’ve activated Instant Connect by completing one of the following: 
  * Clicking on **Activate Instant Connect** in <https://instant.webex.com/integrate>.
  * Clicking on **Generate Secret Key** in <https://instant.webex.com/virtualcare/epic/integrate>.

 |  
| --- | --- |  
| 2  |  Login to your administrator account on [https://admin.webex.com](https://admin.webex.com/), and choose **Workspaces** from the left-hand nav. Select **Add workspace**.  If you don’t have any existing spaces, the **Add workspace** button appears in the middle of the page.  |  
| 3  |  Fill in the relevant information for the new workspace. We recommend adding _Location_ to the mandatory workspace **Name** entry. Select **Next**.  |  
| 4  |  Select **Cisco Collaboration Device** and then select **Next**.  |  
| 5  |  Configure the required services for your new workspace and then select **Next**.  |  
| 6  |  You’ll see a 16-digit activation code which you can use to register your Webex device with your new workspace. Once you’ve made a note of the activation code (there are several options for retrieving the code at the bottom of the dialog when you scroll down), select **Go to workspace**.  |  
| 7  |  In the workspace **Overview** tab, select **Edit API access**.  |  
| 8  |  Select **Add user or bot**.  |  
| 9  |  In the **Select a user or bot** drop down, search for _Cisco Meeting Broker Call Enabler Bot_ and select _Full Access_ in the **Access** level dropdown. Select **Close**.  If you can’t find the _Cisco Meeting Broker Call Enabler Bot_ , search instead for _Ciso Meeting Broker Call Enabler Bot_.  |  
| 10  |  Repeat those steps for any other workspaces and devices as required.  |  
What to do next
Once you’ve configured Virtual Rounding for your workspaces you can direct developers to [Instant Connect Meeting Links](https://developer.webex.com/docs/instant-connect-meeting-links) which explains how to generate Instant Connect Meeting links for hosts and guests.

# Configure Virtual Rounding
![list-menu](https://help.webex.com/images/feedback.svg)Feedback?
You can integrate your Webex Devices with Instant Connect by configuring Virtual Rounding in Webex Control Hub. 
To configure Virtual Rounding:  
| 1  |  Make sure that you’ve activated Instant Connect by completing one of the following: 
  * Clicking on **Activate Instant Connect** in <https://instant.webex.com/integrate>.
  * Clicking on **Generate Secret Key** in <https://instant.webex.com/virtualcare/epic/integrate>.

 |  
| --- | --- |  
| 2  |  Login to your administrator account on [https://admin.webex.com](https://admin.webex.com/), and choose **Workspaces** from the left-hand nav. Select **Add workspace**.  If you don’t have any existing spaces, the **Add workspace** button appears in the middle of the page.  |  
| 3  |  Fill in the relevant information for the new workspace. We recommend adding _Location_ to the mandatory workspace **Name** entry. Select **Next**.  |  
| 4  |  Select **Cisco Collaboration Device** and then select **Next**.  |  
| 5  |  Configure the required services for your new workspace and then select **Next**.  |  
| 6  |  You’ll see a 16-digit activation code which you can use to register your Webex device with your new workspace. Once you’ve made a note of the activation code (there are several options for retrieving the code at the bottom of the dialog when you scroll down), select **Go to workspace**.  |  
| 7  |  In the workspace **Overview** tab, select **Edit API access**.  |  
| 8  |  Select **Add user or bot**.  |  
| 9  |  In the **Select a user or bot** drop down, search for _Cisco Meeting Broker Call Enabler Bot_ and select _Full Access_ in the **Access** level dropdown. Select **Close**.  If you can’t find the _Cisco Meeting Broker Call Enabler Bot_ , search instead for _Ciso Meeting Broker Call Enabler Bot_.  |  
| 10  |  Repeat those steps for any other workspaces and devices as required.  |  
What to do next
Once you’ve configured Virtual Rounding for your workspaces you can direct developers to [Instant Connect Meeting Links](https://developer.webex.com/docs/instant-connect-meeting-links) which explains how to generate Instant Connect Meeting links for hosts and guests.
