* * *
MoAI-Note Transcription delivers AI-powered real-time meeting captions for Webex using advanced Speech-to-Text (STT) technology.
[ Learn more ](https://apphub.webex.com/applications/moai-note-ai-transcription-furence-12414)
Embedded App by [Furence](https://furence.com/)
MoAI-Note Transcription provides real-time meeting captions optimized for the Webex environment using advanced Speech-to-Text (STT) technology.
### Account Requirements
To use this app, you need a paid Webex license and a MoAI account
### Create a MoAI account
Contact us at help@moai-note.ai. Our team will assist with account creation and setup.
### Pricing
Free to use, including up to 300 transcription minutes per month.
### How to create MoAI account
Contact us at help@moai-note.ai and our sales team will work with you to create your account. 
### How to Use
  1. Start or join a meeting as the host.
  2. Click Apps at the bottom of the meeting window and launch MoAI-Note AI Transcription.
  3. Click the Start button to launch the bot.
  4. Admit the bot to the meeting when prompted.
  5. Once the bot joins the meeting, real-time transcription and translation will begin automatically.
