Overview
EOL13457 – Amended
Cisco announces the end-of-sale and end-of life dates for the Cisco Unified Contact Center Express 12.0. The last day to order the affected product(s) is August 1, 2020. Customers with active service contracts will continue to receive support from the Cisco Technical Assistance Center (TAC) as shown in Table 1 of the EoL bulletin. Table 1 describes the end-of-life milestones, definitions, and dates for the affected product(s). Table 2 lists the product part numbers affected by this announcement. For customers with active and paid service and support contracts, support will be available under the terms and conditions of customers' service contract. 12.0 Customers can take advantage of new capabilities found in Cisco Unified Contact Center Express 12.5.
Existing 12.0 customers can take advantage of new capabilities found in the replacement product, Cisco Unified Contact Center Express 12.5. Customers are also encouraged to migrate to subsequent versions of the product using Cisco Collaboration Flex Plan Contact Center (A-FLEX-CC). The link to the Cisco Collaboration Flex Plan Contact Center Ordering Guide is provided below.  
<https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/products/collateral/customer-collaboration/guide-c07-741219.pdf>.
Alternatively upgrades to release 12.5 are available using Software Support Subscription (SWSS) contract.
