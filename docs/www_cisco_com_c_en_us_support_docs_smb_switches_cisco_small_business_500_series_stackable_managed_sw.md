SG500XG-8F8T-K9-NA: Lots of Dropped Packets
## Date Identified
June 15, 2017
## Date Resolved
July 14, 2017
## Products Affected  
|  **Model**  |  **Firmware Version**  |  
| --- | --- |  
|  SG500XG-8F8T-K9-NA  |  1.4.7.06  |  
## Problem Description
There are lots of dropped events on the RMON statistics of interfaces in an SG500XG where a UCS220 device is connected.
To view the port statistics if there are dropped packets, follow the steps below:
Step 1. Log in to the switch web-based utility and go to **Status and Statistics**** > ****RMON**** > ****Statistics**.
[![](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/smb/switches/cisco-small-business-500-series-stackable-managed-switches/images/rjs-01102018-droppedpackets-step1.png)](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/smb/switches/cisco-small-business-500-series-stackable-managed-switches/images/rjs-01102018-droppedpackets-step1.png "Related image, diagram or screenshot.")
Step 2. In the Interface section, click on the Unit/Slot drop-down menu to choose the specific unit if your switch belongs to a stack.
[![](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/smb/switches/cisco-small-business-500-series-stackable-managed-switches/images/rjs-01102018-droppedpackets-step2.png)](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/smb/switches/cisco-small-business-500-series-stackable-managed-switches/images/rjs-01102018-droppedpackets-step2.png "Related image, diagram or screenshot.")
**Note:** In this example, 1/1 is chosen indicating that the switch is the first unit in the stack and is on the first slot.
Step 3. Click the Port drop-down menu to choose the specific port that you want to view.
[![](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/smb/switches/cisco-small-business-500-series-stackable-managed-switches/images/rjs-01102018-droppedpackets-step3.png)](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/smb/switches/cisco-small-business-500-series-stackable-managed-switches/images/rjs-01102018-droppedpackets-step3.png "Related image, diagram or screenshot.")
**Note:** In this example, Port GE3 is chosen.
Step 4. (Optional) Click a radio button to choose the Refresh Rate. This would allow the page to refresh automatically based on the interval you have set.
[![](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/smb/switches/cisco-small-business-500-series-stackable-managed-switches/images/rjs-01102018-droppedpackets-step4.png)](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/smb/switches/cisco-small-business-500-series-stackable-managed-switches/images/rjs-01102018-droppedpackets-step4.png "Related image, diagram or screenshot.")
**Note:** In this example, 15 sec is chosen indicating that the page will refresh automatically every 15 seconds.
Step 5. Check the statistics displayed to verify if there are dropped events in the chosen interface.
[![](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/smb/switches/cisco-small-business-500-series-stackable-managed-switches/images/rjs-01102018-droppedpackets-step5.png)](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/smb/switches/cisco-small-business-500-series-stackable-managed-switches/images/rjs-01102018-droppedpackets-step5.png "Related image, diagram or screenshot.")
**Note:** In this example, the statistics show that there are 595 events dropped.
## Next Steps
Do a packet capture on the problematic port using Wireshark. Follow the steps below:
**Enable Auto Negotiation**
Enabling Auto negotiation allows the port to advertise its transmission speed, duplex mode, and Flow control abilities to the port link partner.
Step 1. Log in to the switch web-based utility and go to **Port Management > Port Settings**.
[![](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/smb/switches/cisco-small-business-500-series-stackable-managed-switches/images/rjs-01102018-droppedpacketsnextsteps-step1.png)](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/smb/switches/cisco-small-business-500-series-stackable-managed-switches/images/rjs-01102018-droppedpacketsnextsteps-step1.png "Related image, diagram or screenshot.")
Step 2. Under the Port Setting Table, click on the radio button of the port where dropped events are found and click **Edit**.
[![](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/smb/switches/cisco-small-business-500-series-stackable-managed-switches/images/rjs-01102018-droppedpacketsnextsteps-step2.png)](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/smb/switches/cisco-small-business-500-series-stackable-managed-switches/images/rjs-01102018-droppedpacketsnextsteps-step2.png "Related image, diagram or screenshot.")
**Note****:** In this example, Port GE3 is chosen.
Step 3. In the Edit Port Setting window, check the **Enable** checkbox for Auto Negotiation then click **Apply**.
[![](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/smb/switches/cisco-small-business-500-series-stackable-managed-switches/images/rjs-01102018-droppedpacketsnextsteps-step3.png)](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/smb/switches/cisco-small-business-500-series-stackable-managed-switches/images/rjs-01102018-droppedpacketsnextsteps-step3.png "Related image, diagram or screenshot.")
**Enable Log Settings**
Step 1. Go to**Administration > System Log > Log Settings.**
[![](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/smb/switches/cisco-small-business-500-series-stackable-managed-switches/images/rjs-01102018-droppedpacketsenablelogs-step1.png)](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/smb/switches/cisco-small-business-500-series-stackable-managed-switches/images/rjs-01102018-droppedpacketsenablelogs-step1.png "Related image, diagram or screenshot.")
Step 2. Under Log Settings, check the Logging**Enable** check box**.**
[![](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/smb/switches/cisco-small-business-500-series-stackable-managed-switches/images/rjs-01102018-droppedpacketsenablelogs-step2.png)](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/smb/switches/cisco-small-business-500-series-stackable-managed-switches/images/rjs-01102018-droppedpacketsenablelogs-step2.png "Related image, diagram or screenshot.")
Step 3. Set the Originator Identifier to None by clicking on the radio button.
[![](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/smb/switches/cisco-small-business-500-series-stackable-managed-switches/images/rjs-01102018-droppedpacketsenablelogs-step3.png)](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/smb/switches/cisco-small-business-500-series-stackable-managed-switches/images/rjs-01102018-droppedpacketsenablelogs-step3.png "Related image, diagram or screenshot.")
Step 4. Under RAM and Flash Memory Logging, check all the check boxes except for Debug and then click **Apply**.
[![](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/smb/switches/cisco-small-business-500-series-stackable-managed-switches/images/rjs-01102018-droppedpacketsenablelogs-step4.png)](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/smb/switches/cisco-small-business-500-series-stackable-managed-switches/images/rjs-01102018-droppedpacketsenablelogs-step4.png "Related image, diagram or screenshot.")
**Clear Logs on the RAM and Flash**
Step 1. Go to**Status and Statistics > View Log > RAM Memory.**
[![](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/smb/switches/cisco-small-business-500-series-stackable-managed-switches/images/rjs-01102018-droppedpacketsclearlogs-step1.png)](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/smb/switches/cisco-small-business-500-series-stackable-managed-switches/images/rjs-01102018-droppedpacketsclearlogs-step1.png "Related image, diagram or screenshot.")
Step 2. Click the**Clear Logs** button under the RAM Memory Log Table.
[![](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/smb/switches/cisco-small-business-500-series-stackable-managed-switches/images/rjs-01102018-droppedpacketsclearlogs-step2.png)](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/smb/switches/cisco-small-business-500-series-stackable-managed-switches/images/rjs-01102018-droppedpacketsclearlogs-step2.png "Related image, diagram or screenshot.")
Step 3. Go to **Flash Memory**.
[![](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/smb/switches/cisco-small-business-500-series-stackable-managed-switches/images/rjs-01102018-droppedpacketsclearlogs-step3.png)](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/smb/switches/cisco-small-business-500-series-stackable-managed-switches/images/rjs-01102018-droppedpacketsclearlogs-step3.png "Related image, diagram or screenshot.")
Step 4. Click the**Clear Logs** button under the Flash Memory Log Table.
[![](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/smb/switches/cisco-small-business-500-series-stackable-managed-switches/images/rjs-01102018-droppedpacketsclearlogs-step4.png)](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/smb/switches/cisco-small-business-500-series-stackable-managed-switches/images/rjs-01102018-droppedpacketsclearlogs-step4.png "Related image, diagram or screenshot.")
**Add****Port****and VLAN Mirroring**
Step 1. Go to**Administration > Diagnostics > Port and VLAN Mirroring**.
[![](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/smb/switches/cisco-small-business-500-series-stackable-managed-switches/images/rjs-01102018-droppedpacketsaddport-step1.png)](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/smb/switches/cisco-small-business-500-series-stackable-managed-switches/images/rjs-01102018-droppedpacketsaddport-step1.png "Related image, diagram or screenshot.")
Step 2. Under the Port and VLAN Mirroring Table, click on **Add**.
[![](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/smb/switches/cisco-small-business-500-series-stackable-managed-switches/images/rjs-01102018-droppedpacketsaddport-step2.png)](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/smb/switches/cisco-small-business-500-series-stackable-managed-switches/images/rjs-01102018-droppedpacketsaddport-step2.png "Related image, diagram or screenshot.")
Step 3. In the Add Port and VLAN Mirroring window, click on the Destination Port drop-down menu to choose the port where the computer that is running Wireshark is connected.
[![](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/smb/switches/cisco-small-business-500-series-stackable-managed-switches/images/rjs-01102018-droppedpacketsaddport-step3.png)](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/smb/switches/cisco-small-business-500-series-stackable-managed-switches/images/rjs-01102018-droppedpacketsaddport-step3.png "Related image, diagram or screenshot.")
**Note:** In this example, Port GE1 is chosen.
Step 4. Click on the Source Port drop-down menu to choose the port where the dropped events are found.
[![](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/smb/switches/cisco-small-business-500-series-stackable-managed-switches/images/rjs-01102018-droppedpacketsaddport-step4.png)](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/smb/switches/cisco-small-business-500-series-stackable-managed-switches/images/rjs-01102018-droppedpacketsaddport-step4.png "Related image, diagram or screenshot.")
**Note:** In this example, Port GE3 is chosen.
Step 5. Click on the **Tx and Rx** radio button to choose the Type and then click **Apply**.
[![](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/smb/switches/cisco-small-business-500-series-stackable-managed-switches/images/rjs-01102018-droppedpacketsaddport-step5.png)](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/smb/switches/cisco-small-business-500-series-stackable-managed-switches/images/rjs-01102018-droppedpacketsaddport-step5.png "Related image, diagram or screenshot.")
Step 6. Launch the capture on the computer running Wireshark.
## Resolution
Enable Flow Control. To do this, follow the steps below:
Step 1. Log in to the switch web-based utility and go to **Port Management > Port Settings**.
[![](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/smb/switches/cisco-small-business-500-series-stackable-managed-switches/images/rjs-01102018-droppedpacketsreso-step1.png)](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/smb/switches/cisco-small-business-500-series-stackable-managed-switches/images/rjs-01102018-droppedpacketsreso-step1.png "Related image, diagram or screenshot.")
Step 2. Under the Port Setting Table, click on the radio button of the port where dropped events are found and click **Edit**.
[![](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/smb/switches/cisco-small-business-500-series-stackable-managed-switches/images/rjs-01102018-droppedpacketsreso-step2.png)](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/smb/switches/cisco-small-business-500-series-stackable-managed-switches/images/rjs-01102018-droppedpacketsreso-step2.png "Related image, diagram or screenshot.")
**Note****:** In this example, Port GE3 is chosen.
Step 3. In the Edit Port Setting window, check the **Enable** checkbox for Flow Control then click **Apply**.
[![](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/smb/switches/cisco-small-business-500-series-stackable-managed-switches/images/rjs-01102018-droppedpacketsreso-step3.png)](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/smb/switches/cisco-small-business-500-series-stackable-managed-switches/images/rjs-01102018-droppedpacketsreso-step3.png "Related image, diagram or screenshot.")
Step 4. Click the blinking [![](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/smb/switches/cisco-small-business-500-series-stackable-managed-switches/images/rjs-01102018-droppedpacketsreso-step4.png)](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/smb/switches/cisco-small-business-500-series-stackable-managed-switches/images/rjs-01102018-droppedpacketsreso-step4.png "Related image, diagram or screenshot.") button to permanently save the settings.

The information does not usually directly identify you, but it can give you a more personalized web experience. Because we respect your right to privacy, you can choose not to allow some types of cookies. From the list on left, please choose whether this site may use Performance and/or Targeting Cookies. By selecting Strictly Necessary Cookies only, you are requesting Cisco not to sell or share your personal data. Note, blocking some types of cookies may impact your experience on the site and the services we are able to offer.
#### Strictly Necessary Cookies
Always Active
These cookies are necessary for the website to function and cannot be switched off in our systems. They are usually only set in response to actions made by you which amount to a request for services, such as setting your privacy preferences, logging in or filling in forms. You can set your browser to block or alert you about these cookies, but some parts of the site will not then work. These cookies do not store any personally identifiable information.

#### Performance Cookies
Performance Cookies
These cookies provide metrics related to the performance and usability of our site. They are primarily focused on gathering information about how you interact with our site, including: page load times, response times, error messages, and allowing a replay of a visitor’s interactions with our site, which enables us to review and analyze visitor behavior, helping to improve site usability and functionality. These cookies also allow us to count visits and traffic sources so we can measure and improve the performance of our site. They help us to know which pages are the most and least popular and see how visitors move around the site. If you do not allow these cookies we will not know when you have visited our site and will not be able to monitor its performance.

#### Targeting Cookies
Targeting Cookies
These cookies may be set through our site by our advertising partners. They may be used by those companies to build a profile of your interests and show you relevant adverts on other sites. They do not store directly personal information, but are based on uniquely identifying your browser and internet device. If you do not allow these cookies, you will experience less targeted advertising.
