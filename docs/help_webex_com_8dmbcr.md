### February 11, 2026
### Webex Expands Data Residency Capabilities in Australia
Webex data residency in Australia is now live. Previously Webex Calling services were already hosted in Sydney and Melbourne data centers , and Webex meetings & Webinar primary data center was hosted in Sydney. With this latest expansion, we have also established backup data center for Webex Meetings and Webinars in Melbourne.
In addition, core Webex services including Messaging, Polling & Q&A (Slido), Translation & Transcription, Identity, Analytics, Key Management, AI Assistant, Logs and more, are now also data resident within Australia. This expansion supports our commitment to providing customers with secure, reliable, and locally hosted collaboration experiences. For more information, see [Data residency in Webex](https://help.webex.com/en-us/article/oybc4fb/)
