## Find partners with proven expertise
The new designations showcase where a partner has invested their time, training, and talent, so you can match a partner to your business needs.
### Global Partners
If you are looking to work across multiple countries, a Global Partner has the experience and purchasing power to support you in every region. They buy directly and deliver consistently, so you can get the same level of partnership in every location you operate in.
### Specializations
If you need deep expertise in Secure AI Infrastructure or Secure Networking, find a partner who has achieved one of these specializations. Specializations are earned by Preferred partners who pass a rigorous, independent audit proving their advanced expertise in certain portfolios.
### Cisco Preferred Partners
If you need advanced technical and sales expertise combined with full lifecycle management, choose a Preferred partner. These partners have made the deepest investments in their Cisco practices and are uniquely equipped to deliver tailored outcomes.
### Cisco Portfolio Partners
If you need reliable execution in specific solution areas like Networking and Security, look for a Portfolio Partner. They deliver validated expertise backed by proven sales, technical expertise, and practice maturity.
### Competencies
If you're looking for a partner with a unique skill or expertise in a specific domain or solution, find a partner who has earned a Competency. Competencies showcase focused capability but don't require the same depth of expertise as specializations.
### Cisco Powered Services
If you're looking for ongoing managed or lifecycle services, find a partner offering Cisco Powered Services. These partners have proven their ability to design, provision, manage, and run services across the full lifecycle on Cisco technology.
