# Let the Sun Shine on Your Meetings
Sunshine on Webex provides real-time insights into meeting dynamics, helping teams collaborate more effectively.
During every meeting, Sunshine reveals:
  * Team productivity and creativity.
  * How team dynamics evolve.
  * Who drives the conversation and who may need more engagement.


Insights and recommendations are based on scientific research.
## Getting Started
  1. Your Webex administrator installs the Sunshine Service App through Webex Control Hub.
  2. Activate **Webex Personally Identifiable Information (PII)** for your organization, as Sunshine requires participant information to provide insights.
  3. Open the **Apps** panel during a Webex meeting to access Sunshine.


## Pricing and Prerequisites
A valid Webex license and activated Webex PII are required.
Sunshine on Webex is currently **free of charge**. No additional subscriptions are required.
