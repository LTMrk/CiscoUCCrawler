Cisco Native WEM  
Solutions Plus+ (CalabrioOne)  
Other Solutions Plus+ Vendors  
From what I've heard (haven't worked on it directly), the middle option is the easiest/most straight forward as far as your previous configuration/data being maintained.  
It sounds like you're going with option 1, though, which I believe will be more involved as far as trying to migrate over your information, etc.
