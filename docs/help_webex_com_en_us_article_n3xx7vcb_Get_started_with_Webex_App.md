# Get started with Webex App
![list-menu](https://help.webex.com/images/feedback.svg)Feedback?
In real-time or anytime, Webex App is engaging, intelligent and inclusive and helps you create exceptional experiences and makes working together distinctly better. 
  * [Welcome to Webex App](https://help.webex.com/en-us/article/n3xx7vcb/Get-started-with-Webex-App#Cisco_Reference.dita_785e38d5-e246-4ebf-acf3-bb84c5401467)
  * [Messaging](https://help.webex.com/en-us/article/n3xx7vcb/Get-started-with-Webex-App#Cisco_Concept.dita_c351b3e8-bcfc-4271-9ce0-90632c794414)
  * [Meetings](https://help.webex.com/en-us/article/n3xx7vcb/Get-started-with-Webex-App#Cisco_Concept.dita_13eec652-b9a0-424e-9bb3-4e90823a23da)
  * [Calling](https://help.webex.com/en-us/article/n3xx7vcb/Get-started-with-Webex-App#Cisco_Concept.dita_117a2dd6-a84e-4247-a9fc-84a8d5644ee7)


The all new Webex App is one easy-to-use and secure app you can use to call, message, meet, and get work done: 
  * It brings everyone together to do exceptional work. 
  * It makes you smarter; it’s personalized to you.
  * It gives you options to choose how you work.
  * It moves fluidly while you call, message, and meet. 


We're glad to have you join us! If your company’s already given you an account, you’re ready to get started. 
If you subscribe to Webex from your Service Provider, you already have an account and can start using the app right away.
If you don’t have an account yet, first choose a [plan](https://www.webex.com/pricing/index.html) that works best for you, then you can get started using web.webex.com or by [downloading](https://cart.webex.com/sign-up) the app. When you sign in for the first time, you're guided through some tips on how to use the app. 
![Webex app on different devices](https://cisco-api.ingeniuxondemand.com/DITA/content/en/us/td/i/400001-500000/450001-460000/454001-455000/454415.jpg)
Our tips here focus on how to do things with the desktop app, but details about how to do these same things on web and mobile are included in the links. You can do some things the same way no matter which app you use, but for others you need to use the desktop app. 
## Start working together
When you start a conversation with someone, it [creates a space](https://help.webex.com/en-us/hk71r4/) between you and the people you invite to the conversation. You can invite anyone (including people outside your organization) to join in on a conversation. All you need is their email address.
All your work takes place within [spaces and teams](https://help.webex.com/en-us/4eawbp/). Spaces give you a place to collaborate on a specific topic or project. In a space, you can [send messages](https://help.webex.com/en-us/jc7z88/), [schedule meetings](https://help.webex.com/en-us/nu5w9x3/), and [call other people](https://help.webex.com/en-us/4eliocb/). 
![Create a space](https://cisco-api.ingeniuxondemand.com/DITA/content/en/us/td/i/400001-500000/440001-450000/441001-442000/441738.gif)
If your original intention changes and your project outgrows its original purpose, you can [create a team](https://help.webex.com/en-us/n3kbxbf/) and then [move a space into a team](https://help.webex.com/en-us/nfzbl3p/). Teams help you keep everything organized by putting multiple spaces under a common theme. Teams are great for a consistent group of people who are going to be working on  _something_ for a long time. That  _something_ can involve many activities or can be subprojects happening at the same time. 
If your organization has [public spaces](https://help.webex.com/en-us/article/r78evy/), you can discover, join or create public spaces and connect with people with similar interests, or keep up with the latest news.
And you find that you're in too many spaces and some of them don't require your input or you don't need to keep up with that particular conversation, you can [leave any space](https://help.webex.com/en-us/nek3fbf/) at any time.
## Elevate important information
Right away, you're notified about all messages coming your way. Work smarter by keeping track of what's important to you. You can [play around with your notifications](https://help.webex.com/en-us/ng7w8cj/) until you figure out what works best for you. For example, you can set it up so that you're only notified when you're @mentioned in [any space](https://help.webex.com/en-us/nmoypobb/) or when any message gets posted in your most [important spaces](https://help.webex.com/en-us/nizi9w8/). And if you're not interested in a particular space, you can [leave it](https://help.webex.com/en-us/nek3fbf/) or [hide it](https://help.webex.com/en-us/nz44xmg/) so that you're no longer notified about messages posted to that space and it doesn't show up in your Spaces list.
![Change notification settings](https://cisco-api.ingeniuxondemand.com/DITA/content/en/us/td/i/400001-500000/440001-450000/441001-442000/441734.jpg)
You can also [see other people's availability](https://help.webex.com/en-us/wghlt5/) so that you can find the right time to connect with them. For example, if you see a green circle around someone's profile picture, it means they've been using the app within the last 10 minutes so chances are, you'll get a response if you reach out. 
![Profile picture with green circle showing the user was active in the last 10 minutes, and is available for an interaction.](https://cisco-api.ingeniuxondemand.com/DITA/content/en/us/td/i/400001-500000/420001-430000/427001-428000/427301.jpg)
On the other hand, if you see someone doesn't want to be disturbed, you might want to wait before reaching out.
![Profile picture with red circle indicating the user is not available and prefers not to be disturbed.](https://cisco-api.ingeniuxondemand.com/DITA/content/en/us/td/i/400001-500000/440001-450000/441001-442000/441692.jpg)
## Personalize your Webex App
Make the Webex App feel more like your own by personalizing it a bit. Start by adding your own photo as your [profile picture](https://help.webex.com/en-us/6xhst1/) so that other people can recognize you. 
![Upload a profile picture.](https://cisco-api.ingeniuxondemand.com/DITA/content/en/us/td/i/400001-500000/430001-440000/438001-439000/438822.gif)
You can change your [display name](https://help.webex.com/en-us/nvs03bq/) to a nickname.
![](https://cisco-api.ingeniuxondemand.com/DITA/content/en/us/td/i/400001-500000/450001-460000/454001-455000/454419.jpg)
Add more color to your view by choosing your [preferred color theme](https://help.webex.com/en-us/hpdx59/).
![](https://cisco-api.ingeniuxondemand.com/DITA/content/en/us/td/i/400001-500000/450001-460000/451001-452000/451671.jpg)
You can [change your status](https://help.webex.com/en-us/nhfxsmr/) to be something more descriptive. If you have to step out for a bit or you're busy, you can let people know just by changing your status in the app. For example, you can set it to **Be right back** or let people know you [don't want to be disturbed](https://help.webex.com/en-us/njzs6wl/). You can also make up a [custom status](https://help.webex.com/en-us/nhfxsmr/) so people know what you're up to. 
![Set a status](https://cisco-api.ingeniuxondemand.com/DITA/content/en/us/td/i/400001-500000/450001-460000/451001-452000/451413.jpg)
If you're in a call or meeting and you're sharing your video, you can [blur your background](https://help.webex.com/en-us/0p4gb1/) to hide anything around you or even use a fun [virtual background](https://help.webex.com/en-us/0p4gb1/).
![](https://cisco-api.ingeniuxondemand.com/DITA/content/en/us/td/i/400001-500000/450001-460000/451001-452000/451615.jpg)
## Send a message
When you write your messages, you can [send a quick one](https://help.webex.com/en-us/jc7z88/), or make it stand out with more [text formatting](https://help.webex.com/en-us/nbf0ydx/) and [emojis](https://help.webex.com/en-us/2eawbp/). You can also [share files, pictures, videos, and even gifs](https://help.webex.com/en-us/yuwad5/). Webex App keeps a list of all the [content shared in a space](https://help.webex.com/en-us/article/bm2zbp), so you'll never lose track of them. Use [@mentions](https://help.webex.com/en-us/p5k20o/) to make sure the right people see your message. Don’t worry if you’ve made a mistake and need to [edit](https://help.webex.com/en-us/3bic4j/) it or if you’ve pasted in the wrong space and need to [delete the message](https://help.webex.com/en-us/rt2wbp/) entirely. 
Your messages are persistent. The next time you message the group, your conversation picks right up where you've left it. And after you send a message, you can [see who's read your message](https://help.webex.com/en-us/nddqw5z/). 
![review your compose message toolbar](https://cisco-api.ingeniuxondemand.com/DITA/content/en/us/td/i/500001-600000/500001-510000/502001-503000/502413.jpg)
## Read and respond to messages
When you get a new message, you're notified right away. If you’re too busy to respond but want to see someone’s message, you can just [take a quick peek](https://help.webex.com/en-us/ntkvoj9/) instead. People won't know that you've seen the message. 
You can also make it easy for yourself and others to follow a specific train of thought using [threading](https://help.webex.com/en-us/nvrq4ycb/), [quotes](https://help.webex.com/en-us/ct7m94/), and even [forwarding](https://help.webex.com/en-us/ijf8fv/) a message to someone else. 
## Organize your messages
Another way to help keep you productive is by organizing your messages. You can [filter](https://help.webex.com/en-us/iqq278/) your messages so that you can focus on just your unread messages, for example, or just spaces where you've been @mentioned.
![Checking for important messages.](https://cisco-api.ingeniuxondemand.com/DITA/content/en/us/td/i/400001-500000/450001-460000/451001-452000/451508.jpg)
You can also [flag important messages](https://help.webex.com/en-us/9hf3oc/) so that you can refer back to them easily. 
![](https://cisco-api.ingeniuxondemand.com/DITA/content/en/us/td/i/400001-500000/420001-430000/427001-428000/427256.jpg)
And if you're looking for a message or a file that was shared but can't remember what space it was posted in, you can [search](https://help.webex.com/en-us/n1fjfjp/) for it.
![find messages and files in a space](https://cisco-api.ingeniuxondemand.com/DITA/content/en/us/td/i/400001-500000/450001-460000/451001-452000/451677.jpg)
![Icons to start, join or schedule a meeting](https://cisco-api.ingeniuxondemand.com/DITA/content/en/us/td/i/400001-500000/490001-500000/495001-496000/495296.jpg)
## Master your meetings
Managing your day is simple and intuitive with the Meetings tab in Webex App. It brings your schedule, meeting tools, and past discussions together in one place, so you can stay organized and ready for every conversation.
Use the Meetings tab as your home base. From here, you can start, join, or schedule meetings, view your upcoming calendar, and revisit past discussions.
Before you begin, use the built-in widgets to make sure everything works as expected:
  * Check your mic and camera— [Start a Webex meeting](https://help.webex.com/en-us/n3rv530/) to verify your mic, camera, and screen-sharing settings.
  * Connect your calendar—Choose an option to connect your calendar and view upcoming meetings.


## Start and schedule meetings
![Schedule a meeting](https://cisco-api.ingeniuxondemand.com/DITA/content/en/us/td/i/400001-500000/450001-460000/451001-452000/451616.jpg)
Start a meeting right away or schedule one for later. Webex gives you flexible options to meet when and how you need:
  * From a space— [Start](https://help.webex.com/en-us/nyrqyyw/) or [schedule](https://help.webex.com/en-us/nu5w9x3/) a meeting directly from a space where you are already collaborating. Webex automatically invites everyone in the space and assigns host privileges so participants can help manage the meeting. You also get quick access to shared files and whiteboards during the meeting.
  * From your calendar—Use your [Personal Meeting Room (PMR)](https://help.webex.com/en-us/ngft4sdb/) for quick or recurring meetings. If you do not have a PMR, you can schedule a meeting from your [calendar](https://help.webex.com/en-us/wy517z/) with anyone, even if they do not have a Webex account. You still get full host controls, including breakout sessions and recording transcripts.


## Stay informed
![View scheduled meeting details.](https://cisco-api.ingeniuxondemand.com/DITA/content/en/us/td/i/400001-500000/490001-500000/495001-496000/495298.jpg)
Your work doesn’t stop when the meeting ends. Click Meetings recaps in the Meetings tab to see an organized view of your recent meetings, including summaries and recordings. You no longer need to dig through emails or chat history to find decisions, everything you need is ready for follow-up and action.
If a meeting was recorded, you can select the recording indicator during the call to see who is managing the recording and contact them if you need access.
## Customize your experience
Webex automatically detects your connected devices, such as headsets or cameras. You can easily tailor your [audio and video settings](https://help.webex.com/en-us/ela6i8/) before or during a meeting to suit your preferences:
  * Audio settings—Select your preferred speaker and mic, use **background noise removal** to ensure you’re heard clearly, even in busy environments.
  * Video settings—Choose your preferred camera, enable **Mirror my video** for a natural view, or apply background blur or virtual backgrounds to keep the focus on you.


You can also save these settings as your defaults so you’re always ready the moment you join a meeting.
## Customize your audio and video preferences
When you make or receive a call, the app automatically detects the audio and video devices you have connected to your computer, such as a headset. You can change your settings right before you make a call, like if you want your video on or you just want it to be an audio call only. You can also make some of these your default settings, if you'd like. 
![Image shows accessing the default settings within Webex](https://cisco-api.ingeniuxondemand.com/DITA/content/en/us/td/i/400001-500000/450001-460000/454001-455000/454420.jpg)
You can customize your [video](https://help.webex.com/en-us/lw2dx4/) settings:
  * Choose what camera to use.
  * See yourself like you're looking in a mirror (Mirror my video).
  * Blur your background or use a preset or custom virtual background.


You can also personalize your [audio](https://help.webex.com/en-us/n139bv9/) settings:
  * Choose a headset, speaker, or microphone.
  * Reduce disruptions with background noise removal and speech enhancement.


## Create or Change your Voicemail PIN
If you're new to the Webex Calling or Call Plan you must create your voicemail PIN. The first time you check your voicemail in Webex App, you're prompted for a PIN. You can [use the steps here](https://help.webex.com/en-us/nptrwuw/Webex-Voicemail#Webex-Set-a-Voicemail-PIN-Webex-Calling-and-Calling-Hosted-by-a-Service-Provider) to help you create your PIN the first time or change your PIN at any time. While you're setting your PIN, you can also do things like personalize your voicemail greeting.
## Call someone on Webex App
You can [call anyone with a Webex account](https://help.webex.com/en-us/n84b09o/) without any additional plans or services. If you’re connected to each other in the Webex App, you can call them on the Webex App. These are special types of calls that let you add guests to the call, share your whiteboard, and share your screens. You don't use their phone number; you just need to be connected to them in the app. Just look for the Audio ![Audio Call button](https://cisco-api.ingeniuxondemand.com/DITA/content/en/us/td/i/400001-500000/420001-430000/429001-430000/429940.jpg) or Video ![Video Call button](https://cisco-api.ingeniuxondemand.com/DITA/content/en/us/td/i/400001-500000/430001-440000/438001-439000/438668.jpg) icon. 
![Image highlights the options for placing a video call in Webex App](https://cisco-api.ingeniuxondemand.com/DITA/content/en/us/td/i/400001-500000/450001-460000/454001-455000/454408.jpg)
## Answer a call
When you get a call, you get a notification that you can use to accept or decline it. If the person calling you has a Webex account, you can also message the person back if you're not able to take the call right away. 
![Image shows and highlights an incoming call notification](https://cisco-api.ingeniuxondemand.com/DITA/content/en/us/td/i/400001-500000/450001-460000/454001-455000/454412.jpg)
## Call someone with a phone number
If your organization has configured your Webex account with a [phone service](https://help.webex.com/en-us/nbtgrzx/), you can also [call anyone else with a phone number](https://help.webex.com/en-us/ndpb9acb/). This type of calling requires additional services so check if your account has a [_phone service_](https://help.webex.com/en-us/nbtgrzx/), associated with it. 
You can only be signed into the phone service on one calling app at a time. For example, you can't be signed into the phone service on both the Webex App and Jabber or the Webex Calling app at the same time. If you're using Webex App and sign into another calling app, the phone service in Webex App is disconnected.
When you call like this, it’s just like making a regular phone call from any other type of phone. If you want to call your favorite restaurant to make your lunch order, call them right from the app, and place your order.
When you're in a phone call, you may have access to certain features. Here are just a few features available:
  * **Put a call on hold** —If you're on a phone call with someone and you get interrupted with something else that you need to take care of, you can [put the call on hold](https://help.webex.com/en-us/npvltok/) and resume it when you're free to chat again.
  * **Transfer a call** —When you're on a call, you can [transfer that call](https://help.webex.com/en-us/5er4f3/) to someone else.
  * **Forward a call** —If you're going to be away from your desk but don't want to miss an important phone call, you can [forward your calls](https://help.webex.com/en-us/n6ycytf/) to another phone number.
  * **Add someone to your contacts** —You can [add people to your Contacts list](https://help.webex.com/en-us/z3397j/), making it easier to connect with the people you message and call most often.
  * **Voicemail** —Don't worry about missing important calls. People can leave you a voice message and you can [listen to the message](https://help.webex.com/en-us/8u5983/) right in the app.
