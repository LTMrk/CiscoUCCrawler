Sx550X, Sx350X, Sx250: PSE will Supply Power to Catalyst PSE Ports 
## Date Identified
May 31, 2016
## Date Resolved
June 5, 2017
## Products Affected  
|  **Model**  |  **Firmware Version**  |  **Fixed In**  |  
| --- | --- | --- |  
|  Sx550X, Sx350x, Sx250  |  2.1.0.63  |  2.3.0.130  |  
## Problem Description
When the PoE port of Sx550X, Sx350X or Sx250 switch is connected to the PoE port of 3750 Catalyst switch, the former will provide power to the latter through PoE.
## Resolution
This issue is fixed when you upgrade to the firmware version 2.3.0.130. To upgrade, click [here](https://www.cisco.com/c/en/us/support/docs/smb/switches/cisco-550x-series-stackable-managed-switches/smb5304-upgrade-firmware-on-a-switch.html) for instructions.
