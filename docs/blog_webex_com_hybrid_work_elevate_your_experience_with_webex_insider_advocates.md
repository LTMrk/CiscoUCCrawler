Are you passionate about Webex and eager to make your mark on the future of collaboration? If so, [Webex Insider Advocates](https://insideradvocates.cisco.com/join/advocates "Webex Insider Advocates"), powered by Cisco Insider, is your gateway to a world of exclusive perks, insights, and professional growth.
## A community of collaboration advocates
This program, formerly known as Webex Ambassadors, has been revamped to offer even more value to its members. As a participant, you’re not just joining a program; you’re becoming part of a vibrant community of like-minded professionals. Here, you’ll have the opportunity to build your network, share experiences, and forge valuable connections.
## Unlock the benefits of Webex Insider Advocates
Membership in the Webex Insider Advocates program comes with a variety of benefits designed to enhance your professional and Webex experience:
  * **Expand Your Network** : Connect with a diverse group of professionals who share your enthusiasm for cutting-edge collaboration and customer experience tools.
  * **Redeem Points** : Earn points for consuming Webex content (blogs, training videos, product announcements, etc.) and exchange them for amazing swag and rewards.
  * **Shape Innovation** : Provide feedback directly to the Webex product team, helping to shape the product that fuels your daily work.
  * **Exclusive Insights** : Gain early access to the newest features and exclusive insights that keep you ahead of the curve.
  * **Expert Strategies** : Discover expert strategies to maximize your company’s use of Webex and become a subject matter expert on all things Webex within your organization.


## What to expect as a Webex Insider Advocate
Webex Insider Advocates is a place where your actions translate into tangible benefits. Whether you’re reading blogs, attending webinars, or penning reviews, every contribution earns you points that you can redeem for exclusive rewards.
Over time, Webex Insider Advocate points can be cashed in for rewards like Apple AirTags, Nike backpacks, desk fans, Webex Merch Store vouchers, and unique experiences such as meeting a McLaren F1 driver.
While Cisco Partners are welcome to join the conversation and contribute, they are not eligible for reward redemption.

This shared experience not only fosters a sense of camaraderie but also facilitates collective problem-solving and goal achievement. It’s a testament to the power of community and collaboration, and it’s something I deeply appreciate about being part of the Webex ecosystem.”
**– Jeremy Shupick, Network Administrator, Wyoming Machinery Company**
“Being a Webex Insider Advocate is a rewarding experience. I love having the opportunity to test new features and provide feedback that can help improve the product. It’s also great to connect with other advocates and learn from their experiences.”

## Join Webex Insider Advocates today
Webex Insider Advocates is more than just a program – it’s a stepping stone to elevating your Webex journey and professional influence. If you’re ready to amplify your impact, connect with peers, and enjoy the rewards of your engagement, visit [webex.com/insider](https://webex.com/insider "webex.com/insider") and join today.
