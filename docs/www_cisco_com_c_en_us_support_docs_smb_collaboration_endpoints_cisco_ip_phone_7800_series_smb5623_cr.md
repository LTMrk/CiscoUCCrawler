Create a Contact From a Call History Record on a Cisco IP Phone 7800 or 8800 Series Multiplatform Phone
## Objective
The Cisco IP Phone 7800 and 8800 Series Multiplatform Phones comprise a set of full-featured Voice-over-Internet Protocol (VoIP) phones that provide voice communication over an IP network. The phones provide all the features of traditional business phones, such as call forwarding, redialing, speed dialing, transferring calls, and conference calling. The Cisco IP Phone 7800 and 8800 Series Multiplatform Phones are targeted for solutions that are centered on third-party Session Initiation Protocol (SIP)-based IP Private Branch Exchange (PBX).
The Cisco IP Phone 7800 and 8800 Series Multiplatform Phones have address books that can hold contact information for ease of access. From a call history record, you can create a contact name and include work, mobile, and home phone numbers. You can even assign a personalized ring tone.
This article provides instructions on how to create a contact from a call history record on your Cisco IP Phone 7800 or 8800 Series Multiplatform Phone.
## Applicable Devices
  * 7800 Series
  * 8800 Series


## Software Version
  * 11.0


## Create a Contact from a Call History Record
### Create a Contact from Call History Record
Step 1. Press the **Recents** softkey on your IP Phone.
[![](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/smb/collaboration-endpoints/cisco-ip-phone-7800-series/images/ijgm-06162017-create-contact-call-history-step1.png)](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/smb/collaboration-endpoints/cisco-ip-phone-7800-series/images/ijgm-06162017-create-contact-call-history-step1.png "Related image, diagram or screenshot.")
Step 2. Press the up or down arrows on the [![](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/smb/collaboration-endpoints/cisco-ip-phone-7800-series/images/ijgm-06162017-create-contact-call-history-step2.png)](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/smb/collaboration-endpoints/cisco-ip-phone-7800-series/images/ijgm-06162017-create-contact-call-history-step2.png "Related image, diagram or screenshot.") navigation button to choose the phone number that you want to add to your address book.
[![](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/smb/collaboration-endpoints/cisco-ip-phone-7800-series/images/ijgm-06162017-create-contact-call-history-step2a.png)](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/smb/collaboration-endpoints/cisco-ip-phone-7800-series/images/ijgm-06162017-create-contact-call-history-step2a.png "Related image, diagram or screenshot.")
**Note:** In this example, call history from phone number 4000 is chosen.
Step 3. Press the **Option** softkey.
[![](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/smb/collaboration-endpoints/cisco-ip-phone-7800-series/images/ijgm-06162017-create-contact-call-history-step3.png)](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/smb/collaboration-endpoints/cisco-ip-phone-7800-series/images/ijgm-06162017-create-contact-call-history-step3.png "Related image, diagram or screenshot.")
Step 4. Choose **Add to address****book** using the circle navigation [![](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/smb/collaboration-endpoints/cisco-ip-phone-7800-series/images/ijgm-06162017-create-contact-call-history-step4.png)](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/smb/collaboration-endpoints/cisco-ip-phone-7800-series/images/ijgm-06162017-create-contact-call-history-step4.png "Related image, diagram or screenshot.") button then press the **Select** softkey.
[![](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/smb/collaboration-endpoints/cisco-ip-phone-7800-series/images/ijgm-06162017-create-contact-call-history-step4a.png)](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/smb/collaboration-endpoints/cisco-ip-phone-7800-series/images/ijgm-06162017-create-contact-call-history-step4a.png "Related image, diagram or screenshot.")
Step 5. Enter the name of the contact that you want to create. In this example, John is used.
[![](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/smb/collaboration-endpoints/cisco-ip-phone-7800-series/images/ijgm-06162017-create-contact-call-history-step5.png)](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/smb/collaboration-endpoints/cisco-ip-phone-7800-series/images/ijgm-06162017-create-contact-call-history-step5.png "Related image, diagram or screenshot.")
The number registered in the call history is automatically added as a Work phone number.
Step 6. (Optional) Enter the mobile phone number of the contact in the _Mobile_ field. In this example, 19161234567 is used.
[![](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/smb/collaboration-endpoints/cisco-ip-phone-7800-series/images/ijgm-06162017-create-contact-call-history-step6.png)](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/smb/collaboration-endpoints/cisco-ip-phone-7800-series/images/ijgm-06162017-create-contact-call-history-step6.png "Related image, diagram or screenshot.")
Step 7. (Optional) Enter the home phone number of the contact in the _Home_ field. In this example, 19162345678 is used.
[![](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/smb/collaboration-endpoints/cisco-ip-phone-7800-series/images/ijgm-06162017-create-contact-call-history-step7.png)](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/smb/collaboration-endpoints/cisco-ip-phone-7800-series/images/ijgm-06162017-create-contact-call-history-step7.png "Related image, diagram or screenshot.")
Step 8. (Optional) Choose **Rington****e** using the circle navigation [![](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/smb/collaboration-endpoints/cisco-ip-phone-7800-series/images/ijgm-06162017-create-contact-call-history-step8.png)](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/smb/collaboration-endpoints/cisco-ip-phone-7800-series/images/ijgm-06162017-create-contact-call-history-step8.png "Related image, diagram or screenshot.") button.
[![](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/smb/collaboration-endpoints/cisco-ip-phone-7800-series/images/ijgm-06162017-create-contact-call-history-step8a.png)](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/smb/collaboration-endpoints/cisco-ip-phone-7800-series/images/ijgm-06162017-create-contact-call-history-step8a.png "Related image, diagram or screenshot.")
Step 9. (Optional) Use the up and down arrows of the navigation [![](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/smb/collaboration-endpoints/cisco-ip-phone-7800-series/images/ijgm-06162017-create-contact-call-history-step9.png)](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/smb/collaboration-endpoints/cisco-ip-phone-7800-series/images/ijgm-06162017-create-contact-call-history-step9.png "Related image, diagram or screenshot.") button to choose a ring tone then press the **Select** softkey.
[![](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/smb/collaboration-endpoints/cisco-ip-phone-7800-series/images/ijgm-06162017-create-contact-call-history-step9a.png)](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/smb/collaboration-endpoints/cisco-ip-phone-7800-series/images/ijgm-06162017-create-contact-call-history-step9a.png "Related image, diagram or screenshot.")
**Note:** In this example, Chirp 1 is chosen.
Step 10. Press the **Save** softkey to add the contact to your address book.
[![](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/smb/collaboration-endpoints/cisco-ip-phone-7800-series/images/ijgm-06162017-create-contact-call-history-step10.png)](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/smb/collaboration-endpoints/cisco-ip-phone-7800-series/images/ijgm-06162017-create-contact-call-history-step10.png "Related image, diagram or screenshot.")
You should now have successfully created a contact from a call history record on your Cisco IP Phone 7800 or 8800 Series Multiplatform Phone.
[![](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/smb/collaboration-endpoints/cisco-ip-phone-7800-series/images/ijgm-06162017-create-contact-call-history-step10a.png)](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/smb/collaboration-endpoints/cisco-ip-phone-7800-series/images/ijgm-06162017-create-contact-call-history-step10a.png "Related image, diagram or screenshot.")
### Verify the Created Contact in the Address Book
Step 1. Press the **Address Book**[![](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/smb/collaboration-endpoints/cisco-ip-phone-7800-series/images/ijgm-06162017-verify-contact-call-history-step1.png)](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/smb/collaboration-endpoints/cisco-ip-phone-7800-series/images/ijgm-06162017-verify-contact-call-history-step1.png "Related image, diagram or screenshot.")button on your IP Phone.

[![](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/smb/collaboration-endpoints/cisco-ip-phone-7800-series/images/ijgm-06162017-verify-contact-call-history-step2.png)](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/smb/collaboration-endpoints/cisco-ip-phone-7800-series/images/ijgm-06162017-verify-contact-call-history-step2.png "Related image, diagram or screenshot.")
**Note:** In this example, John is already added in the address book.
[![](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/smb/collaboration-endpoints/cisco-ip-phone-7800-series/images/ijgm-06162017-verify-contact-call-history-step2a.png)](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/smb/collaboration-endpoints/cisco-ip-phone-7800-series/images/ijgm-06162017-verify-contact-call-history-step2a.png "Related image, diagram or screenshot.")
You should now have successfully verified the created contact on the address book of your Cisco IP Phone 7800 or 8800 Series Multiplatform Phone.
