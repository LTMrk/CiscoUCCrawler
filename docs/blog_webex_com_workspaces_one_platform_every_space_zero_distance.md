At Cisco, collaboration devices aren’t standalone products—they’re the human interface of our complete workplace platform. When networking, security, and collaboration work together as one connected system, organizations can future-proof their workplaces at scale.
We’ve earned market leadership in intelligent collaboration devices by delivering on three principles: easy to use, easy to deploy, beautiful to experience. Our customers prove these principles work—managing thousands of meeting rooms with minimal IT staff while delivering consistent experiences end users love and actionable insights facilities teams depend on.
### That’s the power of One Cisco.
From the network fabric that powers AVoIP, to the zero-trust security that protects every endpoint, to Control Hub’s AI-driven management of millions of devices—our devices are part of something bigger. And when everything works as one system, complexity disappears.
Now we’re extending that platform advantage across every workspace—from complex conference rooms to individual desks to factory floors. Same principles. Same platform. Same consistency our customers already trust.
For every device we build, we ask the same question: Will this make connecting with another person feel more natural, or will it get in the way? That question drove us to leadership in meeting rooms. Today, it’s guiding us to solve the complexity that remains in high-impact spaces—powered by the only vendor with a complete, end-to-end workplace ecosystem.
## Cisco Room Kit Pro G2: The End of the AV Puzzle
The top challenge for IT is the complexity of building and maintaining high-impact spaces. Until now, large rooms required a fragile patchwork of DSPs, extenders, and switchers from multiple vendors.
### Cisco is solving this AV puzzle.
Meet the Cisco Room Kit Pro G2—replacing the complexity with a single, AVoIP-first, Agentic AI-first, simplification powerhouse.
![](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns=%22http://www.w3.org/2000/svg%22%20viewBox=%220%200%201672%201028%22%3E%3C/svg%3E)
For more than a decade, we’ve collaborated with NVIDIA to bring intelligence to every meeting room. And now we’re partnering again to solve the AV puzzle.
We worked together to design a Cisco and NVIDIA Jetson compute module unlike anything seen before. This powerful collaboration device features NVIDIA’s industry-leading AI/ML engines, delivering 25 times the AI processing power of the previous generation. Real-time spatial awareness, enhanced noise removal, advanced people framing are all processed locally for security and speed. Just intelligence where it needs to be: in the room.
  * **One-click AVoIP:** 8PoE ports power and manage cameras, microphones, and controllers —no HDMI adaptors, XLR, or power injectors. Network-based audio, video, power, and control with seamless extensibility with Cisco 9200 series switches.
  * **Cinematic Meetings Intelligence:** Agentic Director supports up to 7 cameras (including Room Vision PTZ) and 8 microphones to enable advanced speaker tracking, cinematic cross-views, and flexible room reconfiguration—all natively supported, with HDMI display output up to 8K.
  * **Complete Visibility:** See everything in the room with Control Hub – including 3rd party displays, analog audio connections and HDMI cables. Workspace Advisor delivers recommendations for how to optimize the room experience.​
  * Designed for the Microsoft Device Ecosystem Platform (MDEP); supporting fully featured Webex, Teams, and Zoom meetings, plus interoperability with Google Meet. You can also Instantly connect via USB-C for BYOD and any meeting platform.
  * New lightweight, structurally reinforced chassis is 50% lighter with zero performance loss.


This is Distance Zero extended to your most complex spaces. Accelerated by NVIDIA. Delivered by Cisco. Orderable in February. Shipping Q2 CY26.
But high-stakes decisions don’t stop with your auditoriums, or training and conference rooms.
## Cisco Desk Pro G2: Bring Distance Zero to More Spaces
We’ve proven we can simplify collaboration at the desk and in large conference rooms. Now we’re bringing that same seamless experience to a new environment: small huddle spaces, where quick decisions and important conversations happen.
Meet the Cisco Desk Pro G2—one device that adapts to multiple workspace scenarios. For high-stakes one-on-ones, dual cameras deliver executive presence. When colleagues gather, it transforms into an instant collaboration space. Accelerated by NVIDIA AI for AI-powered intelligence wherever critical work happens.
![](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns=%22http://www.w3.org/2000/svg%22%20viewBox=%220%200%201672%201028%22%3E%3C/svg%3E)
  * **Professional Video for Any Scenario:** The dual 4K camera system features a 48MP tele-lens for executive presence and a 105° wide-angle for small groups, with both lenses working simultaneously to create natural depth and perspective. Our Agentic Director automatically switches between Frames mode (where every participant gets their own window) and group overview mode ensuring the best view at any moment. The dual camera can also capture Cisco Spatial Meetings for consumption on Apple Vision Pro.​
  * **All-Day Display:** The 27-inch 4K screen delivers 2000:1 contrast ratio and 98% DCI-P3 color coverage for true-to-life visuals, engineered for reduced blue light and eye strain for comfortable, extended use.
  * **One Cable. One Button:** A single USB-C cable delivers 80W charging plus PC port replication—connecting a second 4K display, peripherals, and storage. One button joins any meeting: Webex, Teams, Zoom, or Google Meet.
  * **Built to Scale:** Zero Touch Provisioning via Control Hub enables instant, global deployment. Wi-Fi 6E and Deep Sleep modes provide energy efficiency, while high post-consumer recycled materials support your ESG goals.


The same platform powering more than a million devices worldwide, now extended to any small space. Available to order now.
We believe that [Distance Zero](https://blog.webex.com/workspaces/be-in-the-room-from-anywhere/) needs to reach every worker, including those who are always on the move.
## Cisco Wireless Phone 9821: Communication for the Frontline
Nurses, warehouse staff, manufacturing teams—they need reliable communication that moves with them through demanding environments. Consumer devices lack enterprise integration. Third-party solutions create management complexity. And when milliseconds matter for safety, connection delays are unacceptable.
![](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns=%22http://www.w3.org/2000/svg%22%20viewBox=%220%200%201672%201028%22%3E%3C/svg%3E)
The Cisco Wireless Phone 9821 extends your collaboration infrastructure and enables frontline workers with the leading Agentic AI in Cisco´s calling platform.
  * **Enterprise Integration & Frontline Design: **Native integration with Unified Communications Manager (CUCM) and Webex Calling provides seamless platform extension. Shared device deployments are supported—ideal for shift workers. The rugged design with tactile keypad and swappable battery handles demanding frontline environments.
  * **When Milliseconds Matter:** Wi-Fi 6E delivers ultra-low latency and clear audio for critical, real-time communication in fast-paced environments—factories, warehouses, and healthcare facilities. AI-powered noise removal and integrated man-down detection enhance safety and clarity. The configurable action button and push-to-talk capability enable instant response.
  * **Managed at Scale & Built for Enterprise: **The phone runs on PhoneOS for consistent management with the 9800 Series. Deploy and manage via CUCM or Control Hub and get end-to-end assurance with Cisco Thousand Eyes. It’s secure, reliable, and customizable for enterprise needs.


Available to order in Q2 CY2026.
## One Platform, Complete Visibility
These devices share something that sets Cisco apart: they’re intelligent nodes on a unified platform. Deploy through the same Control Hub interface. Update automatically across your entire estate. Gain insights that optimize your workspace environment—all with security built into the network infrastructure.
When devices are native to the network, IT gains complete visibility across the entire workspace portfolio: device health, room utilization, network performance, security status, environmental conditions. All from a single interface.
This is the ecosystem advantage that sets Cisco apart. Others deliver hardware, but IT is left integrating disparate management systems and troubleshooting across disconnected platforms. With Cisco, collaboration devices, networking infrastructure, and security work as one seamless system.
These devices and this platform enable Distance Zero in any space. But visibility alone isn’t enough—you need intelligence that drives action.
## AI Agent Integrations: Workspace Intelligence in Your Workflow
We’ve proven we can deliver complete visibility through Control Hub. Now we’re extending that intelligence into new workflows enabled by your generative AI tools.
IT administrators shouldn’t context-switch between tools to understand workspace performance. That’s why we’re bringing Control Hub intelligence directly into the platforms you already use—Amazon Quick Suite, Microsoft Copilot, and the Cisco AI Assistant – with AI Agent Integrations.
![](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns=%22http://www.w3.org/2000/svg%22%20viewBox=%220%200%201672%20977%22%3E%3C/svg%3E)
Ask questions that matter: “Which spaces are underutilized in our London office?” or “Show me collaboration patterns across our east coast buildings.” AI analyzes your workspace portfolio and delivers actionable insights on utilization, patterns, capacity, and optimization. 
Unlike generic AI tools, these integrations tap directly into Control Hub’s real-time device data, utilization metrics, and environmental sensors—giving you answers grounded in actual workspace performance. Your data remains secure, maintaining the same enterprise-grade controls you already trust. 
The power comes from seeing the complete picture. Rather than managing individual rooms, you’re understanding organizational collaboration patterns, identifying opportunities to improve experiences, and making strategic decisions about where to invest next. From a single building to your entire global portfolio. 
Starting in Q1 2026, beta participants will be the first to transform workspace management through AI-powered insights in their existing workflow.
## Workspace Designer: Plan Once, Deploy with Confidence
Optimizing spaces over time requires intelligence. Getting devices into the right spaces from the start requires a plan. [Workspace Designer](https://cs.co/workspace-designer) eliminates deployment risk before equipment arrives. Create 3D visualizations of your meeting spaces with optimal camera placement and viewing angles. Walk through your space virtually, test sightlines, and see exactly how the room will function—all before ordering equipment.
![](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns=%22http://www.w3.org/2000/svg%22%20viewBox=%220%200%201672%201028%22%3E%3C/svg%3E)
Now, thanks to our partnership with Samsung, select from [verified displays](https://help.webex.com/en-us/article/7sw4gab/Cisco-collaboration-devices-certification-program)—LCD, large-format LED, or ultra-wide 21:9—that automatically configure resolution, color balance, and refresh rate the moment you enter the room. These aren’t arbitrary recommendations—they’re tested for seamless compatibility. 
Design once. Validate everything. Deploy with confidence. 
## Continuous Innovation, Consistent Principles
We’ve just introduced the most advanced collaboration devices we’ve ever built—accelerated by NVIDIA, managed through Control Hub, planned through Workspace Designer, optimized by AI. Behind every innovation, three principles remain constant: easy to use, easy to deploy, beautiful to experience.
That discipline is why customers manage millions of our devices through a single platform, trust us with their most critical spaces, and invest confidently knowing their infrastructure will adapt.
For every device we build, I ask the same question: Will this make connecting feel more natural, or will it get in the way? These innovations are our answer—where people and AI work side by side, where distance disappears, and where technology gets out of the way.
Because they’re part of **One Cisco** —connected to your network, secured at the edge, managed through a single platform—they’re intelligent nodes in a workplace that learns, adapts, and scales.
