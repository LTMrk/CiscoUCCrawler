Overview
EOL15696
Cisco announces the end-of-sale and end-of-life dates for the Cisco Version 12.5/12.6 of On-Premises Contact Center applications (UCCE, PCCE, UCCX,CVP) and associated On-Premises software and licenses. The last day to order the affected product(s) is December 31, 2025. Customers with active service contracts will continue to receive support from the Cisco Technical Assistance Center (TAC) as shown in Table 1 of the EoL bulletin. Table 1 describes the end-of-life milestones, definitions, and dates for the affected product(s). Table 2 lists the product part numbers affected by this announcement. For customers with active and paid service and support contracts, support will be available under the terms and conditions of customers' service contract.
