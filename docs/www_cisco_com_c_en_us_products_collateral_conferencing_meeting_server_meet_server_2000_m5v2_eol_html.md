Overview
EOL15091
Cisco announces the end-of-sale and end-of-life dates for the Cisco Meeting Server 2000 M5v2. The last day to order the affected product(s) is October 30, 2023. Customers with active service contracts will continue to receive support from the Cisco Technical Assistance Center (TAC) as shown in Table 1 of the EoL bulletin. Table 1 describes the end-of-life milestones, definitions, and dates for the affected product(s). Table 2 lists the product part numbers affected by this announcement. For customers with active and paid service and support contracts, support will be available under the terms and conditions of customers' service contract. 
The CMS 2000 M5v2 was based upon Intel chipsets that have been superseded. Therefore, the CMS 2000 M5v2 is being superseded by the CMS 2000 M6 (CTI-CMS-2K-M6-K9).
CMS 2000 M5v2 is only affected by this notice. The CMS conferencing application is not part of this notice.
