###  What is Webex?
Webex is an AI-powered collaboration platform that enables intelligent workplaces and connected customer experiences. It unifies the Webex Suite for meetings, messaging, and calling, purpose- built Webex Devices for hybrid workspaces, and the Webex Contact Center for customer engagement.
###  How does Cisco Webex help ensure security and compliance for remote collaboration, especially with AI and hybrid-work security concerns? 
Cisco Webex helps ensure secure and compliant remote collaboration through a secure-by-design approach that combines end-to-end encryption and zero-trust security to protect meetings, messages, and files. It enforces strong identity and access controls like single sign-on (SSO) and multi-factor authentication (MFA), while built-in data protection features such as data loss prevention (DLP) and data residency help safeguard sensitive information. Webex also includes native compliance tools like eDiscovery, legal hold, and retention policies, and meets global standards including General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act of 1996 (HIPAA), System and Organization Controls 2 (SOC 2), and ISO 27001—enabling organizations to support remote work without compromising security or control. Learn more about our [Webex Compliance and Certifications](https://help.webex.com/en-us/article/pdz31w/Webex-Compliance-and-Certifications). 
###  What integrations and features does Webex provide to improve collaboration and productivity across different platforms and industries? 
Cisco Webex supports deep, native integrations across Microsoft, Zoom, and Google ecosystems. Webex integrates directly into Microsoft 365 and Teams—enabling scheduling, messaging, and connecting AI tools across ecosystems. Webex emphasizes true interoperability at the device level. This allows users to join Microsoft Teams, Zoom, or Google Meet meetings from Cisco devices with a single tap, delivering a consistent, native-like experience regardless of platform.
