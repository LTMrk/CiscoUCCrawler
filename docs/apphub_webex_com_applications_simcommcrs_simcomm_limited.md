Service App by [Simcomm Limited ](https://simcomm.com/index.php)
Organizations often struggle to retrieve and reuse historical call recordings during platform migrations, client exits, or speech analytics initiatives—especially when OEM platforms lock data into proprietary formats. This limits accessibility, increases costs, and reduces operational flexibility.
SimComm Call Retrieval Solution (SimComm CRS) eliminates these barriers by securely extracting call recordings from any OEM platform and converting them into open, industry-standard formats such as MP3, MP4, and WAV. CRS ensures recordings are portable, secure, and compliance-ready, supporting system migrations, client handovers, and advanced analytics use cases.
To use SimCommCRS, you need a paid Webex account and a SimComm Limited account. To get started, contact support@simcomm.com , and our team will assist with account setup. A subscription is required—please contact support@simcomm.com for pricing and subscription options.
