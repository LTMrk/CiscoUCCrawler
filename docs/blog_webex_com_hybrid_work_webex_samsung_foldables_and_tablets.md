## Take your Android experience to new heights with Webex by Cisco
Unlock a whole new level of productivity & flexibility for on-the-go collaboration with the Webex App on Samsung Foldable phones & tablets. Bringing together best-in-class Webex meetings, messaging, and calling all in one app, purpose-built for Android.
Coming soon, Samsung Foldable mobile users can quickly answer calls or respond to messages directly from the cover screen. And when hands-free video collaboration is required, unfold to the unrivaled Webex Flex mode experience with the ability to see up to 15 attendees at once. It’s a multitasking powerhouse for mobile productivity on the go.
![](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns=%22http://www.w3.org/2000/svg%22%20viewBox=%220%200%20980%20520%22%3E%3C/svg%3E)
## Unparalleled meeting flexibility, with Webex on the Samsung Galaxy Tablets
Whether you are at home, in the office, or on the move, if you need a larger screen – Android tablets provide a dynamic viewing experience. View up to 21 attendees simultaneously during a meeting and co-create like never before with the [S-pen](https://www.samsung.com/us/mobile/mobile-accessories/phones/s-pen-pro-black-ej-p5450sbegus/ "S-pen") and [Webex whiteboarding](https://www.webex.com/suite/whiteboard.html "Webex whiteboarding"). Take your meetings to the next level with features like custom stage view, [interactive polling](https://www.webex.com/suite/polling.html "interactive polling") and Q&A, real-time translations, and advanced AI-powered noise cancellation and voice enhancement. And if that’s not all, Webex messaging allows you to stay connected for a seamless asynchronous collaboration. All these features make effortless multitasking for hybrid workers.
[![](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns=%22http://www.w3.org/2000/svg%22%20viewBox=%220%200%20980%20520%22%3E%3C/svg%3E)](https://blog.webex.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/10/galaxy-tablet-final.png)
We continue to introduce new innovations aimed at enhancing collaboration for everyone, making it simple and more enjoyable. Our customers can look forward to these exciting enhancements coming by the end of the year.
You can [download](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.cisco.wx2.android&hl=en_US&gl=US "download") Webex App for Android from Google Play store.
To learn more about Webex innovations for hybrid work, attend [WebexOne 2023 | October 24-26](https://www.webexone.com/ "WebexOne 2023 | October 24-26"). Whether you attend virtually or in-person, it’s an event you won’t want to miss.
