What's New in Wholesale RTM
![list-menu](https://help.webex.com/images/feedback.svg)Feedback?
The Wholesale Route-to-Market (RTM) is a strategic channel solution that is designed to bring Webex to the SMB segment by leveraging the market power of Service Providers around the world. 
As a Wholesale RTM partner, we want to make sure you know about the latest administration features being released. Subscribe to this article to receive notifications for future releases.
This article is intended for Service Provider administrators that manage Wholesale RTM on behalf of your customers. For end-user features, see [What's New in Webex](https://help.webex.com/en-us/8dmbcr/What-s-New-in-Cisco-Webex-Teams-xa).

## July 2026
### Collaboration Partner Hub Labs
Collaboration Partner Hub Labs is a new framework that gives partners early access to innovative features that are in development by the Webex product team. Partners can try these features and provide feedback on their usefulness.
The features available in Partner Hub Labs include:
  * **Global search** —Quickly search across your entire Partner Hub account, including customers, partner administrators, and customer groups.
  * **Top 5 customers** —See your top customers in one view and track activation, adoption, and key usage trends to stay ahead of issues and opportunities.
  * **Relationships visualizer** —Explore customer organization relationships and understand how access is structured.
  * **AI-powered customer opportunities** —Analyze calling usage, devices, and more to generate real-time insights that help ensure customer success and uncover hidden opportunities and risks.


![Partner Hub Labs in Partner Hub portal](https://cisco-api.ingeniuxondemand.com/DITA/content/en/us/td/i/400001-500000/490001-500000/498001-499000/498169.jpg)
### Assign external administrators to partner-defined customer groups
Partner full administrators can assign the full administrator role to an external administrator so that the external administrator can manage customers in a group. This makes it easier to use customer groups for sub-partners who manage their customers.
### Selective call recording for compliance and cost control
You can now control call recording with flexible policies based on call direction, such as inbound, outbound, or both, and call source, such as internal, external PSTN, or both.
  * Targeted recording—Capture only the calls that you need, such as external PSTN calls, and exclude nonessential or internal conversations.
  * Compliance-ready—Meet industry-specific requirements such as HIPAA, GDPR, and CCPA without over-recording.
  * Cost efficient—Reduce storage requirements and legal risks by avoiding unnecessary recordings.
  * Granular control—Apply different rules for inbound and outbound calls, tailored to your business needs.
  * Consistent experience—Apply recording rules from the start of the call, with independent start and stop announcements.
  * Broad coverage—Apply policies to users, workspaces, call queues, virtual lines, and shared lines.


This helps you balance privacy, compliance, and operational efficiency in Webex Calling.
For details, see [Manage call recording for Webex Calling](https://help.webex.com/en-us/ilga4/), [Webex Calling Customer Assist](https://help.webex.com/en-us/72sb3r/), [Configure settings templates in Control Hub](https://help.webex.com/en-us/n5uf91x/), and [Reports for Your Cloud Collaboration Portfolio](https://help.webex.com/en-us/nmug598/#WxC-detailed-call-history-report).
### Improved localization for Control Hub and Customer Assist analytics
Control Hub and Customer Assist analytics now include expanded localization support, making key dashboard content easier to use in supported languages. This update adds localized text for table headers, filter categories, report generation, disclaimers, and chart labels, including axis titles and pie chart center labels.
For details, see [Reports for Your Cloud Collaboration Portfolio](https://help.webex.com/en-us/n0rlwxe#reference-template_3ce764ad-adf5-4f0e-9fd9-840f38f0619d).
### Full administrator role support for external partner administrators
Partner full administrators can assign the full administrator role to external administrators who manage customer organizations or partner-defined customer groups. This helps service providers support channel-led customer management without creating separate accounts, while giving trusted external administrators the access they need to manage assigned customers.
### Outbound and callback data added to Customer Assist reports
The Customer Assist Queue Stats and Queue Agent Stats reports now include outbound and callback data.

## May 2026
### AI Assistant in Partner Hub
The Cisco AI Assistant is now available in Partner Hub. You can use the AI Assistant to ask "how do I" questions relating to setup and configuration. Answers are sourced from the articles available in Webex Help Center using the Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) technique.
You can also use AI Assistant to query your pre-generated reports using natural language. Based on the question asked, the AI Assistant will either automatically pick the right report from which to answer the question, or if there are multiple reports with overlapping data, ask the admin to choose the right report ("human in the loop" approach).
For details, see [Get started with Partner Hub](https://help.webex.com/en-us/c3q9lz/Get-started-with-Partner-Hub#reference-template_e9d64209-0f34-4771-ae68-ca4ffaccbb01).
### Search enhancements in Partner Hub
Partners can now search for customers by device MAC address or by telephone number in E.164 format in the Customers list, making it faster and easier to find specific customers.
### Support for multiple Dialed Number Identification Service (DNIS) for call queues
This feature is currently unavailable in select European regions. We are actively working to restore full functionality.
You can now assign multiple DNIS numbers to a single call queue and configure unique announcements for each. When an agent receives a call, the DNIS used by the caller is displayed to them. This enables agents to deliver branded greetings and identify the customer or line of business (LOB) they’re serving - all without duplicating queues.
For details, see [Configure Call Queue](https://help.webex.com/en-us/nzkg083/) and [Webex Calling Customer Assist](https://help.webex.com/en-us/72sb3r).
### Support for additional Yealink phones
The Webex Calling platform supports a wider range of Yealink models as Cisco Managed Devices. This update provides greater flexibility for high-quality, reliable communication, fully integrated into the Webex Calling platform.
Supported Yealink devices include:.
  * T8 Series: T85W, T87W, T88W, T88W Pro, T88V, T88V Pro
  * T7 Series: T73U, T73W, T74U, T74W, T77U
  * T5 Series: T58W, T58W Pro
  * T3 Series: T31W

This update enables customers to leverage these Yealink models fully managed within Webex Calling, ensuring seamless integration and dependable communication.
For details, see [Supported devices for Webex Calling](https://help.webex.com/en-us/qkwt4j/) and [Cisco managed third-party devices](https://help.webex.com/en-us/njwkamr/).
### Attendant Console with Enhanced Calling package
This enhancement allows Service Providers to attach an Attendant Console license to the Enhanced Calling Package in Webex Wholesale.
For details, see [Wholesale Route-to-Market Package Offers](https://help.webex.com/en-us/n1xv5rv/).
### Native IPv6 support
We’re introducing support for native IPv6 connectivity, eliminating the need for protocol translation for Wholesale services. This upgrade enables seamless migration of Wholesale services from IPv4 to IPV6 without the requirement of protocol translation, ensuring improved network performance and enhanced security.

We’re introducing the `cdr_stream` endpoint API, which enables customers to collect call detail records (CDRs) immediately after a call ends—typically within one minute. This API also automatically retrieves and delivers any late records as soon as they are processed in the Webex Calling cloud, ensuring reliable and timely CDR delivery.
Also, we’ve enhanced the existing partner and customer APIs to support queries for records from the past 30 days, an increase from the previous 48-hour limit. This enhancement gives users greater flexibility in accessing historical data.

Custom playlists allow admins to advertise multiple discounts and deals to their callers.
