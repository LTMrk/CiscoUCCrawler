Overview
EOL13845
Cisco announces the end-of-sale and end-of-life dates for the Cisco Perpetual Licensing for Cisco Enterprise Agreement for Collaboration Multiparty Telepresence. The last day to order the affected product(s) is August 19, 2021. Customers with active service contracts will continue to receive support from the Cisco Technical Assistance Center (TAC) as shown in Table 1 of the EoL bulletin. Table 1 describes the end-of-life milestones, definitions, and dates for the affected product(s). Table 2 lists the product part numbers affected by this announcement. For customers with active and paid service and support contracts, support will be available under the terms and conditions of customers' service contract.
Customers are encouraged to migrate to Cloud Webex meetings under A-FLEX-3. The Cisco Collaboration Flex Plan 3.0 Ordering Guide is available at: <https://www.cisco.com/c/en/us/products/collateral/unified-communications/spark-flex-plan/guide-c07-744224.html>.
Customers who require On Premises Meetings should consider the option of either the Cisco Collaboration Flex Plan subscription (A-FLEX) , or a Cisco Meeting Server Subscription (A-CMS.) The A-FLEX selection provides a choice of either Cloud or On Premises Meetings, including the ability to mix deployments. Alternatively, A-CMS is a pure On Premises solution.
The ordering guides are available below: Cisco Collaboration Flex Plan Ordering Guide: <https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/products/collateral/customer-collaboration/cisco-collaboration-flex-plan-ordering-guide.pdf>.
Cisco Meeting Server Subscription Ordering Guide: <https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/products/se/2019/4/WW_Partner_Organization/C07-730707-06_Cisco_Meeting_Server_OG_v2a.pdf>.
