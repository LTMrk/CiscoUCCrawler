## Objective
This article aims to show you how to add, edit, or remove a contact from the Personal Directory on the Cisco IP Phone 8800 Series Multiplatform through its web-based utility and phone GUI.
## Applicable Devices
  * 8800 Series


## Software Version
  * 11.0.1


## Manage Contacts for your Personal Directory
### Through the Web-based Utility
**Add a Contact**
Step 1. Log in to the web-based utility of the IP phone and click **Personal Directory**.
[![](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/smb/collaboration-endpoints/cisco-ip-phone-8800-series/images/rjs-08302017-addweb-step1.png)](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/smb/collaboration-endpoints/cisco-ip-phone-8800-series/images/rjs-08302017-addweb-step1.png "Related image, diagram or screenshot.")
Step 2. Click the **Add to Personal Directory** button.
[![](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/smb/collaboration-endpoints/cisco-ip-phone-8800-series/images/rjs-08302017-addweb-step2.png)](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/smb/collaboration-endpoints/cisco-ip-phone-8800-series/images/rjs-08302017-addweb-step2.png "Related image, diagram or screenshot.")
Step 3. In the Add to Personal Directory pop-up window, enter the name of the contact in the _Name_ field. 
**Note:** In this example, Amelia is used.
[![](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/smb/collaboration-endpoints/cisco-ip-phone-8800-series/images/rjs-08302017-addweb-step3.png)](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/smb/collaboration-endpoints/cisco-ip-phone-8800-series/images/rjs-08302017-addweb-step3.png "Related image, diagram or screenshot.")
Step 4. In the _Work Number_ field, enter the work phone number of the contact you wish to add.
**Note:** In this example, 8424 is used.
[![](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/smb/collaboration-endpoints/cisco-ip-phone-8800-series/images/rjs-08302017-addweb-step4.png)](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/smb/collaboration-endpoints/cisco-ip-phone-8800-series/images/rjs-08302017-addweb-step4.png "Related image, diagram or screenshot.")
Step 5. In the _Mobile_ _Number_ field, enter the mobile phone number of the contact you wish to add.
**Note:** In this example, 09240123456 is used.
[![](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/smb/collaboration-endpoints/cisco-ip-phone-8800-series/images/rjs-08302017-addweb-step5.png)](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/smb/collaboration-endpoints/cisco-ip-phone-8800-series/images/rjs-08302017-addweb-step5.png "Related image, diagram or screenshot.")
Step 6. In the _Home_ _Number_ field, enter the mobile phone number of the contact you wish to add.
**Note:** In this example, 9221234 is used.
[![](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/smb/collaboration-endpoints/cisco-ip-phone-8800-series/images/rjs-08302017-addweb-step6.png)](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/smb/collaboration-endpoints/cisco-ip-phone-8800-series/images/rjs-08302017-addweb-step6.png "Related image, diagram or screenshot.")
Step 7. Click **Submit All Changes**.
[![](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/smb/collaboration-endpoints/cisco-ip-phone-8800-series/images/rjs-08302017-addweb-step7.png)](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/smb/collaboration-endpoints/cisco-ip-phone-8800-series/images/rjs-08302017-addweb-step7.png "Related image, diagram or screenshot.")
You should now have added a contact on your Personal Directory through the phone web-based utility.
[![](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/smb/collaboration-endpoints/cisco-ip-phone-8800-series/images/rjs-08302017-addweb-step7b.png)](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/smb/collaboration-endpoints/cisco-ip-phone-8800-series/images/rjs-08302017-addweb-step7b.png "Related image, diagram or screenshot.")
**Edit a Contact**
Step 1. In the Personal Directory, choose a contact on the list and click the **Edit** button.
**Note:** In this example, the first contact Amelia is chosen to be edited.
[![](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/smb/collaboration-endpoints/cisco-ip-phone-8800-series/images/rjs-08302017-editweb-step1.png)](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/smb/collaboration-endpoints/cisco-ip-phone-8800-series/images/rjs-08302017-editweb-step1.png "Related image, diagram or screenshot.")

**Note:** In this example, the name Amelia is edited and is changed into Amy.
[![](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/smb/collaboration-endpoints/cisco-ip-phone-8800-series/images/rjs-08302017-editweb-step2.png)](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/smb/collaboration-endpoints/cisco-ip-phone-8800-series/images/rjs-08302017-editweb-step2.png "Related image, diagram or screenshot.")
You should now have successfully edited a contact on your Personal Directory.
[![](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/smb/collaboration-endpoints/cisco-ip-phone-8800-series/images/rjs-08302017-editweb-step2b.png)](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/smb/collaboration-endpoints/cisco-ip-phone-8800-series/images/rjs-08302017-editweb-step2b.png "Related image, diagram or screenshot.")
**Remove a Contact**
Step 1. In the Personal Directory, choose a contact on the list by checking the box.
**Note:** In this example, the first contact Amelia is chosen.
[![](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/smb/collaboration-endpoints/cisco-ip-phone-8800-series/images/rjs-08302017-removeweb-step1.png)](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/smb/collaboration-endpoints/cisco-ip-phone-8800-series/images/rjs-08302017-removeweb-step1.png "Related image, diagram or screenshot.")
Step 2. Click the **Delete Contact** button.
[![](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/smb/collaboration-endpoints/cisco-ip-phone-8800-series/images/rjs-08302017-removeweb-step2.png)](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/support/docs/smb/collaboration-endpoints/cisco-ip-phone-8800-series/images/rjs-08302017-removeweb-step2.png "Related image, diagram or screenshot.")
You should now have successfully removed a contact on your Personal Directory.
